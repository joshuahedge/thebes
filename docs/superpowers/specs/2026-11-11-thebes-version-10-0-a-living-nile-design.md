# Thebes 10.0 "A living Nile" (design, for Josh's review)

**Josh's asks (11 Nov 2026):** after the v10 review (`notes/2026-11-11-v10-review.md`) and a first design that put the Steam shell first, Josh: "I think desktop scale is good. First view is good, but I still would rather refine the visuals and gameplay first." Visuals: "more life and motion" and "also polish existing visuals further". Gameplay: all four threads, in 10.1 and 10.2 (`2026-11-11-thebes-v10-roadmap-design.md`). "Do all of it, outside of the Steam shell."

**For Josh:** 10.0 changes no rule, no cost and no save. It is judged on four things: the first thing a new player sees is a city, not sand; the game reads well on a 1080p monitor; every drawing meets one bar; and the city looks alive at every rank, on a phone, inside the frame budgets, which this release tightens to the 9.1 targets and keeps. Section 1 is what is true today (read from the code at 0209a40); sections 2 to 7 are the changes, each with today, the change and the proof; section 8 is testing and process; section 9 what does not change; section 10 the open questions for the plan.

## 1. What is true today (checked at 0209a40, 9.3.1)

**The frame** (`src/render/loop.js`, `src/render/renderer.js` `renderFrame`): dt capped at 0.25 s, at most 12 sim days a frame, drawing gated at 60 fps running and 30 fps paused. Layer order: map edge (`drawMapEdge`), terrain chunks (at most 2 bakes a frame), ground (the flood, the season wash, rapids, trade wakes, ferries, then `ambient.draw`), scorch and ground shadows, placing marks, one depth-sorted pass of buildings, decor, hills and walkers (monuments drawn live inside it through `drawMonument`), light (`drawLight`), then fire and the late ambient (crops, pool glints, smoke, a rare bird), illness, unit marks, effects, the v90 effects (`drawV90`: weather, dust, fire fought, festival, raid moment, sweep), overlays, grid. `DPR_CAP` 2.

**Budgets:** `AMBIENT.budgetMs` 2 and `v90BudgetMs` 2 (`src/data/ambient.js`), each with a running-average dropper (`createAmbient.account`, `src/render/v90/dropper.js`) and a drop order (rare, smoke, sway; the water always stays). `tests/render/stress.test.js`: a 64 city at 1280x800, once warm no bakes, over 200 sprites, no cache misses, median frame under 50 ms, zoom 0.5 under 120 ms. `tests/v90/v/budget.test.js`: V's layers within 2 ms on 64 and 4 ms on 112 at the floor zoom; raster cap 14 ms. Same pattern in `tests/v70/s/budget.test.js`, `tests/v83/k/budget.test.js`, `tests/stage3/art/ambient.test.js`, `tests/stage4/motion/ambientNotch.test.js`. **There is no frame-time test for a phone** (`tests/ui/mobileBudget.test.js` is layout height; `tests/v93/p/caches.test.js` is cache size). The 9.3 follow-ups measured desktop DPR 1 p50 29 to 41% over 9.2 (17.7 to 25.0 ms on the Capital) and phone p50 8 to 11% over, with sprites drawn fresh as the camera moves as the cause, and the stress and Huge scenes 3.5x and 2.5x over the 9.1 targets in headless Edge.

**Walkers and figures:** `createUI.js` concatenates `walkersAt`, `unitsAt`, `newcomersAt`, the wildlife, `cartsAt` and `v70Walkers` (caravans, boats, ferries); each entry `{ id, kind, x, y, dir, stand }` draws as slot `fig.<kind>` with depth `x + y + 0.01`. Id ranges: units 100000+, processions 200000+, dedications 600000+, streamers 900000+, newcomers 1200000+, wildlife 1400000+, offset by `FIGURE_ID_BASE`. `src/render/walkers.js`: positions are a pure function of the world and the time, `WALKER_CAP` 40, `TILES_PER_DAY` 2, kinds by building (`KIND_BY_TYPE`: water, priest, porter, firefighter), citizens from `CITIZEN_SHARE` 0.15 of homes within 4 tiles, nearest the middle of the view first; processions at most 3. `src/render/art/figures.js`: `FIGURE_KINDS` citizen, woman, water, porter, priest, farmer, noble, fisher, hauler, firefighter, mason, musician, dancer, shrine, standard, newcomer; `UNIT_KINDS` raider, soldier (and `soldier_cheer`); `footShadow`; walk poses pre-warmed per zoom bucket (`warmWalk`). **No builder kind:** crews are `hauler`, `mason` and `gang()` on monuments; the 9.0 scaffold has poles and no people. Caps: newcomers 12 (per-world state in a WeakMap outside the world), carts 30, animals 40. Walkers move on game time at every Motion level and hold while paused; newcomers none under Still; carts stand at their door under Still; wildlife slow under Gentle, still under Still.

**Ambient** (`src/render/ambient.js`, `src/data/ambient.js`): ground layer glints (`glintShare` 0.93, 0.2 tiles/s, 4 to 7 s fades, 12 alpha buckets), ripples, flow, reeds (±3°), a rare felucca and ducks; late layer crop bands, pool glints, smoke (one puff every 2 to 3 s from `SMOKE_FROM`: potter, brewery, brickworks, weaver, jeweller, builders' guild; 3 live), a rare bird; `rareAt` one of each kind every 45 to 90 s; trees sway through lean slots; everything per tile by `hash2(x, y, seed + SALT)`; the wind is fixed per seed (`windOf`); the ambient runs on the real clock; Gentle drops sway, rare life, drift and ripple shift; Still draws nothing.

**Light and weather:** `src/render/light.js` `lightAt`: day, dusk, night, dawn on a 6-minute loop, `LAMP.most` 260, street lamps every 4th road tile, lit homes and streets, held while paused, off under Still or the Day and night setting; `src/render/seasons.js` the wash, the river's state, palm marks; `src/render/v90/layers.js`: `WEATHER` cloud shadows (3, alpha 0.1, drifting with `windOf`, none in the lesson: softened in 9.1, not removed), Shemu sand gusts along the desert edge (at most 12 streaks), `DUST` (cap 30) behind raiders and carts on sand. **No sandstorm, no heat shimmer, no wind that varies over time.**

**Buildings and the bar:** `src/render/looks.js` (four looks by rank, spreading over 4 months), the 9.3 primitives in `src/render/art/primitives.js` (`shadeWall`, `contactLine`, `insetOpening`, `silhouette`, `parapetSteps`, `awning`, `cornice`, `band`, `porch`, `gildedCap`), `tests/v93/p/bar.test.js` (every slot uses a bar primitive except the `EXEMPT` and `OWED` lists), `tests/v93/n/art.test.js` (a file-content contract that figures use `footShadow`, nature `contactShadow`, and so on). `src/render/spriteCache.js`: zoom buckets 0.5 to 2, desktop 32 MB sprites / 80 MB caches, phone 24 / 72, a sprite drawn this frame never evicted. `drawMonument`: live every frame, 3 sites within 1 ms, `GANG` 4 haulers and a sled per crew plus 2 masons, animated on game time at every level, block fade and settle, course dust under Full, capstone shine. **Festival:** `holdFestival` (`src/sim/festival.js`; from Town, 200 gold, mood 6 for 90 days, once a year), shown as a procession of 4 to 12 figures (standard, shrine, musician, dancer, priest), the temple's streamers, lamps at dusk, petals, palace pennants; dedications for 30 days after a monument.

**The camera** (`src/render/camera.js`): the view's centre may reach the map's own edge (6.0 final review). A new city starts on the entry road at the map's corner: at 390x844 about 55% of the screen is off-map, the sample city 45%, 1920x1080 35%. `drawMapEdge` draws a flat tan fill with a hard diagonal edge.

**Scale and the phone budget:** `--ui-scale` on `body` is set only by the Options text size (100 to 200%); at 1920x1080 the top bar text is about 13 px and the title card about 440 px wide. The phone height budget (`tests/ui/mobileBudget.test.js`, rulings 163-177) allows one alert row and one guidance row; the scribe's bubble (`src/ui/v81/scribe.js`) is not counted and makes a third.

**The interface:** `button.primary` gold on lapis; the chosen state is `[aria-pressed="true"]` with a teal ring; `.pill` is the dock's Build button; the dock (`src/ui/dock.js` `createDock`) draws `CATEGORY_ICONS` (═ ⌂ ❋ ⚒ ✚ ⛊ ▲, `src/data/ui.js`) as text, so each OS and font renders them differently; "Show me" comes in three looks, Layers "Off" is drawn as chosen, Demolish is filled red (9.3.1 could items C1, C3, C4). The first design review's visual notes still open: roads want wear, a kerb shadow and cart tone; housing plots read as interface; problem markers are heavy; burnt ground and rubble look like blotches. Ruling 1929: the raider chariot, elephant, raider boat, ibis, heron, hippo, fishing wharf and charred stump have no foot shadow and no test asks for one.

## 2. The first view (Track F)

**2a. The camera keeps the map on screen** (High, every player, S).
- **The rule:** `holdInMap(cam, view)` in `camera.js` clamps the centre to `[m, size - m]` on each axis, `m = min(0.7 × half the view's extent in tiles at the zoom, size / 2)`, so at most about 30% of the view's area is off-map; when the whole map fits, the clamp centres it. It replaces the 6.0 edge rule; the Huge zoom floor is unchanged.
- **Where:** every camera write: the start of a city, Show me and Fit the city frames, drags, flings, pinches, a loaded save's camera (clamped on load).
- **The start:** a new city frames the entry road's end diamond offset inward by the margin, so the diamond sits in the map-side third of the screen.
- **Proof:** `tests/v100/f/holdInMap.test.js` (by zoom, map size and view; the whole-map case; a save's camera on load); a screenshot test of a fresh classic city at 390x844 and 1920x1080 counting the off-map fill under 30% of the pixels.

**2b. The off-map ground drawn** (High, S).
- **Today:** `drawMapEdge` fills flat tan to a hard diagonal.
- **The change:** the off-map ground is desert that pales and loses detail with distance: a 1-tile shadow ramp along the map's rim (the land reads as raised, not cut), sparse dune strokes seeded by position (`hash2`) and baked once at boot into a 256 px repeating tile, fading over about 12 tiles into a warm haze colour; it takes the season wash and the night tint like the land (one fill and one gradient, drawn before the chunks). No rocks, plants or figures; nothing moves.
- **Proof:** screenshots of a fresh city on each map size at 390x844 and 1280x800, before and after; the render budgets hold.

**2c. The scribe in the phone budget** (Medium, phones, S). The scribe's bubble counts as the guidance row: while an alert row shows, the bubble takes the guidance slot and the goal, tip, caption and legend wait as they do for each other (ruling 176's turns), never a third row; the wide layout is unchanged. **Proof:** `mobileBudget.test.js` gains the court-letter-plus-goal-plus-scribe scene (it fails today); the `sample-390` screenshot is retaken.

## 5. Life and motion (Track L)

**The rules of the layer.** Everything here is render-side only: a pure function of the world, the time and a hash, never written to the world or the save (ruling 852's line); walkers move on game time and the ambient on the real clock, as today; every figure uses a registered `fig.<kind>` with bounds and a foot shadow; everything culls to the view range and takes the nearest the middle of the view first; **Full** shows all of it, **Gentle** shows figures standing or fading in place and no weather motion, **Still** shows nothing that moves; nothing changes faster than about once a second except a walking step. New id ranges: townsfolk 1600000+, river life 1700000+, builders 1800000+, crowds 1900000+.

**The budget.** A new `LIFE` budget of 1.5 ms on a desktop and 1 ms on a phone (`src/data/ambient.js`), with its own dropper and drop order (crowds, river life, townsfolk idle, weather, lamps' flicker; walkers never drop below today's), measured by `tests/v100/l/budget.test.js` on the 64 and 112 scenes at 1280x800 and 390x844 DPR 2. The ambient and v90 budgets are unchanged. A frame over 16.7 ms drops from the life order first.

**5a. Townsfolk** (High, M).
- **Today:** 40 walkers on errands, citizens from 15% of homes, newcomers walking in, carts.
- **The change:** people who are not on an errand. Each home with residents has, by hash and time of day, a chance of a figure at its door or yard: a woman grinding grain, a man mending a net or sitting in the shade, children playing (a new small `child` kind, two poses), a goat or a dog in the yard (new `goat` and `dog` kinds, standing and lying). Figures appear and leave by fading in place over a second, never popping. At the river bank within 2 tiles of homes: washerwomen and water carriers kneeling. At night the yards empty and the doors glow (5e). Cap 24 idle figures on a desktop, 16 on a phone, nearest the middle first.
- **Proof:** `townsfolkAt(world, timeDays, nowMs, range, motion)` is deterministic (same inputs, same figures) and never writes the world; the cap holds; a screenshot of a Town street at 390x844 under Full and Gentle.

**5b. The bazaar crowd** (High, S).
- **Today:** porters walk from the bazaar; the stalls stand empty.
- **The change:** a staffed bazaar with food in stock draws a crowd of 3 to 8 figures around its stalls by the hour of the day (busy in the morning, thin at noon, gone at night), standing, turning and moving between stalls; a bazaar short of food has one figure and an empty stall; a plaza or garden nearby draws two more sitting. **Proof:** the crowd's size follows the bazaar's state and the hour (test); a `bazaar-crowd` dev scene for the shots.

**5c. River life and boats with somewhere to go** (Medium, M).
- **Today:** a rare felucca, ducks, trade boats on routes, ferries, the wharf's fishing as state only.
- **The change:** a staffed fishing wharf sends a small boat out and back along the bank in view (2 tiles out, 60 s round, one at a time), with the fisher casting; feluccas at City and above cross the river between the two banks at a cadence by rank (one, then two); herons stand in the shallows and lift when a boat passes; a crocodile basks on a sandbank in Shemu and slides in at dusk; a hippo surfaces in the deep channel once a minute. Under Gentle the boats are still and the birds stand. Cap 6 river figures. **Proof:** deterministic and capped (test); screenshots at the wharf and the crossing.

**5d. Builders on the monuments and the scaffolds** (Medium, M).
- **Today:** crews are 4 haulers and a sled plus 2 masons per site, animated on game time; the scaffold has poles and nobody on it; a building rising shows two haulers at its corners.
- **The change:** a new `builder` kind (carrying, climbing, hammering poses) on the scaffolds of every monument course and every rising building, 2 to 4 by the site's size, moving between pole and course on game time; a mason's hammer strikes with a small dust puff under Full; a finished course sends a hauler down. The crews' animation gains a rest beat (the sled stops, the haulers wipe their brows) every game month. **Proof:** `drawMonument` stays within 1 ms for 3 sites (the existing bound); builders appear only on active sites and never on a paused crew's scaffold (test); screenshots of the pyramid and a rising home.

**5e. Lamps, hearths and smoke at night** (Medium, S).
- **Today:** street lamps every 4th road tile, lit homes and streets, workshop smoke by day.
- **The change:** at dusk every home with residents lights its door (a warm inset glow through `insetOpening`), a hearth smoke rises from a share of homes (hash, 1 in 4, thin and slow), lamps flicker gently (alpha ±8% over 1 to 2 s, under Full only), the temple's lamps burn brighter on a festival night, and the palace's windows light in a sweep at dusk. Under Gentle the lights are steady; under Still as today. **Proof:** `drawLight` stays within its 2 ms share on the 112 scene (`tests/v70/s/budget.test.js`); a `night-town` screenshot.

**5f. Weather: heat shimmer, a varying wind and the rare sandstorm** (Medium, M).
- **Today:** cloud shadows, Shemu gusts, dust behind raiders and carts; the wind is fixed per seed.
- **The change:**
  - **the wind varies:** `windOf` gains a slow drift (a sine over about 4 minutes of real time around the seed's wind), so reeds, smoke, pennants and gusts lean together and change together;
  - **heat shimmer:** in Shemu at the noon phase, over sand tiles farther than 6 tiles from water, a horizontal ripple of the baked chunk rows (two or three slices offset by 1 px, alpha 0.3, in the V layer, within the V budget), only under Full, never over the interface;
  - **a sandstorm:** a rare weather beat, once or twice a year in Shemu, seeded by the city's seed and the year (so a map always has the same storms, and a test can find one), 20 to 40 s of real time: the wind swings, dust streaks thicken from the desert edge (the existing `DUST` and gust systems scaled up, cap 60), a warm haze covers the map (alpha to 0.35), figures lean, lamps dim; it has no effect on the sim; under Gentle it is the haze only, under Still nothing; never during the lesson, and never in the first game year of a city (the first hour stays clear).
- **Proof:** the V budget tests hold with a storm up; `stormsOf(seed, year)` is deterministic and never in year 1 or the lesson (test); screenshots mid-storm at 390x844 and 1280x800.

**5g. Festival processions that fill the street** (Medium, S).
- **Today:** 4 to 12 figures, streamers, petals, temple lamps, pennants; at most 3 processions.
- **The change:** on a festival day the procession doubles (8 to 20, more musicians and dancers, a second shrine), a crowd of townsfolk lines the street it walks (5a's figures standing facing the road, cap 16), the bazaar's crowd joins, the hearths light early, and the music's festival piece is cued (it exists; the cue is the only sound change). **Proof:** the procession's length and the crowd follow the festival state (test); the `v70-festival` scene retaken.

**5h. The lesson** (standing rule). Under the lesson, life is as today plus townsfolk and hearths only: no storms, no shimmer, no crowds at the lesson's bazaar until its food step is done, so the camera's subject stays the step. **Proof:** a test counts the life kinds present during each lesson step.

## 9. What does not change

- **Game rules:** the sim, the balance, costs, footprints, the lesson's steps, the save format (`WORLD_VERSION` 12), the records, the scores. Nothing in section 5 reads anything the sim does not already expose; storms and shimmer change no number.
- **Sound:** no new sound; the festival cue uses the existing piece.
