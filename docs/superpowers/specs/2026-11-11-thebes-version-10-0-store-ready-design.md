# Thebes 10.0 "Store-ready" (design, for Josh's review)

**Josh's ask (11 Nov 2026):** review the game for v10 with two lenses: minimise the chance of a refund in a store's first window (Steam: any reason within 14 days and under 2 hours of play), and improve the visual design. The review is `notes/2026-11-11-v10-review.md` (risks R1-R10, look items L1-L8). **Josh's call (11 Nov): plan 10.0 "Store-ready" as laid out there, Steam Windows first.** This design turns that into one release. 10.1 "Worth the price" (the lesson's decision step, the middle monuments, the heirs, the Capital alert count, the decree bars) and Google Play come after it and are not designed here.

**For Josh:** 10.0 is judged by one question: does a buyer on Steam get through their first two hours on their own Windows PC without anything broken, confusing, empty or small, and does the page promise what the first minute shows. Section 1 is what is true today; sections 2 to 8 are the changes, each with its why, where, size and proof; section 9 is what does not change; section 10 the open questions for the plan. Every claim about today's code was checked at 3582196 (9.3.1 live).

## 1. What is true today (checked at 3582196)

- **The game is a PWA.** `index.html` loads `src/boot.js` as an ES module with relative paths (GitHub Pages serves it under `/thebes/`, so nothing is root-absolute outside `src/dev`). `sw.js` caches every file in `sw-files.js` (generated by `tools/sw-files.ps1`); `?nosw=1` unregisters it. Saves are IndexedDB (`src/save/store.js`: staging, current, backup) with a localStorage fallback. Export builds a `File` and either shares it or downloads it through a hidden anchor (`src/save/exportImport.js` `downloadFile`); Bring rulers reads a file through a hidden `<input type=file>` (`pickTextFile`). The tutorial's picture cards load their marks with `fetch` (`src/ui/tutorial.js`). Nothing else touches the network after load (ruling 610 holds).
- **Install plumbing is everywhere a store build must not show it:** `beforeinstallprompt` is wired in `boot.js`, the title has an Install button, `installFlow.js` detects iOS and standalone and raises install asks and notices, and the Update ready toast comes from the service worker.
- **Versions:** `APP_VERSION` in `src/data/ui.js` is the one constant and the newest `RELEASES` entry (`src/data/releases.js`) must match it; the build sha is stamped into `sw-files.js`; What's new lists every build.
- **The camera** (`src/render/camera.js`): the view's centre may reach the map's own edge and no further (6.0 final review). A new city starts on the entry road, which lies at the map's corner, so a fresh city at 390x844 opens with about 55% of the screen off-map; the sample city shows about 45% on a phone and 35% at 1920x1080. The off-map ground is a flat tan fill with a hard diagonal edge.
- **Interface scale:** `--ui-scale` lives on `body` and is set only by the Options text size (100, 125, 150, 200%). At 1920x1080 the top bar text is about 13 px and the title card about 440 px wide.
- **The phone height budget** (`tests/ui/mobileBudget.test.js`, 9.1 rulings 163-177) allows one alert row and one guidance row under the top bar. The scribe's bubble (`src/ui/v81/scribe.js`) is not counted: with a court letter and a goal up it makes a third row covering about 40% of a 390x844 screen.
- **Keys:** Esc is Back (`src/data/ui.js` `back: ['Escape']`); + and - zoom; ] and [ change speed. `noZoom` stops page zoom in the browser.
- **The dock's icons** are text glyphs (═ ⌂ ❋ ✚ ⚒ ⛊ ▲), drawn by whichever font the OS has.
- **Performance:** the 9.3 follow-ups measured phone p50 +8 to 11% and desktop DPR 1 +29 to 41% over 9.2; the stress city is 3.5x and the Huge map 2.5x over the targets in headless Edge; a desktop far view holds about 130 MB of canvas. The budget tests pass because their bounds are looser than the 9.1 targets the follow-ups measure against (`tests/render/stress.test.js` allows 50 ms a frame; the 9.1 follow-ups, lines 31-33, report the stress city 3.5x and the Huge map 2.5x over target in headless Edge).
- **Nothing exists for a store:** no `package.json`, no Electron, no Steam files, no packaging script. Josh's machine has no Node (the 6.0 plan already said "prebuilt Electron, no Node needed").
- **No real device** has run the game since 7.0; every phone result is emulated, and the music has been heard by nobody on a speaker.

## 3. The Electron shell for Steam (Track W)

**3a. The shape** (High, every Steam player, M).
- **Where it lives:** `electron/main.js`, `electron/preload.js`, `electron/package.json` (`main: "main.js"`, no dependencies, so no npm). The game's files are copied beside them unchanged.
- **Loading:** a privileged custom scheme `app://thebes/` registered before `ready` (standard, secure, fetch allowed) and served by `protocol.handle` from the app folder. Why not `file://`: Chromium refuses ES module imports from `file://` origins and gives IndexedDB a null origin; `app://thebes` gives the game a stable origin, so saves persist across updates. Index is `app://thebes/index.html?host=steam` (the query is a belt to the braces below, so a page opened by hand still knows).
- **The host bridge:** `preload.js` exposes `window.thebesHost = { platform: 'steam', storeVersion, build, quit(), onBeforeQuit(cb) }` through `contextBridge`. `contextIsolation` on, `nodeIntegration` off, `sandbox` on, no remote module, `webSecurity` on. The renderer never sees Node.
- **Window:** opens fullscreen on the primary display; F11 toggles fullscreen and is the only key that does (Esc stays the game's Back, ruling below); the window's last size, position and fullscreen state are kept in `window.json` under `app.getPath('userData')` and restored, with a floor of 960x600 and a check that the saved position is on a connected display. `setVisualZoomLevelLimits(1, 1)` and the menu bar off (`autoHideMenuBar`, no default menu, so Alt does nothing). The background colour is the parchment, so the first frame is not white.
- **Quit:** closing the window (the X, Alt+F4, the game's own Quit item) sends `host:before-quit` to the renderer, which saves through the existing autosave path and answers `host:saved`; the window closes on the answer or after 2 s, whichever first. The game's menu gains **Quit** in the wrapper only, next to Home ("Saves your city and closes Thebes").
- **Export in the wrapper:** `downloadFile` stays; `main.js` handles `will-download` with a save dialog that defaults to the player's Documents folder and the export's file name. Bring rulers' file picker works as it is.
- **Errors:** an uncaught error in the main process writes `crash.txt` to userData and shows a plain dialog ("Thebes stopped. The file crash.txt in [path] says why; the last save is kept.") before quitting. The renderer's error card is unchanged.
- **What the game does when `thebesHost.platform === 'steam'`** (read once in `readEnvironment` as `env.host`, so every later check is a pure function of `env`):
  - no service worker (the files are local; the cache and the Update ready toast are meaningless), no `beforeinstallprompt`, no Install button, no install asks or notices, no iOS guide;
  - the share sheet is not offered (export goes straight to the download path);
  - the title and About show the store version (section 3b); the menu shows Quit;
  - How to play opens on the keys section first (it is a desktop);
  - Options gains one line under Display: "Fullscreen: F11".
- **Proof:** `tests/ui/host.test.js` boots with an injected `thebesHost` and checks each item above (no SW registration call, no Install button, Quit present, the version label, How to play's first section). The shell itself is proved by the smoke run (3d).

**3b. Versions on the store** (Medium, S). `STORE_VERSION = '1.0'` beside `APP_VERSION` in `src/data/ui.js`. In the wrapper the title's version line reads "1.0" and About reads "1.0 (build 10.0 · sha)"; What's new's newest entry is headed "1.0 on Steam" with the 10.0 entry's text and every older entry stays (the game's history is part of the game). On the web nothing changes. Ruling 401's rule (one constant, the newest release must match) now covers both constants: a test fails if `STORE_VERSION` is not in the newest entry's `store` field.

**3c. Packaging without Node** (High, M). `tools/electron-pack.ps1`:
- downloads the pinned Electron release zip (`electron-v<X>-win32-x64.zip`, version and SHA-256 written in the script; the download is refused if the hash differs) and `rcedit-x64.exe` (pinned the same way) into `build/dl/`, once;
- unzips to `build/win/`, renames `electron.exe` to `Thebes.exe`, sets its icon (from `tools/render-assets.ps1`'s icon, now also written as `.ico`), product name, file description and version with rcedit;
- copies `electron/*` and every file in `sw-files.js`'s list (the one list of what the game needs, so a file the game needs and the package lacks is impossible unless the SW list is also stale, and `tools/sw-files.ps1` runs first) into `build/win/resources/app/`;
- leaves Electron's own `LICENSE` and `LICENSES.chromium.html` in place (their licences ask for it) and writes `THIRD-PARTY.txt` beside them naming Electron and Chromium;
- prints the folder's size and file count. `build/` is gitignored.
- **Proof:** the script runs on a clean checkout; the folder holds exactly the SW list plus the shell files; `Thebes.exe` launches (3d).

**3d. The smoke run** (High, S). `tools/electron-smoke.ps1` starts `build/win/Thebes.exe --smoke=<png>`: in smoke mode `main.js` opens a 1280x800 window, not fullscreen, loads the title, waits for the renderer's `host:ready` (sent by `boot.js` when the title shows, on any host), captures the page to the PNG, then quits; exit code 0 only if ready came within 60 s and the PNG exists. A second mode `--smoke-play=<png>` presses Play by script and captures the lesson's first card. This is the check before every Steam upload and the proof of 3a on a clean Windows account (ruling: proved on a user account that has never run Node, Edge DevTools or the dev server, so a dependency on Josh's setup shows).

**3e. Steam files** (Medium, S). `steam/app_build.vdf` and `steam/depot_build.vdf` with the app and depot ids as placeholders (`THEBES_APPID`, `THEBES_DEPOTID`), content root `build/win`, default branch `beta`; `tools/steam-upload.ps1 -Branch beta` runs the smoke run, then SteamCMD (a standalone Valve download, pinned in the script's notes; its login is interactive on first use and Josh's) with `+run_app_build`. `steam/README.md` lists the partner-site steps in order: create the app, one depot (Windows 64), one launch option (`Thebes.exe`), the `beta` branch for testing and the manual "set live" to default, the store assets (3f), and what 10.0 does not do: no Steamworks SDK, so no overlay, no achievements, no Steam Cloud (10.1 candidates; the overlay needs the SDK in the process and is not promised on the page).

**3f. The store page set** (Medium, S). `tools/shots.ps1 -Store` renders six 1920x1080 scenes from `devScene` (a village at the river in the Village look, a Market town with a bazaar crowd, a raid at the tower, a monument half built, the Capital street, the great temple) and the same six at 1080x1920, a 616x353 capsule and a 1232x706 header from the title's backdrop with the cartouche, named `store-<scene>-<WxH>.png` in `docs/superpowers/notes/screenshots/store/`. The page's one-line pitch is the title card's sentence ("The king has made you vizier of the village at Thebes. Make it a city fit to be named the Seat of the Two Lands, and crown your service with the great temple."), the page says the web version exists and is free (Josh's call, section 10), and the "about" text names the lesson's length and the 3-to-6 hour arc so no review can say "short" with surprise.

## 4. The first view and the look (Track V)

**4a. The camera keeps the map on screen** (High, every player, S).
- **The rule:** the view's centre is held inside the map by a margin, so that at most about 30% of the view's area is off-map: `holdInMap(cam, view)` clamps `cam.x, cam.y` to `[m, size - m]` on each axis with `m = min(0.7 × half the view's extent in tiles at the zoom, size / 2)`. When the whole map fits in the view (a small map zoomed out on a desktop), the clamp centres the map. The 6.0 rule (the centre may reach the edge) is replaced; the Huge zoom floor is unchanged.
- **Where it applies:** every camera write: the start of a city, Show me frames, drags, flings, pinches, Fit the city, a loaded save's camera (clamped on load, so an old save opens inside the map).
- **The start:** a new city still frames the entry road's end diamond, offset inward by the margin, so the diamond sits in the map-side third of the screen, not the middle.
- **Proof:** `tests/render/holdInMap.test.js` (the clamp by zoom, map size and view; the whole-map case; a save's camera clamped on load); a screenshot test at 390x844 and 1920x1080 of a fresh classic city counts the off-map pixels (the tan fill is the one colour not in the terrain palette) under 30%.

**4b. The off-map ground drawn** (High, every player, S).
- **What:** the off-map area is desert that pales and loses detail with distance from the map: a 1-tile soft shadow ramp along the map's edge (the edge reads as the land's rim, not a cut), then a sand fill with sparse dune strokes seeded by position, fading toward the horizon to a warm haze colour; no rocks, plants or figures. It follows the four looks' palette (the Village sand) and the day-night tint. In the Still motion level nothing moves (it never moves anyway).
- **Cost:** one pattern fill and one gradient per frame; the dune strokes are baked into a 256 px repeating tile at boot. Measured in the budget tests; no bound loosens.
- **Proof:** a screenshot of a fresh city on each map size at 390x844 and 1280x800, before and after; the render budget tests pass unchanged.

**4c. The scribe in the phone budget** (Medium, phones, S). The scribe's bubble counts as the guidance row: while an alert row shows, the bubble takes the guidance row's slot (the goal, tip, caption and legend wait as they do for each other, ruling 176's turn-taking), and never a third row. On a wide layout nothing changes. **Proof:** `mobileBudget.test.js` gains the scene (court letter + goal + scribe) and fails today; the `sample-390` screenshot is retaken.

**4d. Button language** (Medium, S). One look per meaning: filled lapis for the one primary action on a surface (the only "Show me" style), outlined for the rest, a chosen state (the frieze-edged ring) only for a choice that is on (so Layers "Off" is outlined when off, not filled), and Demolish outlined with the red glyph (filled red only while the Demolish tool is live). C1, C3, C4 of the 11 Nov mobile review. **Proof:** a CSS contract test lists every `.pill.primary` outside its surface's one primary and must be empty; screenshots of the tray, Layers and the lesson card.

**4e. Foot shadows** (Low, S). The raider chariot, elephant, raider boat (on water: a darker ripple, not a shadow), ibis, heron, hippo, fishing wharf and charred stump get the contact shadow every other sprite has (ruling 1929). **Proof:** the art contract test that lists sprites without a shadow is extended to these and must be empty.

**4f. Drawn dock and panel icons** (Medium, S). The seven category glyphs become small drawn icons from the game's own art (a road tile, a home, a grain ear, an ankh, a pick, a shield, a pyramid), 20 px ink line drawings in `--ink` on the parchment, with the chosen state as today. The same icons serve the wide panel's tabs. **Proof:** a screenshot test on Windows headless Edge and a visual check in Josh's pass on iOS and Android; the glyph characters appear in no `*Text.js`.

## 5. The desktop scale (Track D)

- **Default scale by width** (High, every Steam player, S). On a pointer-primary device (`(pointer: fine)` and no coarse pointer) with no stored text-size choice, `--ui-scale` defaults to 1.25 from 1600 px wide and 1.5 from 2200 px (so 1920x1080 reads at 1.25, a 2560x1440 at 1.5, a 4K at 1.5 too; a 1366x768 laptop stays at 1). The Options text size shows the default as "Auto (125%)" and any choice overrides it. The wide panel, the minimap, the cards and the title card scale with it (they use `--ui-scale` tokens already; the panel's `--panel-w` and `--drawer-w` gain the factor). The map's own zoom is untouched.
- **The top bar's date** (Low, S). On a wide layout "Year 2, month 9, day 1" becomes "Y2 · M9 · D1" with the full words in the stat explainer on hover or tap, so the bar never crowds at 1.5.
- **Proof:** `tests/ui/uiScale.test.js` (the default by width and pointer, the override, the stored choice winning); screenshots of the title, a sample city and the tray at 1920x1080 and 2560x1440, before and after; the 1920 title card is at least 560 px wide (ruling 1673 closed).

## 6. Performance (Track P)

- **The budget tests mean it** (High, M). The bounds in `tests/render/stress.test.js` and `tests/v90/v/budget.test.js` tighten to the 9.1 targets (the follow-ups' numbers) and a new `tests/render/phoneBudget.test.js` runs the same scenes at 390x844 DPR 2. They fail at 3582196 by the 9.3 margins; the track ends when they pass on this machine.
- **The work, in order of expected gain:** (1) warm the sprite cache for the band just outside the view in idle frames (the 9.3 note's own next step), so a pan draws from cache; (2) cap the far-view canvas: at zoom 0.6 and under the terrain chunks bake at 2 device pixels per art pixel, not 3, and the chunk cache's ceiling follows the device memory rule from 9.3 §7; (3) the 9.3 blending bake measured and its two-a-frame limit kept; (4) the off-map pattern (4b) counted.
- **Proof:** the tests above; a before-and-after table in the followups note at 390x844 DPR 2, 1280x800 and 1920x1080 for the stress city, the Huge map and the Capital look.

## 9. What does not change

- **Game rules:** the sim, the balance, the lesson's steps, the save format (`WORLD_VERSION` 12), the records. 10.0 adds no content.
- **The web version:** every change in sections 4 to 7 reaches it; nothing in section 3 does except `host.test.js`'s plumbing and the smoke `host:ready` message, which is harmless on the web.
- **Standing rules:** no colour-blind work (ruling 1700, Josh's call); "Pharaoh" in no game text; the sim stays deterministic; nothing needs the network after load (610); one panel at a time and nothing floating over the middle of the map; the employer's name in no public file.
- **Not in 10.0:** the Steamworks SDK (overlay, achievements, cloud), a Linux or Mac build, Steam Deck checks, controller input, Google Play, asar packing, code signing (a Windows SmartScreen warning is Steam's to absorb; outside Steam the exe is not distributed).

## 10. Open questions for the 10.0 plan

- **Q1. Order of waves.** Recommended: Josh's real-device pass first (it can run while Track 0 writes the contract); wave 1 = V, D, P; wave 2 = W, M; then S and the final review. Why: V, D and P change what W packages and what the pass's findings fix.
- **Q2. The Electron version.** Recommended: the newest stable at the time Track 0 runs, pinned by hash in the script and named in the followups note; moved only by a later release.
- **Q3. The scale steps.** Recommended: 1.25 from 1600 px and 1.5 from 2200 px, confirmed on Josh's own monitor in the pass.
- **Q4. The 30% off-map rule.** Recommended: 30%, with the fresh-city screenshots at three sizes as the check; if the 48 map at a desktop zoom-out centres awkwardly, the clamp's whole-map case keeps the map centred.
- **Q5. The free web version on the page.** Recommended: say it exists, link it, call it the demo. Why: hiding it is the surest "not what I expected" review, and a player who tried the web version refunds less.
- **Q6. The version shown on Steam.** Recommended: 1.0 outside, 10.0 inside (About shows both).
- **Q7. Price.** Josh's, not the plan's; the page text (3f) is written for a 3-to-6 hour game at the low end of the small-city-builder range.
