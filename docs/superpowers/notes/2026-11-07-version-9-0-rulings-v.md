# Version 9.0 "Dynasty": rulings 1600-1619 (Track V, animations and visual effects)

The frame is src/render/renderer.js (`DPR_CAP`, the walk slot in the depth pass), src/render/ambient.js (`createFrameGate`'s running cap, `SMOKE_FROM`, the flow) and src/render/loop.js (the frame's game time); the effects are src/render/v90/ (`effects.js` the seam's layers, `dropper.js` V's budget, `clock.js`, `scaffold.js`, `moment.js` and `raidMoment.js`, `layers.js` dust, fire fought, festival and weather, `wakes.js`), src/render/art/figures.js and art/armies.js (the walk slots, the raised spear), src/render/units.js (the glide, the fight's spot), src/render/monuments.js (the sleds' dust), src/render/trade.js and ferries.js (the wakes), src/render/effects.js (the scaffold's notes) and src/ui/v60/battle.js (the moment's call); data in src/data/ambient.js (`runningFpsCap`, `v90BudgetMs`, `v90DropOrder`); css/v90.css. Tests: tests/v90/v/.

1600. The canvas draws at most at DPR 2, and a running game at most 60 frames a second (the paused and idle 30 stays); the sim still runs on every frame the loop sees. The renderer's `dpr` is the canvas's, so the minimap made from it is at 2 on a DPR-3 phone too. — Cost if wrong: a DPR-3 phone's map is a little softer.
1601. No reduced motion (Josh, 7 Nov): the device's Reduce motion is not read, production stays Full (ruling 1147 stands), and 9.0's effects are Full only, with no Gentle or Still forms. — Cost if wrong: a player who turned Reduce motion on still sees every effect; adding the forms later means touching each effect.
1602. V's effects share a 2 ms budget on the stress city with their own dropper (the river's flow, the weather, the dust, 9.0's workshop smoke, the festival's lamps, petals and pennants shed in that order, at most one every 2 s and back after 10 s under half the budget); the scaffolds, the beaten raid, a fire fought and the wakes are never shed. An effect that would break T8 or T9 is cut and reported. — Cost if wrong: a slow phone sees fewer effects.
1603. Every effect is drawing only: a building's rise, the scaffold and the beaten raid's moment never change what the sim does or when; none is saved, so a reload shows none of them. — Cost if wrong: none to the sim.
1604. The panels' fade is off in the lesson (`body.lesson-on`); every map effect is on the canvas, under the overlays and the page, so none can cover a lesson pointer. — Cost if wrong: the lesson's panels snap open.
1605. The walk cycle is two poses and a mirrored copy of each, made from each figure's own drawing (its feet moved out for the stride, in with a pixel's bob for the passing step), changing every half tile walked; a figure heading left on screen (dir 2 or 3) is mirrored, and one standing (a unit whose glide is done, a walker with nowhere to go) shows its plain drawing. Chariots and the elephant are mirrored only; a fighting pose keeps its own facing. All of a kind's walk slots are drawn into the cache the first time the kind is drawn at a zoom, so a frame's cost stays flat once warm. — Cost if wrong: about six sprites a kind in the cache (some 30 kinds), a few hundred kilobytes.
1606. A unit's glide starts on the day the sim moved it (the whole day of the frame's time) and takes 0.6 of a day, so it stands where the sim has it for the rest of the day: never a day behind (it was up to a whole day). — Cost if wrong: a unit marches in short pushes rather than gliding the whole day.
1607. A building's rise keeps 8.4's 900 ms reveal, so a building placed while paused still appears at once; V3 adds scaffold poles at its three front corners for one game day (half a day for a home's upgrade), with a dust puff at the start, at most 8 sites, never on a monument or rubble. Held while paused, as game time is. — Cost if wrong: a paused city shows poles until it runs a day.
1608. The panels "slide" (V12) is an opacity fade (160 ms for a sheet and the side panel, 150 ms for a toast), not a move: a transform would put the boxes the layout tests and a tap measure somewhere else mid-way. — Cost if wrong: the panels fade rather than slide.
1609. The beaten raid's map moment stands at the last place blows were traded (else the last raider's fall) in this raid's last 20 days, found on the first frame after the raid ends; it lasts MOMENT.days game days, or until the next raid is warned or starts, and is kept outside the world (a reload drops it, as the alert row's moment). The soldiers out within 4 tiles of it hold their spears up ('soldier_cheer'); the chieftain's banner lies where he fell when the report says one fell. A raid lost has none. — Cost if wrong: a raid beaten by towers alone, with no fall seen, has no moment on the map.
1610. A bare frame (renderFrame with no clock: a test's or a preview's) draws only what the world holds, the same each time: no weather, the rest at time 0. The game always passes its clock. — Cost if wrong: none in play.
1611. A fire fought is drawn from the sim's own cover (`staffedCover`): the arcs come from the nearest working firehouse within 12 tiles of a burning building it covers (at most 6 fires), and steam rises for 2.6 s where a burning building is no longer burning and still stands. — Cost if wrong: the arcs may come from a nearer firehouse than the one whose crew the sim counts.
