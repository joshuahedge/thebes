# Version 10.0.1, Track S: rulings 2355-2374 (the rules)

Where the plan and the code disagreed, the code won; each ruling says both. Numbers measured on this branch (`v1001-s`, cut at `7ee481b`).

- **2355. Defenders go anywhere: the nearest raider to the unit, the lower id on a tie; no patrol is kept anywhere.** `combatDay` (`src/sim/combat.js`) picks each unit's foe with `nearestRaider` over every live raider it does not shun (Track E's `SHUNS`: your chariots never go to a war elephant), measured from the unit, ties to the lower id; with none left it walks home as before (`walkHome`, the chariot by `driveTowards`, the foot by `stepFoot`'s walk round water). The archer's post (`archerPost`) loses its "within its patrol of its building" test and its `home` argument. The plan reads as "a unit with no raider in reach marches to the nearest anywhere"; with no patrol left, "in reach" and "anywhere" are the same rule, so there is one. `DEFENCE.patrol` (8) and `UNIT_TYPES[kind].patrol` (8, 10, 12) are gone from the data (`src/data/raids.js`, `src/data/armies.js`) rather than left unread. Towers keep `towerRange` 4. `src/render/overlays.js` `RANGE_OF` drew a barracks' patrol as a ring of tiles when it is selected; it is `{ tower }` alone now, so a selected barracks tints as other buildings do (a one-line edit in a file no 10.0.1 track owns). Determinism: the same raid twice gives the same world (`tests/v1001/s/rules.test.js`).
- **2356. The words: no "within N tiles" for units.** New strings under `// Version 10.0.1, Track S` in `src/ui/text.js` (`V1001S_TEXT`): the barracks', archery range's and stables' Patrol row reads "Anywhere on the map" (`src/ui/dangerSheets.js`, `src/ui/s46/armies.js`; was "8 tiles", "10 tiles", "12 tiles"); the raid alert's fix reads "Towers hit raiders within 4 tiles; soldiers go after raiders anywhere" ("your units", "your archers", "your chariots" as before by who is at home; was "soldiers fight within 8 tiles of their barracks" and "your units go out within 8 to 12 tiles"), every one inside 10.0's banner budget (the old soldiers' line, 83 characters); `S46_ARMY_TEXT.patrolLine` (read by nothing) is overridden to say the same. The two old functions (`ALERT_TEXT.raid` and the Stage 4.6 wrapper) call the new string in place, since they read the removed patrol. Pins moved: `tests/stage3/raids/warning.test.js` (the fix), `tests/stage46/armies/words.test.js` (the sheet rows, the alert lines, the budget's line kept as 10.0's literal, the chariots line).
- **2357. Raiders 1.5 tiles a day, every kind x 1.5, and a band's fractional step.** `UNITS.raider.speed` 1 -> 1.5, `UNITS.soldier.speed` 2 -> 3; `ENEMY_TYPES` archer 1 -> 1.5, chariot 2 -> 3, elephant 1 -> 1.5 (foot and boat read `UNITS.raider`); `UNIT_TYPES` archer 2 -> 3, chariot 3 -> 4.5. `bandDay` (`src/sim/raids.js`) adds the band's pace to `rec.stride` (the part of a tile not yet walked) and takes its whole tiles: 1.5 is one step, then two. Steps a wall or the goal cut short are not kept (as before); the strike's waiting days add nothing. The plan's `step < speed` loop would have taken two steps a day at 1.5 (0 and 1 are both below it). `stride` lives on the band's record (`raid.active`, `raid.active.second`) and only while a part is left, so a whole pace (a band of chariots alone: 3) leaves the record exactly as before. A save holds it inside `raid.active`, a free record in the save check (`isRaid` reads only `active === null || isRecord(active)`), and a save without it reads as 0: no new world field, `WORLD_VERSION` stays 12. `bandSpeed`'s cap (`UNITS.raider.speed * 4`, read by `src/sim/armies.js enemySpeed` too) is 6 now; no kind comes near it. The raid sheet's counter lines read the speeds: "they ride 3 tiles a day, faster than raiders on foot (1.5)". Raids still drop the game to 1x when they start (unchanged). Pins moved: `tests/stage3/contract/dangerData.test.js` (`UNITS`, `DEFENCE`), `tests/stage46/armies/armies.test.js` (the soldier's numbers: speed for patrol), `tests/stage46/armies/raids.test.js` (a band's pace over two days: 6 and 3 tiles).

- **2362. Storehouses fetch for their neighbours: the rule as built.** `src/sim/carts.js autoFetchMap(world)`: a store (storehouse or granary) with no orders of its own keeps stocked, as a Keep stocked order of `AUTO_FETCH.amount` (40, two cart loads; `src/data/s47.js`) would, each good that (1) a working workshop consumes (not a brickworks or quarry Advice has idled: `blocked 'idle'`) with the store within its `producerReach`, or a working bazaar sells, one of the foods or household goods, while its stall has none of it left, with the store within its `supplyReach`; (2) no other store within that workshop's or bazaar's reach holds; (3) some store beyond that reach does hold (the plan's "goods held further away": nothing to fetch otherwise, and the sheet would name goods no one has); (4) the store is built to hold and takes in (`storeAccepts`), and is not today the source of a cart carrying it. The store's own stock is not counted in (2): a store fetching for its neighbours goes on until it holds `AUTO_FETCH.amount`, and keeps that much from other stores' orders, which is what stops two stores taking turns to draw a good from each other. The plan's "nearest store with surplus anywhere" is `findSource`'s rule as it stands; `nextTrip` serves a store's automatic orders when it has no others, in the goods' own order with the biggest share wanting first, and `cartDay` works the map out once a day, before any cart sets out, and dispatches the stores in their order, the automatic ones among them. No ping-pong: `findSource` passes over a store fetching the same good for its neighbours when the home store is one too, and a store fetching a good for its neighbours keeps `AUTO_FETCH.amount` of it from other stores' orders, as its own order would (`spareOf`). The plan's "never fetches a good it is the source of" is (4).
