# Version 9.0 "Dynasty": fix wave rulings (1630-1639: D 1630-1633, L 1634-1636, V 1637-1639)

1630. Every number a sentence quotes about the flood, the unit pay and the work gold is the sim's for this city: the Nilometer's note reads `levelHarvest` (prepared) and `heirLoss` (not), the pay lines multiply the data's pay by `heirFactor 'pay'` (one unit's, to a tenth of a gold; the whole bill is still rounded once in `soldiersPayOf`), and the crews' work gold a month multiplies by `heirFactor 'crews'`. — Cost if wrong: a figure a tenth of a gold off on a unit's line; the bill itself is unchanged.
1631. The rows before a first flood say this city's levels: the farm row's range is `heirLoss` over every level (src/ui/s46/river.js passes it to `S46_RIVER_TEXT.farmRow`), and the First flood card's words come from `FIRST_TEXT.first_flood.whyFor(world)` (createUI reads it when a card has one; the plain `why` stays 8.4's). — Cost if wrong: a Bountiful city reads 60% for a Low flood on those two lines until the flood has risen.
1632. Pass the throne writes the ruler row first, and the city's row and the chronicle stamp only once the row is read back as kept; a failed Pass puts the dynasty's name back as it was and the seal undone. — Cost if wrong: a failed Pass leaves a city row and a stamp for a reign that carried on.
1633. The Pass sheet gives `passSheetExtras` the sealed score as `{ total, parts }` (the kept seal's own parts), and draws the side by side above Pass the throne and Not yet, below the score and name parts. The name field's hint lists the allowed characters. — Cost if wrong: the side by side showed today's parts beside a sealed total, or sat under the buttons.
1634. Bring rulers' Merge keeps the device's dynasty (heir and name) when the device already has rulers, and takes the file's dynasty only on a device with none (a new device restoring); in that case the answer says before Merge "Your next city will be heir of the Builder, under the name Ramesses". — Cost if wrong: a restoring device would start as the Steady with no name until the player sets them.
1635. Play this map's choices (level, size, layout, seed) are applied to the New city card at once, and given back when the card is backed out of (Back, Esc, or Back from the replace question); a city starting keeps them. — Cost if wrong: a player who looks at a map and backs out keeps its level and seed for the next New city.
1636. The side by side's line wraps under 480px wide (name and number above, the bar across the full width), so large text never squeezes the bar to a sliver. — Cost if wrong: at 390px and 150% text the bars read as stubs.
1637. The running frame cap steps a due time by a whole period (1000 / 60 ms, 2 ms of slack, resynced when a loop falls a period behind) instead of measuring from the last frame drawn less 4 ms, so 90, 120 and 144 Hz screens all draw close to 60 a second (the gate test holds each within 55-61); the sim still steps every frame, and the idle 30 cap steps the same way. — Cost if wrong: a screen whose frames land oddly against the due times draws a frame more or less than 60 in a second.
1638. Each layer is counted in one budget only: V's effects (the river's flow, the wakes, the weather, the dust, 9.0's workshop smoke, the festival and the rest) in V's dropper, and ambient's own (glints, ripples, sway, rare life, its own smoke) in ambient's, which leaves out whatever V spent while it drew. The weaver's, jeweller's and builders' guild's smoke is timed into V's budget, so ruling 1602's shed order for V stands and ambient's rare, smoke, sway order runs on ambient's own cost. — Cost if wrong: a frame's time is split by measured spans, so a layer timed oddly can count a little to the wrong side.
1639. Small leftovers of V: a ferry's wake is drawn only while the crossing's own boat is (not while a cart's boat has taken its place); the dead #panel fade in css/v90.css is removed (the side panel never toggles hidden; the sheet and the toast keep theirs); a dust puff fades in over the first fifth of its life and a sand gust fades in and out over its window; and V's own Still and Gentle branches (the trade wake's, the river flow's) are removed, effects being Full only (ruling 1601), while the older 8.x motion branches stay as they were. — Cost if wrong: none to speak of; a Reduce motion form would be added back by hand.
