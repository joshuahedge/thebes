# Version 9.2, Track A (reachable for everyone): rulings 1825-1834

Each ruling is one line with its cost if wrong.

1825. The confirm dialog is a real modal: Cancel takes focus once when the question comes up (not on every store change), Tab and Shift+Tab cycle among its buttons wherever focus was, closing it returns focus to the control that was focused when it opened, and `#dialog` has `z-index: 100` (above the confirm bar's 3 and the Layers popover's 6). Cost if wrong: a sheet that rebuilds the dialog's opener would leave focus on the page body (the restore skips a disconnected opener).
1826. A sheet opened from the keyboard takes focus at its title, makes the bars, rails, corners and dock behind it `inert` (the first pointer-down anywhere lifts that, so a touch user is never blocked and the map stays tappable), and any sheet, however opened, gives focus back to its opener on close. A sheet opened by a tap or click does NOT take focus in (the brief said "however opened"): an open sheet with focus inside is skipped by the daily refresh (createUI refreshDaily), so a touch player's numbers would go stale, and tests/ui/createUI.test.js pins that refresh. A sheet the game opens by itself never takes focus. Cost if wrong: a mouse player using a screen reader gets no focus move on a click-opened sheet.
1827. Words at large text: the hiding rules at 125% and above for the Layers legend's name and keys, the Goods supply name (at large text only: at 100% it stays hidden, the 30% budget, with the original row order), the tip's label and the lesson's label now show the words, smaller (12 px times the text step up to 1.25), and wrap. NOT restored: the Ground legend's title, room key and hills rule (the Ground legend shows while a farm is placed, and with its words the 40% budget and the ghost's frame in tests/v70/p/review.test.js fail) and the cheer's "why" (a second sentence, not a label; tests/ui/celebrate.test.js pins it cut). The top bar's stat labels do not exist as elements any more (8.1 ruling 1223 replaced each word with a picture and the name lives in the button's aria-label), so the two `.label` hiding rules there were dead and are removed; T10 asserts instead that each stat keeps its picture and name and nothing runs out of the bar. Older tests updated: tests/ui/tips.test.js (the Tip label is on screen at larger text). Cost if wrong: Josh may want words under the top-bar pictures at 150% (the bar gets about 16 px taller) or the Ground title back with a taller budget; neither was done.
1828. A fourth text step, 200% (`data-text-size="200"`, `--ui-scale: 2`), in Options' Text size. Every large-text rule keyed on 150% now also names 200% (mechanical: `[data-text-size="150"]` became `:is([data-text-size="150"], [data-text-size="200"])`, and the 125/150 pairs gained 200). The mobile budget runs at 200% on the portrait phones with 70% of the height as the limit on a 667 px phone and half (55% with no sheet) on a 844 px one, because 200% cannot meet 40%; on 375x667 the farm, farm-raid and raid-overlay scenes end at 59-66%; nothing hides, the legend wraps. Cost if wrong: landscape phones at 200% are not budgeted.
1829. Pinch zoom is allowed (Q7): the viewport meta no longer has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=no`, the chrome's `touch-action` is `pan-x pan-y pinch-zoom`, and `noZoom.js` cancels iOS gesture events only on the map canvas. The double-tap guard on bare chrome stays. Older test `tests/v81/d/noZoom.test.js` updated to say so. Cost if wrong: an accidental two-finger touch on the bars can zoom the page (the player can pinch back).
1830. Teal text token `--teal` and `TOKENS.teal` are #1A5F57 (5.44:1 on parchment-2; the bridge's tint and the share card keep the old teal, as they are pictures). `TEXT_PAIRS` grows from 14 to 28 (teal, coral and amber words on parchment-2 and their soft grounds, lapis pairs, muted ink on parchment-2 and the soft grounds, coral and amber on ink), all at 4.5:1 or better. The two sub-12 px sizes (the locked layer's lock and name) are 12 px times the text step; the badge (to 125% of its size: more covers the icon it sits on), the error card's details and the help chevron scale with the text setting. The "More" links keep their 44 px hit area (an `::before` over the 12 px word, as `tests/ui/a11y.test.js` measures), not a 44 px box, because a 44 px box would cover the row's last words. Cost if wrong: pairs on parchment-3 for coral and amber words are not listed because they fall under 4.5:1 and no rule sets them.
1831. The minimap canvas is a control: `role="application"`, the name "Minimap", a description, `tabindex="0"`, and the arrow keys move the view by a sixteenth of the city (`onMove`, wired to the camera's pan in createUI for both minimaps). Cost if wrong: `application` makes some screen readers pass keys through; the arrows are the only keys it takes.
1832. The alerts' live region is two: `#live` stays polite for good and `#live-urgent` (added beside it in index.html and made by `createAlerts` if absent) stays assertive for good; a change says its words in the one that fits and empties the other. The tests that read `#live`'s aria-live flipping were updated (`tests/stage3/ui/alertTiers.test.js`). Cost if wrong: a rig without the second element gets one made beside the first.
1833. Screenshot mode keeps a small pinned "Show interface" button (44 px, bottom right, text "Show interface" in `A92_TEXT`), the only thing on screen; `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light">` is in the head. Cost if wrong: the button is in the picture of a screenshot; a tap or Esc still exits.
1834. The compact rail names each icon in a 12 px word under it (inside the 44 px button, so the rail's height does not grow); the buttons take the rail's whole width and the word is set slightly tight (-0.05em) so "Monuments" fits the unchanged 56 px rail (a 62 or 66 px rail narrowed the legend and broke the fire legend's 40% budget at 667x375, 150%). Cost if wrong: "Monuments" may clip a pixel or two on a font wider than the test browser's.
