# Version 9.3, Track G: ground, yards and streets (rulings 1910-1919)

One line each, with the cost if wrong.

- **1910. Soft edges.** `blobFor` has sixteen points a diamond (a corner, three quarter points on each side) and a second, finer octave of seeded noise (one hash a point on a quarter-tile grid, `EDGE_DETAIL`), so a straight edge between grounds leaves its ideal line by at least 1 art px within every tile. A tile with the ground all round it keeps its old eight points and no fine octave. Cost if wrong: edges look ragged (turn `EDGE_DETAIL` down) or bake time rises on a map with long edges.
- **1911. Edge details.** `edgeFringe` puts a few marks within a third of a tile of each side that meets another ground: grass tufts and green flecks at a meadow's edge, wet-sand flecks along the water, loam crumbs where the floodplain meets sand, pebbles at rock. The river's outer bank is cut into quarter-tile steps and pushed by seeded noise, with a darker damp line between the bank and the halo. Marks are collected and drawn in batches (`src/render/batch.js`), and the fertile speckle goes through the same batches. Cost if wrong: marks read as noise at far zoom.
- **1912. Yards.** Every building but farms, walls, gates, bridges, rubble, plazas, gardens, statues and sphinxes has a trodden yard 0.3 tile past its footprint, in `MATERIALS[look].ground` (packed earth, swept gravel, a City front paved with a kerb, a Capital court with joints and a gold border). Buildings of one look with a tile or less between them share one patch (a bridge rectangle over the gap). `drawPlots` skips buildings that have a yard. Cost if wrong: a yard under a type that should stand on bare ground.
- **1913. Paths.** A worn path runs straight out from a footprint side to the nearest road tile when one or two free tiles lie between (reach 3), front sides first; none when a road touches the footprint. Cost if wrong: a path that crosses a yard it should not.
- **1914. The signature.** `chunkSignature` gains, for each building tile, its four footprint-edge bits and the building's look, and for each road tile its look, only above the Village look (`lookReader`, `src/render/groundLooks.js`, holds each look until the day, the rank, the splits or the buildings change). The two-tile ring stays: a path or kerb decision that reaches past it can lag until the next re-bake. Cost if wrong: a chunk re-bakes a frame late at a ring edge. The signature costs about 0.03 ms more a chunk, and nothing when the city is a Village.
- **1915. Roads by look.** The road's look is `lookAt` of the tile. Village: today's track with wheel dust fraying into the sand and dust flecks. Town: gravel with a firm pebble edge. City: stone kerbs. Capital: paved with joints and a gold centre line. A segment draws in the look of its first tile. Cost if wrong: a road changes look a tile before its neighbour.
- **1916. Paving in a City.** A City road is paved only where a building stands within a tile of the road tile; elsewhere it is gravel inside stone kerbs. A Capital road is always paved. Cost if wrong: a long empty avenue stays gravel.
- **1917. Trees and lamps.** From the City look, a built-up straight road tile with a free verge tile carries a street tree every third tile (City) or a tree and a lamp alternating on every fourth tile (Capital), on the verge at the road's edge, baked after the road. Cost if wrong: a tree drawn on a tile a later building sprite covers.
- **1918. Contact shadows.** A building's walls have two soft shadow layers at the base (the light is from the upper left), one path a layer for the whole chunk. Cost if wrong: a shadow under a sprite that already draws its own.
- **1919. Bake time.** Measured on the stress city in the City look, bakeChunk at scale 1, the baseline and this branch in one page, interleaved, best of eight rounds, headless Edge on the desktop with other builds running (so the figures are high, the ratio is the point): Normal (64, 16 chunks) 108 ms to 171 ms in all (6.8 ms to 10.7 ms a chunk); Large (80, 25 chunks) 218 ms to 329 ms (8.7 ms to 13.2 ms a chunk). About a half more, mostly the yards (about 3.5 ms a chunk on the stress city, which holds 2,900 buildings) and the ground's finer edges. The two-bakes-a-frame limit is unchanged. Cost if wrong: two bakes in a frame take about 1.5 times as long on a slow phone, and P (wave 2) should look at the cap.
