# Version 8.4 "Gold and war": rulings 1540-1549 (wave 2, the retune)

Josh chose to retune the test city, not the game: the scripted players in the balance runs were written for 8.3's raids (about 7 in a 20-year reign) and now meet about 13. No number in src/ moved. Each change is a choice a player makes through the commands at that moment, reading what the game shows (the raid sheet's size, sides and mix; the sheets' stall and "no room" rows; the treasury). The drivers are tests/stage47/pressure/lateRun.js (`meetWarning`, `bandAims`, `towersNear`), tests/v60/a/greatWorkRun.js (`meetWarning`, `sellSurplus`, `foundTemple`, `rushTemple`, `standDown`) and tests/v60/balance/dangerRuns.js (`lateDangerRun`). The pins each settled are listed in 1547-1549; `git grep 8.4-BALANCE-OPEN` finds the ones still open.

1540. The late and great-work drivers meet each band of a warned raid where it strikes first: each band (warningBands) needs `towersNeeded` of its share of the raid sheet's strength (a war elephant counting as 4, ruling 1523) standing within `towerRange + 2` (6) tiles of its first target (firstRaidTarget, the building the advice's tower item aims at since ruling 787); the late run tries its own tower spots near that building first, then where Show me's search lands from it, and the great-work run where Show me's search lands (it stops when no spot is left within reach). They counted the towers standing anywhere against the whole raid, at spots tried east first; with 8.4's early raids all on foot from one side, the late run's year-10 raid from the north (20 raiders) met 3 of its 7 towers and was lost, and the great-work run's year-12 raid from the south found the 7 towers built for the north raid before it and met 3. Measured: the late run beats all 6 of its raids in years 8-15 (the sixth under way at the end), the great-work run all 13. — Cost if wrong: the drivers build more towers than before (the late run 7 where it built 6; the great-work run ends with 11), which a thrifty player might not.
1541. The dangers-on late run's player defends from the capital years on (`dangerPlayer({ defend: true })`: the late run's meetWarning, so 1540's rule). The capital script lays three towers at Market town and no more, and 8.4's clock brings raids of 16-19 on foot from the north in years 3-5; the year-5 raid met those three and was lost. Measured: none of its 10 raids lost (the tenth under way), the lowest 13,967, 18,942 at year 15. — Cost if wrong: the dangers-on capital years spend about 1,000 gold on towers the dangers-off capital run never builds (its own raids are all beaten without them).
1542. The great-work player sells what piles up (`sellSurplus`, each month from year 11): beer, linen and jewellery are set to Sell above `SURPLUS_KEEP` (200, what the homes draw between caravans) or the offering still owed of that good, whichever is more; a good the luxury habit buys is left alone. The grand run's beer Ignore (kept for the Manors), the linen and the jeweller's output filled the main storehouse beside the potters (500 beer and 270 jewellery by year 14), so its clay pits stood with "no room in the stores", the potters had no clay, and some 50 Townhouses fell to Houses for want of pottery: taxes fell from 65,000 a year to 33,000 and the temple's crews stood empty for years for want of workers. — Cost if wrong: the run earns some caravan gold it did not before; a player who never opens the clay pits' sheet would not.
1543. B7's band is not met by the dangers-off great-work run, from the other side: with 1540 and 1542 it is won on day 6,380 (year 18), 520 days before the band's 6,900, with 8,866 the lowest (9,194 from the Seat on) and every raid beaten, so the 8.3 pins (won by day 7,100; never under 2,000) hold again and are restored. The run is that sensitive to the player's keep (Keep 300: day 6,520; 400, 600 or 800: not won, a debt of 3,000-7,000), so no sensible keep is chosen to land in the band; selling beer alone wins on day 6,842 but falls to -7,537. — Cost if wrong: the reign is shown winnable 18 months early by a player who sells its surplus; Josh may restate the band or ask for a slower player.
1544. The great-work player clears the temple's plot before founding it: while no raid is warned or under way, the towers standing on `TEMPLE_PLOT` are demolished (the demolish tool). The grand years' advice put towers at 34,26 and 33,27 in the dangers-on seed 5, so neither the north road nor the west one would go; the road routed round them left the temple beyond every quarry's reach, and its site stood "no stone" from year 11 to year 20. — Cost if wrong: two towers lost until the next warning rebuilds them beside the temple.
1545. With the dangers-on habits the great-work player rushes the temple's course while the treasury stays above the spending player's line (tests/v84/balance/spending.js `SPEND`: 6 months of Normal-rate taxes plus 30 days of the rush) and cancels below it. Seed 5 held 25,000-40,000 from the founding on while its crews stood short. — Cost if wrong: none to the game; the habit is 8.4's Rush used as the design's spending player uses it.
1546. With the same habits the towers stand down between raids: while the monuments stand short of workers and no raid is warned or under way, every tower is paused (its sheet's Pause, so its workers go to the crews and it costs nothing), and all are manned again at the first month end after a warning (60-90 days before the raid) or once the temple is finished. The city ends with 11-13 towers (8.3: 7-8) staffed before the guilds (safety before monuments), and seed 5's guilds stood at 3-6 workers of 16 for years. — Cost if wrong: a raid warned less than a month before it arrives would meet unmanned towers; 8.4's warnings come 90 days ahead.
1547. The dangers-on seeds: seed 5 (with the habits) is won on day 6,927 with 7,732 the lowest and none of its 13 raids lost, so B7's "won by day 7,200, no raid lost" is met; the 6.0.1 pins (won by day 6,800, never under 20,000 from year 10) are not, and keep their 8.4-BALANCE-OPEN markers with the measured values. Seed 10 (the script as it was) beats all 11 raids and reaches Great Capital on day 3,660 (B7's fallback met) but never the Seat: its treasury is gone in year 12 (the lowest -36,172, was -46,638), before the great work's habits apply; with the habits it never reaches Great Capital (3 of 11 raids lost, -21,073). Its 8.3 pin (won by day 6,200, 4,000 from year 10) stays open. — Cost if wrong: one seed of the two is not shown winnable by the scripted player.
1548. B3 is not met by playing better: the late spending run beats every raid and meets every quota, but ends year 15 at 1.55 years of profit (16,160 gold; bound 1.5; it was 1.52 with one raid lost). The design's spending player never reaches its lines in years 7-15: the court's due takes about a third of the treasury each New Year, so the treasury peaks near 4.5 months of taxes, under Rush's 6 and Low's 12. The ratio is set by the due and the run's other outflows and swings year to year (years 13-15: 1.62, 1.42, 1.55); a raid beaten no longer pays the theft a lost one did. A yearly festival (200 gold) was tried and moved it to 1.61. The pin keeps its marker at 1.55. — Cost if wrong: B3 says nothing about the late game; Josh may restate it over several years or lower the spending player's lines.
