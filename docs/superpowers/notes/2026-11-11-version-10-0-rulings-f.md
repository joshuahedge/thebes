# Version 10.0, Track F: rulings 2110-2119 (the first view)

Where the plan and the real code disagreed, the code won; each ruling says both.

- **2110. Every camera write holds; the pans in `input.js` and `keyboard.js` pass the view too.** `cameraControl.js` calls `holdInMap(cam, view())` at the end of `jumpTo`, of an instant `begin` (a zero-ms move), after every tween step and its end, and the fling's `panBy` is given `view()`. `fitCamera` gains a `size` option (`frameCity` passes `cam.mapSize`) and returns the held centre, so its result satisfies `holdInMap`. The drag, the pinch's pan (`input.js`) and the keyboard pan (`keyboard.js`: the view is twice `cameraControl.centre()`, since the keyboard has no `view`) were Track 0's ruling 2100 loose ends, and are not in Track F's ownership row; the edit is one argument each. In `boot.js` `startGame` the camera (the UI's own framing, or a save's focus) is held after `createUI`. A save stores no camera (the camera opens on `world.focus`), so "a loaded save's camera" is its focus; the test sets a corner focus on a 64 map and the camera opens held. The 6.0 edge tests (`v60 d1 huge map: a pan stops at the map's edge`, the camera, input and keyboard suites) pass unchanged: they pan without a view, so the margin is still 0 there (ruling 2050 amends the rule only where a view is given). Cost if wrong: a caller that has no view still pans to the very edge, as before.
- **2111. `startCamera(world, view, zoom = 1)` runs after `createUI`, for a fresh city only.** The plan has `startCamera(world, view)`; the zoom is a third argument because on a phone `createUI` frames the opening at 0.54 (not 1: ruling 1852's frame for the start area and the road's end) and the start must keep it. `boot.js` applies it (`Object.assign(cam, startCamera(...))`) when the game is not from a save, a lesson, a decree or the stress city and has no buildings; the sample city and every other opening are only held. The diamond is the last tile of `world.startRoad`; the centre is held, then moved `m / 2` further in from the edge the entry stands on (the nearest of the four, from `world.tradeEntry`: always the +y edge today) and held again. The diamond ends on the entry side of the middle of the screen (the edge side) and always on screen. The plan's "off-map fill pixels of one colour" count cannot be made now that the ground is not one colour (2112), so the share is counted geometrically: a 48 x 48 grid of screen points, each through `screenToTile`, counted when it falls outside the map (`tests/v100/f/helpers.js` `offMapShare`). A map diamond narrower than twice the screen's long side cannot fill it whatever the camera does (a 48 map at 1920x1080 and zoom 1), so the unit test holds only bigger maps to 30%; the booted tests (the default 64 map at 390x844 and 1920x1080) hold it. By the shots, the off-map share of a fresh 64 map at 390x844 went from about 45% to a few percent.
- **2112. `TERRAIN.offMap` keeps `#C8AE7C`; the ground is the rim colour paling to a haze.** The plan calls `TERRAIN.offMap` "the sand paled 10%", which would be lighter than the sand; the code's `offMap` is the darker off-map sand (`#C8AE7C`, its alias `sandOff`) and `tests/render/terrainLook.test.js` pins it (darker than the sand, the edge band darker still). It stays; it is the colour at the rim, and the ground pales away from it to `OFF_MAP_HAZE` (the sand blended 40% with `#E9DCC3`, `#EAD8B3`) 12 tiles out, so "pale with distance" holds and no palette value moves. The test "differs from every terrain palette colour" skips the alias. `terrainLook`'s "fills past the map" test is amended: the far pixel (nearly four tiles out) is no longer within 3 of the flat colour but within 40 of it and lighter; the edge and inside assertions stand.
- **2113. How `offMap.js` is made, and why not as the plan wrote it.** `g.fillPattern` does not exist and `SALT.dunes` is not a thing (the code has per-feature salt tables); `drawOffMap(g, world, cam, view, nowMs, { season, dark })` is drawn on `g.ctx` and uses its own salt (`DUNE_SALT`). The plan's 12 full-canvas fills and an alpha pattern fill cost 16 to 30 ms in the software canvas of headless Edge (the flat fill was under 1 ms), so the ground is built to cost: the haze fills the canvas; each of the map's four sides gets one quad (rim to 12 tiles out, mitred at the corners, which are the bisectors) with one linear gradient across it (rim colour to haze); the dunes are three evenodd ring fills (four tiles wide each) of a pattern drawn UNSCALED (a scaled pattern fill is 6.4 ms a full screen, unscaled 1.0) with the strokes' alpha baked in (an alpha pattern fill is 2.7), the rings' patterns keeping 100%, 55% and 25% of the strokes, so the detail goes with the distance. The pattern tile is 256 CSS px (256 x the device ratio in pixels, rounded), 640 hash-placed 4 to 11 px runs, a crest in the sand darkened 6% (the plan's colour) and, under part of each, a lip darkened 18% (so they read as dunes); it anchors at the art origin (the transform is only the translation), so it moves with the map and does not scale with the zoom. The season wash is mixed into the haze and the rim colours (the land's own wash covers the land only; the plan's "drawn before the chunks so the wash multiplies over it" is not how `drawSeasonGround` works), the dunes carry none. `dark` is in the signature and not read: `drawLight`'s tint covers the whole canvas after the ground. Nothing moves (a test draws at two clock values and compares the hashes), so Still draws what Full does. Measured, software canvas, one frame mostly or wholly past the map: 1280x800 5.6 ms (flat fill about 0.3), 390x844 DPR 2 7.0, 1920x1080 11.6; a first view with the edge crossing it is a quarter of that; a GPU canvas is cheaper. The bound in the test is 16 ms for a frame wholly past the map, the worst case, which a held camera never shows (30% off the map at most).
- **2114. The rim shadow is outside the rim.** The plan puts a one-tile ramp "inside the map's rim"; the chunks are drawn over everything inside it, so it would never show. It is a one-tile gradient quad on the off-map side of each edge (`edgeFade` at alpha 0.18 to 0), over the existing `drawMapEdge` band, which stays in `renderer.js` (the renderer's fill and the `drawMapEdge` call keep their places; `renderFrame` calls `drawOffMap` where the fill was).
- **2115. The scribe is a participant in the guidance row, ahead of everything.** `guidanceKind()` (createUI) returns `'scribe'` while a DOCKED line is showing (`scribe.js` `docked()`: speaking and not pinned to a control), on a phone layout, not in a tool's mode and not under a lesson card (where css/v81-t.css hides the bubble anyway), BEFORE the sheet and alert checks, so it has the row over an alert row or a sheet; a pinned line floats and takes none. `data-guidance="scribe"` hides the legend, goal, tip and caption by the existing `ui.css` rules (`:not([data-guidance="goal"]) #goals` and the like), and a new rule in `css/v81-t.css` hides a docked bubble whenever `data-guidance` names anything else on a phone layout. `watchScribe(fn)` calls the arbiter the moment a line starts, ends, is hushed or the scribe unmounts, so the goal comes back at once when the bubble fades (its `SAY_MS`); the wide layout has no `data-guidance`, so nothing changes (a test asserts the bubble is still `#under-bar`'s first child and shown). The plan says "while an alert row shows, the bubble takes the guidance slot"; in the code a court letter's alert row does not make the guidance `'none'` (only a critical banner on a landscape phone at large text does), so today a court letter plus the goal is already two rows and the bubble was the third; the test reads exactly that (the alert and the goal, then the alert and the scribe, then the alert and the goal). `measureRows` (`src/dev/scenes.js`, Track 0's file) gains the layer `scribe: '.scribe-bubble:not(.pinned)'` so the budget sees the bubble; the test is in `tests/ui/mobileBudget.test.js` (375x667 at 100% and 150%).
- **2117. `tests/v50/layers/ui.test.js` waits for the scribe.** The Town milestone makes the scribe speak a docked line; on a phone layout that line now has the guidance row (2115) and the Town tip waits, off the screen, until it ends (so the tip's words are not in `#tip` at that instant). The test calls `hush()` before reading the tip's text. The behaviour (the tip comes after the line, within `SAY_MS`) is the ruling 176 turn-taking the spec asks for.
- **2118. The lesson's framing tolerance is 80 px, not 2.** `tests/v80/n/lesson.test.js` held each step's target within 2 px of the middle of the free map; the lesson city's targets lie near the map's edge, where `holdInMap` (every camera write, 2110) moves the camera about 50 px in (the bazaar 143,431 against 195,405 at 390x844). The map's edge on screen outranks exact centring (ruling 2050); the tolerance is `TOL` = 80 px, and the "the tower step moves the view" test keeps its meaning (the bazaar is still not where the focus sits).
- **2116. Shots.** `fresh-corner` takes a size from the page's own `?size=`, so before and after were taken on 48, 64, 80 and 112 at 390x844 DPR 2 and 1280x800, and at the default size at 1920x1080 (`tools/shots.ps1 -V100` takes the default size only, so `scratchpad/f-shots.ps1` loops `tests/screenshot.ps1` with the page query; names `v100-f-fresh-corner-<size48|size64|size80|size112|default>-<before|after>-<WxH>-t100.png`). Extra after shots: `v100-f-rim-after-<WxH>-t100.png` (the camera jumped to the corner and held, to show the rim and the ground at the 30% limit) and `v100-f-sample-390-after-390x844-t100.png` (court letter in the alert row, the scribe's line docked, the goal waiting). The `sample-390` before is the review's own picture (the 11 Nov review note); it was not retaken here.
- **2119. Fix round 1: the start frame, the off-map cost, the lesson bounds, the resize.** (a) `startCamera` no longer trusts a fixed `m / 2` nudge: on a tall phone view at the zoom createUI framed (0.54) the booted city left 35% off the map (the unit test only tried zoom 0.6 and 1). It now tries inward nudges of 0.5 m, 0.75 m, ... up to 2 m, keeps the first whose diamond stays 24 px inside the screen and whose sampled off-map share (24 x 24 grid) is under 27%, else the least; the booted tests pass at 390x844 and 1920x1080. The booted tests now run under try/finally so a thrown expect no longer skips cleanup (the 1920x1080 "null dispatchEvent" was only that cascade). (b) `drawOffMap` costs: it returns at once when the view's four corners are all inside the map (the map is convex), so a view inside the map makes no fill, gradient or pattern call (counting-context test); the 8 gradients and 3 patterns are made once per context (WeakMap, keyed by size, scale and the washed colours; a pan is only the translation); and the ground is baked to an offscreen canvas keyed on the art origin's screen place, scale, zoom, canvas, size, seed and wash, so a frame where none moved is one `drawImage`. Measured in the software canvas, fresh corner 390x844 at device ratio 2 (64 map, 0.54 zoom): 8.35 ms a frame before; 0.9 ms a still frame after; while panning each frame re-bakes, about 9 to 10 ms (the fills, unchanged; a GPU canvas is far cheaper). One side effect: a view wholly inside the map no longer gets the haze fill under it, so a chunk not yet baked shows the previous frame (or the page) for a frame instead of haze. `opts.bake === false` paints straight onto the context (the counting tests use it). (c) The lesson framing test is now two tests (390x844 and 375x667): each step's target centre must lie in the free map (x a `FRAME_MARGIN_PX` in from the sides, y between the lesson card's 30% and the dock's 34%) and within 60 px of its middle; measured worst offsets 58 px and 22 px. The 80 px `TOL` stays only for the tower-step comparison, which compares two steps. (d) `watchLayout` takes `onSize`, called on every resize or turn (not only a change of layout); `createUI` passes it a `holdInMap(cam, view())`, so the camera is re-held for the new view; a test turns a phone camera into a 1920x1080 window and back.
    **Round 2.** (e) No smear: `renderFrame`'s chunk pass fills the diamond of any visible chunk that has no sprite (not baked, no stale one) with `TERRAIN.offMap`, one path fill per missing chunk and nothing when all are baked, so the early-out no longer lets the last frame show through (test: a magenta frame, then a frame with `maxBakesPerFrame: 0`). (f) Panning is a blit: the bake is the canvas plus a margin each side (half a canvas, less where the bake would pass 4 MP: 0.37 at 390x844 DPR 2, so 1560 x 3376 = 5.3 MP is cut to 4 MP), keyed on size, scale, zoom, canvas, seed and wash (no longer on the origin); a frame draws it at the rounded offset of the art origin from where it was baked, and re-bakes (centred on the camera) only when the offset passes the margin or the key changes. Measured, 120 frames of a pan across the fresh corner (64 map, 390x844, DPR 2, 0.54 zoom, 0.03 tile a frame each axis, software canvas): painting every frame (the round-1 cost while panning) mean 9.72 ms, worst 19.4; blit mean 1.27 ms, worst 34.1 (the worst is the first bake, which allocates the canvas; the one re-bake is within it).
