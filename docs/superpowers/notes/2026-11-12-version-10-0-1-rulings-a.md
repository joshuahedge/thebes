# Version 10.0.1, Track A (the motion): rulings 2375-2389

Where the plan and the code disagreed, the code won; each ruling says both.

- **2375. The glide takes the whole day (`GLIDE_DAYS` 1, was 0.6).** The plan says "interpolate between the sim's last two positions over the step's real duration". The code already starts a glide on the day the sim moved the unit and carries it on from where it is drawn (`placed` in `src/render/units.js`), so the fix is the duration alone: a unit that steps every day is moving at every instant of the day, a step of any length (Track S's 1.5 tiles a day, whether it arrives as steps of one and two tiles or as fractions) is crossed at an even pace, and a unit stands only when the sim has not moved it today (looting, fighting, waiting). The cost: a walker is now up to a day behind the sim at the start of its step (it was at most 0.4 of a day ahead of standing before), so a fight pairs up when the drawn figures meet; the sim's own spots still mark the clash and the fall (ruling 1613). Pinned tests moved: `tests/v90/v/effects.test.js` (V2) and `tests/stage3/art/unitsArt.test.js` (the glide's sample times and `GLIDE_DAYS`).
- **2376. Where each frame is chosen: in the walker entry's `kind`.** The plan says "walk cycle while the herd moves, a graze pose while it rests". Animals have no `.w0/.m` walk slots (those belong to `walkSlots` for the citizens' kinds), so each animal pose is its own figure slot named in the entry's `kind` (`gazelle_w2_l`, `heron_u`, `hippo_e`, `croc_t1`), made by `poses()` in `src/render/art/river.js` and baked once on first sight like every slot. The renderer is untouched.
- **2377. Herds walk and graze.** `herdAnimals` adds `pose` to each animal: under Full, while the herd is on the move, `_w0`-`_w3`, stepped by the ground covered (a four-frame cycle is `WALK_CYCLE` tiles: gazelle 0.5, ostrich 0.7; so the feet keep pace with the ground), each animal a hash of a cycle out of step; every animal of a walking herd faces the way the herd heads (the old random one-in-five turned back would moonwalk); at rest the animals swap slowly between standing and grazing (`GRAZE`: 0.13 cycles a day, grazing 60% of it). Gentle: the herd still glides, standing, and grazes at rest; Still: standing. Two existing tests pinned `/^gazelle(_l)?$/`: `tests/stage46/river/riverRender.test.js` now allows `_g`.
- **2378. Take-off and landing.** `heronFly` and `ibisFly` (`src/render/art/river.js`) draw four frames each: `o` wings open on the ground, `u` and `d` the two flap frames (feet trailing, neck drawn in, no foot shadow), `f` the wings folding in. River life's heron (`riverLife.js`) uses them as before-and-after of the boat: rising (lift 4 then 9) it shows `_o` then `_u`/`_d` swapping every `HERON_FLAP_MS` (150 ms); landing (the boat drawing off) it shows the flap, `_o`, then `_f` for the last half tile. The plan names only the river-life herons; the bank birds of `wildlife.js` also hop (`hopAt`: every 11-19 days, 2.4 days long, up 11 art px, wings open, two flap frames, down, open, fold), Full only, pure in the game day.
- **2379. Hippo and crocodile.** A surfaced hippo (the bank ones in `wildlife.js` and river life's) bobs 0.6 art px (the walker's `lift`, negative and positive) and, every few days (wildlife) or every `HIPPO_FLICK_MS` (river life), lays its ears back and puffs spray at the nostrils (`hippo_e`). The bank crocodile's tail sways through four poses (`croc_t1`, `croc`, `croc_t2`, `croc`).
- **2380. River life's crocodile.** The Shemu crocodile is `figures.js`'s `crocodile` (Track 0's, with walk slots); its sway frames (`crocodile_t1`, `crocodile_t2`) and a three-stage slide (`crocodile_slide`, `crocodile_slide2`, `crocodile_slide3`: snout in the water, forebody sinking, only the back and the eye above) are drawn by `crocLife` in `river.js` instead, so `FIGURE_KINDS` (and its pinned art test) are not touched; their left-facing copies carry `_l` in the name (the old two are mirrored by `dir`). Basking sways under Full only; Gentle keeps one slide frame. `tests/v100/l/riverLife.test.js` allows the new kinds.
- **2381. Flying birds flap.** `ambient.js`'s rare bird draws four wing frames (`FLAP_FRAMES`, 110 ms each, on the real clock) instead of one stroke. The bird is a few strokes on the ground layer for the one bird on screen, so it is not baked into the sprite cache (the plan's "no per-frame path building" is for the animals' sprites, which are all baked); its cost is the same few calls it was.
- **2382. A.3's numbers.** All 50 new slots bake once on first sight (the test draws every kind twice and proves the second frame adds no bake); the animals are inside the existing `ambientCap` 40, so the draw count is unchanged; the LIFE and V budget tests pass.
- **2383. The gazelle is redrawn on longer legs.** In the first after-shots the herd read as little tables (a wide pale belly on four short vertical legs). The body now rides 1.6 art px higher, the legs are slim, darker and bend at the hock, and the belly patch is smaller (all poses; the pre-10.0.1 standing gazelle changes with it). `tests/stage46/river/riverRender.test.js` pinned the mirrored copy to the same inked pixel count; the bent legs sit on sub-pixel positions, so it now allows a pixel or two (513 against 514).
- **2384. Review fix: the flap frames no longer overwrite the feeding ibis.** `poses('ibis', ibisFly, ...)` made `fig.ibis_d` (a flap's wings-down frame) and, spread later, replaced the feeding ibis, so every right-facing feeding ibis under Full showed a flying figure. The flap keys are now `fu`/`fd` (`heron_fu`, `ibis_fd_l`, ...; `hopAt`'s modes and riverLife's poses follow), the registry is built from `RIVER_ART_PARTS` (one part a pose group) so a test proves no slot is defined twice, and a test proves `fig.ibis_d` is the head-down ibis.
- **2385. Review fix: test gaps.** The river-life heron's `_f` fold and Gentle's pose-free herons, hippo and crocodile are tested. The Sonnet review also found `FROM_CITY =milestoneIndex` (a lost space): restored.
- **2386. Review fix: the gazelle's torso.** A deep chest tapering to a slim waist and rump, the belly a thin pale line (the pale slab is gone), thick thighs over slim shanks.
- **2387. Known, left as it is (predates this track):** with the whole-day glide a raider leaving at a gate is judged by `fellInField` from its lagged drawn spot (`t.last`), so a fall near the gate can be called a fall in the field (or the reverse) a step late; the 9.0 glide already lagged by 0.4 of a day. For the final review.
