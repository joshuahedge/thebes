# Version 8.4 "Gold and war": rulings 1500-1514 (Track E, war elephants and ivory)

The data is src/data/armies.js (`ENEMY_TYPES.elephant`, `ELEPHANTS`, `MATCHUPS`' elephant cells, `TOWER_VS`, `SHUNS`, `MIX_KINDS`); the mix, the upriver entry and the counters src/sim/armies.js (`drawMix`, `elephantRaid`, `elephantsIn`, `raidStrength`, `upriverEntry`, `counterModel`); the raid src/sim/raids.js (`nextRaidEntry`, `raiseWarning`, `arrive`, `tributeFor`, `tributeQuote`); the fight src/sim/combat.js; the panic, the stampede, the ivory and the report src/sim/elephants.js; the words `V84E_TEXT` in src/ui/text.js; the drawing src/render/art/armies.js, src/render/units.js, src/render/effects.js; the scenes src/dev/v84/elephants.js. Tests: tests/v84/e/.

1500. A war elephant is a fifth row of the enemy table: 60 hit points, 1 tile a day, 6 a day to a wall, its 3 damage to every unit beside it. From raid index 7 (the 8th raid) a raid is an elephant raid when hash2(seed, index, RAID_SALT.elephant) < 0.5, and brings min(3, floor(size / 8)) of them; the first raid never. — Cost if wrong: too rare or too strong; data retunes.
1501. Matchups as the plan's data: archers open 2, nearWall 2, melee 0.5; soldiers melee 0.5, gate 1.5; a tower's shot x `TOWER_VS.elephant` (1.5); `SHUNS.chariot` keeps your chariots from going out to one (the nearest other raider, or they wait). Factors multiply as for every kind, so an archer beside one deals 1x and a soldier at a gate 0.75x, and the raid sheet says the products ("2x at range (1x beside one)", "half in melee (0.75x holding a gate or breach)") so the words are what combat does. — Cost if wrong: a defence that cannot answer elephants; the lines say what helps.
1502. Panic is drawn once, on the first day an elephant ends below half its hit points (`panicChecked`), and only stampedes when the last blow it took that day was a tower's or an archer's and hash2(seed, its id, RAID_SALT.panic) < 0.5. A stampede deals 3 to every other raider of its band still standing (those it fells are counted `trampled`), and the elephant leaves the raiders without joining `gone`: it counts as fallen for the retreat, drops no ivory and never sets `left`, so the raid's last raider stampeding beats it. A soldier's blow below half, or another elephant's stampede, uses the draw up. — Cost if wrong: elephants beat themselves too often or never.
1503. An elephant counts as 4 raiders for towers needed and tribute (`raidStrength` = size + 3 x elephants), in `raiseWarning`'s tribute and `tributeQuote`'s towers; size stays the count of raiders. The lesson's and the score's calls stay on size. A raid without elephants prices exactly as 8.3. — Cost if wrong: tribute jumps on elephant raids.
1504. `nextRaidEntry(world, index)` gives an elephant raid's index (by the index alone) the raider entry nearest a south-edge water tile, else nearest any edge water, else the 8.3 draw (the Great oasis). Every elephant of a two-band raid comes with band 0 (the upriver one); the other kinds split as before, band 0 taking what band 1 has no room for, which is 8.3's split exactly when there are none. — Cost if wrong: on an odd map "upriver" looks arbitrary.
1505. A felled elephant leaves 10 ivory in the nearest (straight line from where it fell, ties to the lower id) working store whose `stores` include ivory and which has room: as much as fits; the rest is lost, and the report and the label say so. An elephant felled by another's stampede leaves its ivory too. — Cost if wrong: ivory wasted in a city without storehouses.
1506. A mix keeps 8.3's four keys and carries `elephant` only when the raid brings any (`MIX_KINDS`, `footMix`), so every raid without elephants, its warning, report and save read as in 8.3. The plan's "foot keep MIX.footMin" is read as "the wall-breakers keep it": elephants break walls and count with the foot towards footMin, with at least one raider on foot left. Read literally, late raids (whose archers and chariots already fill the room footMin leaves) would bring no elephants at all, and B8 could not be met; this way a raid of 8 or more always brings its 1 to 3. — Cost if wrong: elephant raids have fewer raiders on foot.
1507. An elephant raid's index from the 8th raid on comes in from upriver even when it is too small (under 8 raiders) to bring one, because the entry is decided before the size is fixed, so the scouts stand where it will really come. — Cost if wrong: a rare small raid enters upriver with no elephant.
1508. The warning (`warning.elephants`) and its `raid_warning` event carry the count; `tributeQuote` adds `elephants` only when there are any. "War elephants!" leads the alert's title, the phone raid sheet's arrival line (which carries the title) and the wide sheet's mix line (the phone's countdown chip keeps "Raid from the east · 2 m 10 d": "War elephants from..." cut off its countdown at 375 px and 150% text, and the sheet it opens leads with "War elephants!"); the `trumpet` plays in place of the horn: one line in src/ui/danger.js, a file no track owns. — Cost if wrong: one line to move.
1509. The elephant is drawn as `fig.raider_elephant` (grey bulk, column legs, fanned ear, trunk, pale tusk, a red saddle cloth and a crested rider with a spear), its health bar over the rider. The sim's `stampede` event (`{ id, x, y, to }`) draws it running back towards its band's entry under Full, fading in place with a still arrow under Gentle, holding with the arrow under Still, with a "Stampede!" label at every level; src/render/units.js is told (`noteStampede`) so no fallen figure is left. The `ivory` event labels its store "+10 ivory" (a sparkle too) or "10 ivory lost" where it fell. — Cost if wrong: render only.
1510. The first raid with war elephants (warned or under way) shows a picture card of its own, `elephants`, after the city event's: its picture made by tools/tutorial.ps1 from `wallScene`. As 7.0's event card did, this touches src/data/tutorial.js, src/dev/tutorialScenes.js and assets/tutorial/, which the plan's ownership table does not list. — Cost if wrong: a fifteenth card to drop.
1511. The `war_elephants` tip fires at the first warning with elephants, in place of the mixed raid's tip for that warning (one tip at a time); its words and its How to play line read the data. — Cost if wrong: the mixed raid's tip waits for a later mixed warning.
1512. A raider's panic flag is named `panicChecked` (the plan's name; the design's save list calls it `panicked`): true once the draw is made, whatever it said. A raid under way may also carry `stampeded`, `trampled`, `felled`, `ivory` and `ivoryLost`; the report carries `elephants`, `felled`, `stampeded`, `trampled`, `ivory` and `ivoryLost`, each absent when 0. Old saves need nothing: every reader takes a missing field as 0. — Cost if wrong: a field renamed in one place.
1513. `tally.elephantsMet` counts the elephants that arrive, `elephantsFelled` those that fall, `elephantsStampeded` the stampedes and `ivoryTaken` the ivory stored; nothing counts a stampede as a lost raid. — Cost if wrong: 9.0's "the Unconquered" reads them.
