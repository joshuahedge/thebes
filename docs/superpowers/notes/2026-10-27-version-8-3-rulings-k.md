# Version 8.3 "Lines": rulings 1410-1428 (Track K, keep stocked and supply lines)

The rule is src/sim/carts.js (setSource, findSource, orderSource, sourceChoices) and src/sim/layers.js (supplyLines); the sheet src/ui/s47/carts.js, the pick src/ui/s47/sourcePick.js with src/ui/modes.js, the layer src/render/layers.js and src/ui/layersLegend.js, the Advice fixes src/sim/adviceFixes.js and src/sim/bazaarFood.js, the words Track K's section at the end of src/ui/text.js. Tests: tests/v83/k/.

1410. An order may name its source (order.from). setSource(world, store, good, fromId | null) sets it or, with null, puts the order back on Nearest (today's rule). Besides the plan's four refusals (not_storage, same_store, not_holder, unlinked) it refuses 'no_order': a source belongs to an order, so a good the store does not keep stocked has none. — Cost if wrong: one more refusal word; the sheet only offers "from" on an order's row, so a player never meets it.
1411. A chosen source is the only source: findSource returns nothing while it cannot spare one (burning, no road or ferry to it now, or nothing above its own order), and the order is 'dry' with that reason (empty, kept, unlinked, burning). Its cart waits; it never draws on the next nearest (ruling 1384). The row says dry ahead of "the cart serves another good first" (it is the one thing the player must answer), but not when the store already holds its amount. — Cost if wrong: a store starves until the player looks; the amber row and Nearest instead? are the way out.
1412. A store with none to spare is a choice all the same: setSource accepts it, the pick shows it dimmed with the reason in words, and the row then goes amber. — Cost if wrong: a tap on a dimmed store sets a dry line; one tap on Nearest instead? undoes it.
1413. The pick is a mode of its own ('picking', with pick { home, good }): the sheet steps aside and one bar stands at the map's foot (in the side column on a wide or landscape screen), with a 44 px button for every store that can hold the good (nearest by road first, the unlinked last), Nearest and Cancel; the dock and the corner buttons step aside while it is up. The buttons are the keyboard's path (Tab, Enter or Space); Esc and Back leave it for the store's sheet, focus back on "from". — Cost if wrong: on a phone the list scrolls after three stores; the map taps still pick any of them.
1414. A chosen store that goes (demolished, burnt to rubble) reads as Nearest at once; the next day's work clears from, marks the order gone until the month ends (the row says "The chosen store is gone: Nearest again") and a sourceGone event says it once in Recent. — Cost if wrong: a demolish taken back with Undo after the next day's work finds the order on Nearest.
1415. moved counts what an order brought home this month; at the month's first day's work (cartDay) it becomes lastMonth, and both fields are left off when 0, so an order nothing moved for looks like an 8.2.1 order. The roll is in cartDay, not runMonth (clock.js is not Track K's), so lastMonth turns over one day's tick after the month ends. — Cost if wrong: none a player can see; one call to move if a later release wants it exact.
1416. Supply lines is the layer kind 'lines', from Town, after Workers in the picker, in a deep navy (#23407A) so the stuck amber stands apart. supplyLines(world) keeps nothing between calls; the render keeps its list for one frame signature (the day, the roads, the buildings, a pause or resume, every order, the stocks of the goods ordered, every cart). — Cost if wrong: a change no signature part covers (an accept switch flipped mid-day) shows on the next day.
1417. A line is LINES.thin (2 art px) plus the square root of last month's amount over 20, to LINES.thick (5.5) at most, on a white halo, and a store's several lines run side by side 4 art px apart; its dashes move toward the store it feeds at 10 art px a second (standing under Still), with a chevron at the store's end so the direction never rests on motion; a stuck line (dry, or no source) is amber with long dashes that stand still, and an order with no source at all rings its store in dashed amber. — Cost if wrong: thicknesses to retune in src/data/layers.js LINES.
1418. With Supply lines on, a tap within 0.75 of a tile of a line's road route (a ferry's hop over the water excluded) opens that line's store, ahead of the building nearest the tap; a tap on a building's own footprint is always that building's. — Cost if wrong: a tap on a road tile right beside a building, where a line runs, opens the line's store instead of the building.
1419. Build another copies an order's chosen source with it; the bar says "pottery 100 from the same store". A copied source that has gone reads as Nearest, as any gone source does. — Cost if wrong: a copy far from its original draws on a far store until the player changes it.
1420. Keeps stocked stands open right under Stored. Each order is one row (the good's icon and name, Keep stocked [On], up to [− n +], from [store ▸]) with its live line; while the store keeps nothing stocked every good it may hold is listed, and once it keeps one the others fold under "Keep another good stocked (n)", remembered per store. A storehouse's switches follow under Takes in. Switching an order off forgets its chosen source. — Cost if wrong: one more tap to start a second good.
1421. A Keep stocked Show me (City advice, the bazaar's food alert, the bazaar goal) opens the store's sheet at that good's row: unfolded, scrolled to and its switch focused (aimKeepStocked). — Cost if wrong: none; the row is near the top anyway.
1422. The keep_stocked tip is asked for after every command while the city has two stores or more (a granary counts); the tips module shows it once a device, when a tip may show. — Cost if wrong: a city loaded with two stores sees it at its first command, not at once.
1423. A bazaar's granary_low alert carries spare: the nearest store outside the bazaar's own stores that can spare a food its granary could be set to keep stocked now (it takes the food in, can send its cart and has no order for it). The alert's fix, its Show me and the bazaar goal then point at Keep stocked, ahead of 8.2.1's farm. — Cost if wrong: a city with a far granary is told to cart grain before it is told to farm more.
1424. A site stalled for stone or bricks: the nearest storehouse within MONUMENT.supplyReach of it that could take the order (as 1423), drawing on the nearest store outside that reach with some to spare; with no storehouse in reach and a store somewhere with some to spare, a storehouse to build at the spot nearest the site that the placing rule takes, beside a road within the reach. A storehouse in reach that cannot take the order (refusing the material, short of workers, or already keeping it stocked) gives no item. — Cost if wrong: a refusing storehouse hides the fix until its Takes in switch is on; the site sheet's own line (Track C) still names the stall.
1425. The two fixes are City advice items of kind 'keep_stocked' (after 'stranded' in the tie order), each weighing 6 points (src/data/s47.js KEEP_ADVICE, a Manor's tier, as the Quay and the Large plot weigh). — Cost if wrong: it ranks above or below a neighbour it should not; two numbers to retune.

1451. The pick frames the map with the top of the free map as the page measures it (layout.js freeTop: the top bar and every row under it that shows, the court notice and goal row at large text on a phone) and the bar's own height below, not a fixed 104 px; the top is held at least two frame margins above the bar so a tall notice cannot take the whole band. — Cost if wrong: on a very short, very large-text phone the free band is a strip, and the store framed there is as near the bar as the margin allows.

While a pick is up the category rail steps aside with the dock and the corner buttons, and on a phone held sideways the bar takes the rail's place at the screen's edge. — Cost if wrong: the Roads, Homes and other category buttons are out of reach until the pick ends (Cancel, Back or Esc), which is one tap.
