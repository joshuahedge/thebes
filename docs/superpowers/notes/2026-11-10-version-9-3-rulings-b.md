# Version 9.3, Track B: rulings 1900-1909 (services, workshops and landmarks)

- **1900. One recolouring pen, not 20 redraws.** Every non-landmark service and workshop runs today's drawing through `styled()` (services.js): a pen that swaps whitewash and thatch for the look's materials (Village mud, City sandstone roofs, Capital limestone), then adds a painted band (City, Capital), a pot, a lamp or a small palm by the door, a lit doorway (working, short) or a plank door and barred shutter (shut). The silhouette is today's. Cost if wrong: Josh may want each drawing redrawn by hand at the wave 0 bar; the pen is the part to replace, one building at a time.
- **1901. Three buildings are the wave 0 references, lifted.** The bazaar, granary and potter's kiln are drawn from `looksheetArt.js`, with the fill stages (granary 0-3, bazaar 0-2) kept in every look; the City and Capital shapes are described in 1903. Cost if wrong: the stage cues (sacks, heaps) are re-drawn to the reference's style, not the old one.
- **1902. Old keys stay.** `granary.s2`, `storehouse.s1`, `potter`, `quarry.idle` and the rest still exist as today's art (cards, assets and the look sheet use them); `slotFor` names the new `<base>.l<look>.<state>`. `<type>.idle` is now an alias of the Town look shut. Cost if wrong: some two hundred more slot keys exist (drawn lazily).
- **1903. The eight landmarks change by additive shape.** Granary: City domes on drums and a loading porch, Capital taller drums, porch and gilded finials. Bazaar: a covered market (City), a colonnade (Capital). Temple: obelisks (City), plus a pillared forecourt (Capital). Kiln: a chimney stack (City), plus a gilded cap (Capital). Brickworks: a stack (City), plus a gilded kiln (Capital). Weaver: a loft (City), a taller loft, a porch and a gilded cap (Capital). Well: a well house (City), a basin and gilded cap (Capital). Firehouse: a watchtower (City), a taller one under a gilded cap (Capital). Cost if wrong: the extras can be dropped one at a time; the bounds table takes a height per look.
- **1904. Short and shut hide the yard.** A workshop short of workers shows no goods in its yard; a shut one also shows no figure and no smoke, and its flag hangs lowered. A storehouse is a store, so only a shut one is bare. Cost if wrong: a short storehouse would look fuller than a short brewery; it shows its stock so the fill stages still read.
- **1905. The registry expands the tables.** The art files cannot call `withLooks` (registry.js imports them first, before `EXPANDED` exists), so each exports a `*_LOOKS` table and registry.js expands them at its end. Cost if wrong: none for play; H, who has the same problem, should do the same and the lines will merge.
- **1906. `serviceState` reads `b.blocked === 'idle'`.** One clause in wave 0's function: a quarry or brickworks no monument site needs employs nobody, so it is shut, as the old `.idle` slot said. Cost if wrong: the clause is the one thing to revert.
- **1907. The quay and the ferry landing take the states too,** with the wharf's stone unchanged (only the shed's walls follow the look). The firehouse's, tower's and barracks' mud faces take the look's wall.
- **1908. Games' smoke is not drawn by the art.** The kilns' smoke in the sprite is only the drawn puffs; the animated smoke is `ambient.js`, which already reads `isWorking` and `b.blocked`. A shut building has no drawn puffs. Cost if wrong: a short building (some staff, `isWorking`) keeps smoking in ambient, as today.
- **1909. No ground shadows in the sprites.** The reference drawings called `groundShadow`; the renderer draws each slot's `shadows` blocks itself, so the lifted drawings leave it out (it doubled the shadow and went outside the bounds).
