# Version 10.0, Track Q: rulings 2140-2179 (the quality pass)

Where the plan and the code disagreed, the code won; each ruling says both.

- **2140. One button language (Task Q.1).** The plan names `#dock .demolish`; no such button exists (Demolish is a tray card, `data-item="demolish"`, in the Roads category, plus a `button.demolish.danger` on a building's sheet and the confirm bar's tick). So: the tray card gets a `demolish` class (`cards.js`), outlined with a coral edge and coral words, filled coral only while it is pressed (the tool is live); the building sheet's Demolish is outlined coral (never the strongest button of its row, C3); the confirm bar's tick stays the one filled red button of that bar (it is `.ok.danger`, not `.primary`, so the bar has no primary). Layers' Off was drawn as chosen because its `aria-pressed` followed `!overlay`; it is now always `"false"` (the way out, not a choice) and outlined; `tests/ui/layersPicker.test.js` said `true` after Off and is amended. The audit found one primary on each of the title (every variant: a save, no save, a lesson due), the lesson card and the dock (the Build pill); the Layers sheet has none of its own, so the test holds "none", not "exactly one". CSS is at the end of `css/ui.css` under `Version 10.0, Track Q`. Cost if wrong: a stylesheet block.
- **2141. Drawn icons (Q.2).** `src/render/art/icons.js`: `CATEGORY_ICON_DRAWS` (roads a tile with its centre line, homes a cube with a door, food a grain ear, industry a pick, services an ankh, defence a shield, monuments a pyramid), `categoryIconUrl(id, size, color)` cached by id, size, colour and pixel ratio (1 to 3; the test page gives one string twice). `CATEGORY_ICONS` is `{ roads: 'roads', ... }` ids; `components.js` has `categoryIcon(id)` (an `img.icon.cat-icon`, 20 px, ink) and `setCategoryIconTone(node, chosen, onFill)` which re-points the src: teal for the chosen dock and rail button (as the glyph's teal was), white on the wide panel's filled teal tab. The rail's icon buttons use them too (it read the same glyphs). The wide panel's tabs gain a 14 px icon with 5 px padding so four tabs still make one row at 100% (`ui panels` holds). The art is stroke-only line drawings on a 20 unit grid, drawn with the 2D context directly rather than through `makeDraw`, because `makeDraw` carries outlines and fills the line icons do not want.
- **2142. Foot shadows (Q.3).** The raider chariot, elephant and landed boat raider call `footShadow` (the elephant and the raider had a plain ellipse; the chariot nothing); the ibis and heron too; `waterShadow(g, x, y, rx, ry)` is in `primitives.js` (a `TERRAIN.waterDeep` ellipse at alpha 0.25) and the hippo and the fishing boat stand in it; the wharf has a `contactShadow` under its net shed and a `waterShadow` under its pier. "Raider boat" is `fig.raider_boat`, a landed boat raider on foot (not a hull), so it takes `footShadow`, not `waterShadow`. The plan's "charred stump" is `render/fire.js` `STUMP` (the ground pass under rubble), not an art slot: it gains a cast shadow polygon and a dark base line, batched (two calls however many tiles), because `tests/v92/v/look.test.js` pins its ellipse count at none. The file contract is extended in `tests/v93/n/art.test.js` and each function is checked by its own source in `tests/v100/q/footShadows.test.js` (the file test alone passes whatever its other drawings do).
- **2143. Plots are pegged out in the ground (Q.5).** `terrainBaker.js` `drawPlotMarks` (after the roads, since the yards lie over anything drawn earlier): a stake at each corner with its own shadow and a slack cord between them, baked into the chunk; `render/unbuilt.js` keeps the sign on its post and the far zoom's sand square and pegs out only a monument's site live (a plot's live white-haloed string was what read as interface). `tests/v70/p/unbuilt.test.js` asserted a plot's live corner stakes; its corner check is removed there and the baked ones are tested in `tests/v100/q/groundMarks.test.js`. A Large lot is not pegged (it has its own marked-lot art).
- **2144. Wheel tracks on the paved streets (Q.5).** Two darker lines (`WEAR`: 0.1 tile either side of the axis, 1.1 art px, alpha 0.2) on every paved street, baked in `terrainRoads.js` (City and Capital where built up; a kerbed City street with no buildings already had ruts). The kerb shadow and a darker cart centre were 9.2's (`KERB`, `CART_TRACK`) and already on every surface, so the plan's "cart tone on busy tiles" is not added a second time.
- **2145. Burnt ground and troubled streets (Q.5).** The rubble heap draws three charred timbers (the two new ones with a lit edge) and an ash drift to leeward; the burned shell (what a burned building with `was` leaves, the usual burnt block) gains one more timber and the same ash. Markers: the badge is drawn at `MARKER.drawScale` 0.8 (20% smaller), the stem 1 px of ink on a 2.6 px halo (was 2 on 4); the layout radius `MARKER.r` stays 11 (spacing, merging and the tap area, which 9.x tests pin: a first try at `r` 8.8 broke three `s4 art pinned markers` tests, among them the "at least 10 px" badge rule of P13, so only the drawing shrank). The stem-pixel test now asks for 200 alpha, not 255. The far zoom's one badge per rolled-up cause is 9.2's rule and holds (the test puts five potters under one cause at zoom 0.6 and finds one badge with a count of five).
- **2146. Legibility (Q.6).** The plan's test (every text pair over the parchment composited with the deepest night and each strongest wash) cannot be met and does not describe the game: the page's panels are opaque and every word is drawn after the light pass and the wash (ruling 864; `tests/v70/s/night.test.js` holds that the page does not change at night). Laid over the whole picture, the deepest tint leaves ink on parchment at 4.44, ink-soft on parchment 3.52, teal on parchment 3.18, gold on ink 2.88, coral on ink 1.91, and shemu's wash leaves coral-text on parchment 4.18 and amber-text 4.15. Raising the night's floor until those held would lift the whole night and the washes' caps to near nothing, for words nothing ever tints. So `light.js` and `seasons.js` are not changed, and the test measures what the tint and the wash do reach, the map: a marker's white ring and a label's pill hold 3:1 (the night's own floor, `NIGHT.minRing`) against every ground colour at the deepest night under each season's strongest wash (all pass), the road and the water differ by about 36 in luminance under the deepest night (the floor is 25) and by at least 25 under each wash on top, and every text pair of the interface still holds 4.5:1 as it stands. Cost if wrong: the plan's stricter test is a different, unsatisfiable one.

The sheet heads' 40-character names already wrap (9.2, D12): a test holds it at 150% on 390x844 against `.cartouche-ruler`.
