# Version 10.0.2 "A clearer phone": rulings 2440-2459 (2440 to 2458 used; 2452 to 2455 are the review fixes)

Josh played 10.0.1 on his phone: the steady bar (tray or placing card, then a full-width row only for Play, Speed, Map and Layers, then the tab row) covered about 37% of a 390x844 screen, and while placing the tab row's space was an empty band. He picked a side rail from mock-ups. Portrait phone only; `WORLD_VERSION` stays 12; nothing in the sim or the renderer changed.

## Rulings

**2440 (the rail).** On a portrait phone (`body[data-layout="portrait"]`) `#corners` is a column on the right edge of the map: Play, Speed, Map, Layers from the top, each a round 44 x 44 px button (`--phone-rail-btn`), 8 px apart, 8 px from the edge (`--phone-rail-edge`). The markup is unchanged (the left and right groups become `display: contents`; `.speed-corner` stays a column so the Speed popup can hang off it). The block is at the end of `css/v93.css`, the last stylesheet, so index.html's stylesheet list (which older tests pin) is the same. A left-handed setting puts the rail on the left. — Cost if wrong: four buttons out of place.

**2441 (one place, and what each button shows).** The rail's foot is `--phone-rail-b = safe-bottom + --dock-tray + 8px`, the same in every state: idle, any tab's cards, a card picked (folded or More), a drag, a sheet open or shut, a layer on (tests/v1002/rail.test.js: four rects within 1 px at 390x844 and 375x667, t100 and t150). Nothing in it uses `--ui-scale`, so it is as slim at 150% as at 100%. Play: the drawn play or pause picture only (the word is not drawn), with the "Paused" tag kept small (12 px, no scaling) above the button, right-aligned so it stays on screen. Speed: the chevron button, which shows the speed ("1×", "2×", "3×"; paused it shows the speed Play will resume at) in a new `.speed-now` span. Map: its count badge as before. Layers: tinted (`--teal-soft`, teal edge) while `aria-pressed` is true. The words under Map and Layers and Speed stay in the DOM, visually hidden (the accessible names are the aria-labels as before). Titles, aria labels, `aria-keyshortcuts`, the double tap on Map, the click and long-press paths, and the Speed popup's 1×/2×/3× are untouched; the popup opens to the left of the Speed button and the Layers picker is `position: fixed` beside the rail, its foot level with the rail's, inside the screen. — Cost if wrong: a button that looks different.

**2442 (the rail never covers the rows or a toast).** `updateRailClear` (createUI.js): with the class taken off, if a row under the top bar reaches the rail's top edge and its right edge passes the rail's left, `body.rail-clear` makes `#under-bar` end 8 px from the rail. It runs on every store and settings change and from a `ResizeObserver` on `#under-bar`; measured with the class off, so it never flaps. A toast takes the same gutter on its right while a tray is open or a tool is on. A toast now stands 8 px above the dock (its own height, tray included: createUI publishes `--dock-h` from the dock's offsetHeight, on store and settings changes and a ResizeObserver), or 8 px above the placing panel (`--confirm-h`). The U.5 `--toast-lift` stays for compact, where the corner row is still under the toast. — Cost if wrong: a row or toast under a button. Known cost: at 375x667 and 150% text the rail reaches the top rows (it stands above a 350 px tray), the Ground legend ends at its edge and wraps one line more (rows end at 41.8% of the height, not 40%).

**2443 (the ghost clear of the rail and above the panel).** `railInsets()` is the rail's reach into the map from its edge, plus 8 px, as a right (or, left-handed, left) inset. `placingInsets` uses it, and the bottom is the panel's measured top edge (`min(bar, h - top - room)`): the 10.0.1 floor of 30% of the screen up to 240 px and the dock-strip estimate are gone (the panel is short now, so the floor would have held the ghost too high). Before the panel is up the guess is `min(0.3 h, 240)`. `leavePlan` (where a building goes once a touch tick has ended the tool): the bottom is the dock box (tray included) or the panel, with the toast above it, and the rail's inset; U.5's steps 2 and 3 (moving the toast into the free map under the corner row, and framing the building there) are dead and removed. The `toast-low` machinery is left in place but inert on a phone (the plan never asks for it) because compact and the older tests still name it. The farm review cases (tests/v70/p/review.test.js) now hold for every size, including 375x667 at 150% with the synthetic 110 px and the real 67 px goal row, whose fallback 10.0.1 needed; More open too (tests/v1002/panel.test.js). — Cost if wrong: a ghost behind the panel or the rail.

**2444 (the folded panel).** On a portrait phone `confirmBar.js` adds `phone` (and `more-open`) to the bar. Folded it shows: the picture (48 x 24, hidden at 125% text and more, where the name needs the room) and the name (18 px times the text scale up to 1.15) beside Undo, Cancel and Confirm; the cost line under them; then one row holding the conditions pills, Show me when it is offered, and the More toggle. Everything else (`.balance`, `.reason`, `.why`, `.effect`, `.nicer`, the monument and copy lines) waits for More. A road, a plot or a demolish has no pills, so its `.reason`, effect and monument lines stay, in a block that keeps room for the cost and two lines (so the panel does not bob as the words change). The drag, style, spacing, route and crossing rows are controls and always show. Folded height at 390x844, t100: 137 px (it was 233 px, with the dock's 72 px left as an empty band under it). — Cost if wrong: a fact that was always there needs a tap.

**2445 (the pills row).** One row, scrolling sideways when the pills are wider than the room (the scrollbar is hidden); the pills that say no come first, then later, then met, so a row that has to scroll never hides the reason a spot is refused. Opened with More they wrap beside the Less toggle, in 10.0.1's own order. Show me joins the row (it was a full-width 44 px row) so a refused terrain spot no longer makes the panel taller (tests/ui/placementFit.test.js "one height"). — Cost if wrong: a pill off screen until a swipe.

**2446 (coverage).** See the tables above. tests/v1002/coverage.test.js holds, at 390x844 and 100% text: idle 12% or less, the shortest tray 25% or less and the tallest under 31%, folded under 20%, More under 31%. The cards-open target is missed for the Roads tray, as said above.

**2447 (More, remembered).** `store.placingMore` (new, `false`; ui state, never saved, kept for the session; no setting fitted). The toggle is a button (`.more-toggle`, text "More" or "Less", `aria-expanded`, an aria-label naming the building: "More about Well"); it is shown for every building tool and for any other tool that has a line to open. The bar's held height (`keepTallest`) is keyed by it, so Less folds the panel again. More is the whole 10.0.1 panel, balance and effect included also on a short phone at large text, where 10.0's C14 had folded them (overridden under `.more-open`). Toggling More while a building is being placed frames the ghost again above the panel's new top (`stopMoreWatch`, createUI.js). Strings: `V1002_TEXT` in text.js under the Version 10.0.2 header. — Cost if wrong: More forgotten between cards.

**2448 (the panel takes the tab row's place).** `#confirm` is at the foot (`bottom: 0`) and the dock is `display: none` while a tool is on (U.5's `visibility: hidden` rule for portrait is gone, so the general rule applies): no empty band. A More-open panel is capped at `--dock-tray` (232 px times the text scale) and scrolls inside it, which is also why the rail's foot at `--dock-tray + 8` is above every panel. — Cost if wrong: a very long More scrolls instead of growing.

**2449 (removed, and what came back).** Removed as dead: `body.stack-tight` (the four CSS rules and the class), `updateStackTight` and its calls in `pick`, `showMeAt` and the settings watch, `--corner-h` and `--stack-b`, the fixed tray and `.tray-more` rules, the dock strip offsets in `placingInsets` (`strip`, `estimate + strip`, the `cornersHeight` term), the `trayCover` and `tagLift` terms and U.5's steps 2 and 3 in `leavePlan` (the `cornersBottom` import with them), and the portrait `visibility: hidden` dock rule. Kept: the `toast-low` machinery (2443), `cornersHeight` and the `over-half` hiding (compact and wide still use them), the compact and wide corner rules and `--toast-lift`. Back: the tray is in the dock again, between the handle and the tab row (`order`), as it was before U.5, so the dock is one box again and grows with an open tray. — Cost if wrong: a rule that something else still needed; the full suite is the check.
