# Version 8.3 "Lines": rulings 1390-1409 (Track X, crossings)

The road tool lays a bridge or a ferry, and the Ferry card is the Crossing card. The rule is src/sim/bridges.js (crossingRuns) and src/sim/commands.js (previewRoadTiles, drawRoadTiles); the landings src/sim/ferryLay.js (layLandings); the undo src/sim/undo.js; the card src/sim/ferryFind.js (crossingChoices, previewCrossing); the tool src/ui/placement.js, the bar src/ui/confirmBar.js, the words src/ui/v71/ferryText.js (CROSSING_TEXT). Tests: tests/v83/x/.

1390. A road stroke judges each run of water as a whole: 1 to 4 tiles straight with land at both ends is a bridge, as before; 5 to FERRY.max (12) is a ferry, whose two bank tiles become its landings, with no road on them or on the water; wider is 'too_wide'. Its price adds 2 x costOf(FERRY_LANDING) per ferry (ferryCost). A map with no river keeps bridges only (ruling 1333). — Cost if wrong: none for the numbers (ruling 1380); a player who meant a long bridge gets a ferry, which the bar names before the tick.
1391. layLandings(world, landings, water) is the one way two landings go down (factored out of layCrossing), so a road-laid ferry is 8.2.1's pair exactly: ferry.mate, water, run false, counted as built. It staffs, runs and carries walkers and carts the same, and raiders never use it. — Cost if wrong: none; the tests compare the two pair for pair.
1392. A stroke that lays a ferry is one undo step: the 'road' entry carries ids and landingCost. Undo takes the road and the landings away, refunds the road's share whole and the landings' share for those still standing, as a 'place' entry does. An entry without ids (before 8.3) undoes as it always did. — Cost if wrong: Undo removes a ferry the player meant to keep with its road (ruling 1382).
1393. A ferry's bank tile must be free land: a building, a road already there, a wall or hills refuse the stroke with 'landing_taken' (block says which, at the tile), and the bar says it ("this one has a road on it"). A strip of land one tile wide between two runs of the stroke, or a bank another crossing of the stroke took, is 'taken'. A ferry's banks must be in line with its water; a bridge may still be come at from the side. — Cost if wrong: a road that already runs to the water's edge cannot be dragged on across as a ferry; the refusal's Show me opens the Crossing card, whose crossings lay their own short road.
1394. A landing needs a road beside it once the stroke is laid (one there already, or one this stroke lays), else 'landing_no_road' ("carry the road on past the bank"). The road tool lays no stubs of its own; the Crossing card still does (rulings 1342, 1343). A touch plan skips that ferry's tiles with the reason. — Cost if wrong: a drag that stops exactly on the far bank is refused until it goes one tile further.
1395. The words while dragging: the bar leads with each crossing laid ("Bridge: 3 tiles of water", "Ferry: 7 tiles of water, walkers wait at the bank", then the road tiles and the ferrymen's bill); too wide names both limits ("Too wide: 14 tiles (a bridge crosses up to 4, a ferry up to 12)"); a bend is "A crossing must go straight across". A stroke that ends in the water, on in a line from the bank, is on its way across: the bar names what the water ahead takes ("Ferry: 7 tiles of water · drag on to the far bank"), the water is tinted to match and the tick waits (idle); water ahead too wide, or with no far bank, is refused at once. The idle road bar says both limits ("Water: bridge up to 4 tiles (25 a tile), ferry 5 to 12"). The tints: teal planks for a bridge (css --teal, darker than the river), blue waves for a ferry, coral for a refusal; a refused stroke is coral throughout, as before. — Cost if wrong: a touch plan that ends a tile into the water no longer lays its dry part until the finger comes back to the bank.
1396. The Ferry card is the Crossing card: its item id and tool kind stay 'ferry', so saves, keys and the tray order are unchanged; buildingName('ferry') is 'Crossing' (Track X's section of src/ui/text.js); its line is "Cross water: bridges up to 4 tiles, ferries 5 to 12"; its price reads "160 gold a ferry" (a bridge's price is per tile and depends on the water). — Cost if wrong: words only.
1397. crossingChoices(world, from) ranks bridges and ferries as one list by 8.2.1's rules 1344-1345, so, narrowest water first, every bridge comes before every ferry; a standing bridge counts as laid (ruling 1352), so no crossing is offered beside one. ferryChoices is unchanged (ferries only) for 8.2.1's callers. — Cost if wrong: on a river with narrow spots near the roads the card shows bridges only; a drag across wider water still lays a ferry.
1398. A bridge plan's banks may hold road already (the bridge carries it on); a bare bank gets road, with 8.2.1's stubs where no road is beside it. The plan is { kind: 'bridge', ends, water, stubs, roads, tiles }, its tiles the whole line from the near side's stub to the far side's, laid by drawRoadTiles (buildCrossing dispatches on kind), one 'road' undo entry. Its price is its dry tiles at the road price and its water at the bridge price, which a test checks equals previewRoadTiles' for its tiles. — Cost if wrong: the card's bridge lays up to 3 road tiles the player did not draw; Undo takes them back.
1399. The Crossing card's drag (previewCrossing) lays a bridge over water of 4 tiles or fewer, snapping to the nearest bridge within FERRY_FIND.snap as 8.2.1 snaps ferries; 'ferry_bridge' no longer arises there. The 8.2.1 previewFerry is unchanged. — Cost if wrong: a drag over a narrow stream with the card in hand lays a bridge where the player meant to look for a ferry.
1400. The card's buttons name each crossing's kind with its water and price ("Bridge 3 · 60", "Ferry 7 · 172"; screen readers "Bridge, 3 tiles of water, 60 gold, with 2 road tiles"), so the kinds never differ by colour alone. A shown bridge is a dashed outline with its price on a label and no boat; picked, its water is drawn as bridge squares (teal planks) and its banks and stubs as the road it lays. — Cost if wrong: a phone at 150% text wraps three buttons a little sooner than 8.2.1's shorter names.
1401. The ferries tip is the crossings tip: its id stays 'ferries' (so it shows once per device, as before) and its trigger is 'roadCrossing', which the Map seam fires the first time the road's line meets any water on a map with a river, ahead of the bridges tip. Its words: "Roads cross water: a bridge up to 4 tiles, a ferry 5 to 12. Drag straight across, bank to bank." How to play's entry is Crossings. — Cost if wrong: a player who saw 7.1's ferries tip does not see the new words; What's new and How to play say them.
1402. Show me from a road refused for water too wide, or for a ferry's landing (a busy bank, no road to it), opens the Crossing card ranked from where the road met the water; a bent line keeps the road tool and shows the nearest bridge's place, as since Stage 4.6; on a map with no river, always the bridge's place. The button is named for where it goes ("Show me where a crossing fits"). — Cost if wrong: a bent line over ferry-wide water shows a bridge's place further off rather than the card.
1403. Route mode is unchanged: it still crosses water by bridges only (runReason) and says "lay a ferry" when the water is wider; it never lays a ferry. — Cost if wrong: a player in Route must switch to Straight, or use the card, to cross wide water.
1404. Old saves load as they were: no new world fields; a road undo entry without ids is undone as before; a road-laid ferry saves as two ordinary landings. — Cost if wrong: none found.
1405. Outside Track X's files, kept minimal: src/render/overlays.js (the bridge ghost's teal, and a label without a boat for a bridge the card shows), src/ui/s46/map.js (the crossings tip's trigger, one line), src/dev/s46/map.js (its 'refused' scene bends the line in the water, since a line stopping in it is no longer a refusal). — Cost if wrong: a merge touches three more files than the ownership table lists.
