# Version 9.1 "First hours": rulings 1735-1739 (the fix wave after the final review)

1735. The city the lesson's hand-over adopts is not one the player started: its records id is kept in the setting `lessonCityId`, and `firstCityLayout` leaves that row out when it counts, so a lesson player's first own city (and one after "I've played before") opens New city on the classic river, and the next on Surprise me, as ruling 1660 says. — Cost if wrong: a device that adopted the lesson's city and never started another keeps one extra setting; nothing else reads it.
1736. A lesson step that sets a speed of its own (the raid's) says it on its card before Play, as the docked row does: "The game runs at triple speed until the raiders arrive, then double." — Cost if wrong: the raid card is one sentence longer.
1737. Goals the city already meets at the hand-over complete quietly (`settleGoals` without events, in `adoptLesson`): no toast, no message, nothing counted; their gold is paid as on any completion. — Cost if wrong: the hand-over's first goal pays its gold without a cheer.
1738. The error card closes any open picture cards (`hooks.closeCards`, without their onDone, which may start a city) so it is the one panel, and says only what is true of the save: "Your city was last saved this month." and Reload "Goes back to the last save", since a save also happens when the tab is hidden. The dead strings `limitHead` and `where.storm` are removed. — Cost if wrong: a player who had picture cards open loses their place in them when an error shows.
1739. Back to gold lists buildings of one kind as one row with a count ("2 Builders' guilds: 152 a month"); Pause stops every building in the row and takes the whole cost off the month. — Cost if wrong: a player cannot pause one of two like buildings from this sheet (the building's own sheet still can).
