# Version 6.0, Track D2: the guided first city and the picture cards

Written 23 Oct 2026 on branch `v60-d2` (Track D2, rulings 760-779). Cut from `version-6-0` at b664755 (waves 1 and 2 merged).

## What it is

A mandatory first city on a device with no saved city and no finished lesson, replayable from the title and the menu. Fourteen steps on a fixed map (seed 7, 48 tiles, Normal, classic river): road, homes, well, families, farm, granary, bazaar, workers, month, Large plot, firehouse, fire, tower, raid. Each step lights what it asks for and moves on only when the sim shows it done (`src/sim/lesson.js stepDone`); the last ends on "Your first city stands" and back on the title with New city.

- `src/data/lesson.js`: the map's places and the steps (declarative: a check, a place to light, a card for Show me, what the step stages).
- `src/sim/lesson.js`: the lesson's city, `stepDone`, `lessonAt` (passes every step the sim already shows done, stages what the next one stages), `STAGES`, and `LESSON_SCRIPT` (the commands a player gives for each step: the tests' walk-through and the resume's replay).
- `src/ui/v60/lesson.js`: the row, the lit target (scrim with a hole, a ring, a ring on a control, the guide's "Suggested" tag and dimmed cards), the end sheet. `src/ui/lesson.js`: the title's rule and the facts the words are made from. Words: `src/ui/text.js` section `v60 d2` (`D2_TEXT`).
- `src/boot.js`: `hooks.lesson`, `startLesson`, `leaveLesson`, the lesson's game hooks and settings view; `src/ui/titleCard.js`: the lesson-only card; `src/ui/menu.js`: Lesson and Leave the lesson rows.
- Picture cards: `src/data/tutorial.js` (13 cards, 7 `intro`, 6 with a `first` moment), `src/ui/cardMoments.js`, `src/dev/tutorialScenes.js` (six new pictures through the v60 dev scenes), `tools/tutorial.ps1`, `assets/tutorial/*.webp` and `cards.json`.

## It never touches a saved city

The lesson's game gets `lessonRun` hooks (`save` answers true and writes nothing; no export, import, install or New city) and `hooks.save` itself returns true for a lesson (`current.lesson`), so the month's autosave, the page hiding, an update's save and an export are all no-ops. The lesson's game sees every tip and picture card as seen (a view of the settings; the device's own `seenTips` is not written), so the first real city meets its tips fresh. `tests/v60/d2/walkthrough.test.js` plays the whole lesson over a saved city and checks the slot byte for byte and that no write was attempted.

## Staged, and said so

A city this young has none of three things the lesson teaches, so each step that needs one sets it up in the lesson's own throwaway city and says so in its words: the Large plot card (a real city opens it with a Townhouse), a small blaze in the bazaar (lit only once a staffed firehouse covers it), and the Market town's tower card with the raid clock (a real city meets raiders at 250 people). A staged step cannot trap: a blaze that burns anyway, or raiders that come before a tower stands, still end their step.

## Rulings (760-779)

760. The lesson is fourteen steps on one fixed 48-tile Normal map, each moved on only by what `stepDone` reads from the sim; a step done once is never asked again, even if the city later stops showing it (a tower takes workers a bazaar had). Cost if wrong: a player who undoes a step is not sent back for it.
761. Three steps stage what a young city lacks (the Large plot card, a small blaze, the Market town's tower with the raid clock), each said in its words, and none can trap the lesson. Cost if wrong: a player thinks raiders come at 60 people; the words say 250.
762. The lesson's city is never saved, by two guards (the game's own hooks and `hooks.save`) and a settings view that leaves the device's tips alone. Cost if wrong: a new hook that writes the world must also check `current.lesson`.
763. On a device with no saved city and no finished lesson the title is Start the lesson (Continue the lesson with Start again, part-way), Settings and What's new, and Install and Bring your city where offered; no New city, difficulty, size or How to play. Cost if wrong: a player who knows the game must bring their city in or sit 10 to 15 minutes.
764. A lesson left part-way keeps its step in the settings and resumes by replaying the scripted walk-through to it (the player's own streets are not kept, ruling 626); a finished lesson replays from the first step and changes no setting. Cost if wrong: the resumed city is the script's, not the player's.
765. The lesson ends on a sheet, "Your first city stands" (lessonDone and cardsSeen are set then), and New city takes the player back to the title with New city primary and a note, rather than starting a city: the difficulty and size are theirs to choose. Cost if wrong: one more tap before the first real city.
766. The lit target is a ring on the map with the rest dimmed round it, a ring on a control, and the guide's own tag and dimming on the cards; the goals strip, tips and captions stand aside in the lesson; a phone's row has no x (the menu leaves the lesson). Cost if wrong: a phone player looks in the menu to leave.
767. The seven picture cards of before are the intro cards; six more (Large plots, the court's demand, plague, a low Nile, locusts, raiders) each open alone, once a device, the first time the loop reports their moment, never while a tool, a sheet or a question is open or under Help: Off; How to play shows all thirteen, and a set of more than eight has no row of dots. Cost if wrong: a card can open while the player plays (the game is paused behind it, one Got it).
768. The `workers` step asks for more homes: six homes cannot staff a bazaar after the farm and granary, and the step's words give the sim's jobs and workers. Cost if wrong: none (a data row).
