# Version 8.4 "Gold and war": rulings 1460-1464 (wave 0, the shared pieces) and 1469 (the partner rows)

The goods are src/data/goods.js (`LUXURY_GOODS`, `ASKABLE_GOODS`); the partners src/data/trade.js (`DRIFT_LAST`) and src/sim/trade.js `drawDrift`; the save src/data/v84.js, src/sim/world.js, src/save/serialize.js and src/save/migrate.js `v10ToV11`; the seams src/sim/raids.js `nextRaidEntry` and `endRaid`, src/sim/spoils.js, src/sim/elephants.js, src/render/scouts.js, src/audio/sfx.js and the three headers at the end of src/ui/text.js. Tests: tests/v84/w0/.

1460. World version 11. The ten new tallies (`V84_TALLY`) start at 0 in a new and a migrated city. `world.luxuryFrom` is required and a whole day: 0 for a new city, the load day + `LUXURY_GRACE_DAYS` (360) for a v10 city, so no Villa or Estate falls on load. Every other 8.4 field is optional, absent meaning 8.3's behaviour, and a scheduled `raid.nextWarnDay` keeps its day. A v10 city loaded and played a year matches the same city played without the load. — Cost if wrong: one more migration to keep.
1461. The luxuries are cedar and ivory, new raw goods appended after incense (so every 8.3 list keeps its order and storehouses keep them with no change), and incense, which keeps its place and gains the flag. All three carry `luxury: true` and `perResidentPerMonth: 0.01`; there is no lapis. Court values: cedar 45, ivory 50. The court's requests, its guided letter and the city's demands draw from `ASKABLE_GOODS`, which leaves out cedar and ivory (bought, never made); incense stays askable as in 7.0, so no 8.3 city's letters or demands change. — Cost if wrong: a fourth luxury is one data row; a court that asks for ivory is one list.
1462. The yearly drift draws every good but `DRIFT_LAST` (cedar, ivory) first, in 8.3's order, then those, so every 8.3 price draws as it did (held for years 1 to 30 on three seeds). — Cost if wrong: none.
1463. The raid clock's numbers (300; 360 to 600; the 60-day lean) land in wave 2's balance check; Track R builds the rules with today's numbers, and its tests set 8.4's numbers on a world themselves. — Cost if wrong: a careless test could leak the numbers.
1464. The seams, each a stub with 8.3 behaviour: `nextRaidEntry(world, index)` factored out of `raiseWarning`; `endRaid` spreads `raidReportExtras(a)` (Track E) into the report and calls `raidSpoils(world, report, won, events)` (Track R) after the next warning is drawn and before the `raid_end` event; `unitsAt` appends `scoutWalkers(world, timeDays, range)` (Track R); `SFX.trumpet` and `SFX.fanfare` with no notes; `RAID_SALT` elephant 8109, panic 8110, scouts 8111; the text.js headers for G, R and E in merge order. Cart loads for cedar and ivory are placeholders Track G may redraw. — Cost if wrong: none.
1469. Byblos sells cedar (45, 240 a year) and buys ivory (40, 80 a year); Nubia sells ivory (60, 80 a year). Byblos opens at Great city, so cedar needs no `from`. Incense is unchanged. No caravan buys or sells a new good unless the player orders it or the city holds it, so no 8.3 run's trade moves. — Cost if wrong: prices retune in data.
