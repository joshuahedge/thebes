# Version 9.3.1, Track L rulings (2015 to 2019)

- **2015 (S1, lesson row at larger text on a phone):** the row wraps (face and words on the first line, More and Show me under), the words are never line-clamped, and the chapter label is read by screen readers only until the row is open. Cost if wrong: the row is up to about 20 px taller than before at 150%; it scrolls inside its own height budget (--guidance-max) if the words were ever longer.
- **2016 (S10, goals strip at larger text):** the strip's words take four lines, and More gets a line of its own under them (padding-bottom only while More shows). Cost if wrong: a goal longer than four lines at 150% still shows More and opens in place.
- **2017 (S4 and S9):** the bar's building name uses `overflow-wrap: break-word` and the title row wraps, so a name that does not fit beside the picture goes under it (cost: the bar is one line taller with a long name). While a raid is warned or on, at 125% text and above, Defence leads the dock's row by `order: -1` (cost: the category order changes for the raid's length; the row's scroll snap is off then so the reorder does not carry the scroll).
- **2018 (M2 and S15):** `#rail` is `--rail-w` wide (the notch is its right padding, counted once), and each rail category keeps 8 px under its word for the colour band and no longer shrinks onto it (cost: at 150% sideways the rail scrolls by a little more to reach Monuments).
- **2019 (S14):** the viewport meta gets `maximum-scale=2`, keeping the 9.2 deliberate pinch (ruling 1829) but capping it at 200%; the chrome's `touch-action: pan-x pan-y pinch-zoom` is unchanged. Cost if wrong: iOS Safari ignores the cap (as it does by design), so an iPhone can still zoom past 200%; the review's one-spread-to-5x case is closed on Chrome. Two older tests that banned `maximum-scale` outright (tests/v81/d/noZoom.test.js, tests/v92/a/audit.test.js) now accept a cap of 2 or more.
