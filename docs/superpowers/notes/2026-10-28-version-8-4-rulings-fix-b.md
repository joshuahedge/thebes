# Version 8.4 "Gold and war": fix wave, Agent B (interface and drawing), rulings 1538-1539

The scouts' rule is `scoutsAt` in src/sim/scouts.js (with `comesTwoSided` and `secondBand` in src/sim/raids.js); they are drawn by src/render/scouts.js; the words are `V84R_TEXT` and `V84_G_TEXT` in src/ui/text.js. Tests: tests/v84/fix/ui.test.js.

1538. A raid that will come as two bands has scouts at both entries for the SCOUTS.days before its warning, and the caption and alert name both sides ("Scouts on the eastern and southern horizon"). `scoutsAt` asks `comesTwoSided` (the same test `raiseWarning` makes, at the raid's stage) and `secondBand` for the second entry, so the scouts stand exactly where the bands will come; each band has its own two or three scouts (ids 10 up for the second band), and `raid_scouts` carries `dirs` only for a two-sided raid. A raid that goes by boat (one band on foot, the boat landing as its second) still shows scouts at its first entry only: the landing is chosen at the warning. — Cost if wrong: on the day a two-sided raid's stage or its chance changes between the scouts and the warning (the chance is higher from the late years), the warning may come from one side the scouts did not show, or the scouts show a second side the warning does not use; the sim's draws are untouched, so nothing else moves.
1539. The small faults of the final review, settled in words and layout, no rule changed.
