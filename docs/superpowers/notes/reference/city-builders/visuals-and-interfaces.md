# City builders: visuals and interfaces, as reference for Thebes
**Scope:** palette, readability, map life and interface chrome in 18 city builders (6 historical, 4 modern PC, 8 cozy or phone-native), read for what a code-drawn 2D isometric Pharaoh-like can borrow, adapt or avoid.
**Date:** 11 Oct 2026.
**Method:** 2-9 screenshots per game, at least one with the interface showing, from Steam, Steam Community, GOG, the App Store, Google Play and the Nintendo eShop; detail was cropped at native resolution. Nothing was saved and all text is paraphrased.
**Caveats:** colours are eyeballed from JPEGs, so hex values and coverage percentages are approximate. Store shots are curated, and all phone shots found were landscape, so portrait layouts are inferred. Older games were seen at 1024 px or below.

## Part 1: game by game

### Pharaoh: A New Era (2023). The target look

1. **Palette and light.** Pale warm sand (about #E9D3A0) with a fine hatch grain that never competes with the buildings. Whitewash walls are warm cream on the lit face and lavender-grey on the shade face (about #A99DB8), with soft violet ground shadows. The river is deep teal-blue, with a turquoise shallows band about a quarter of a tile wide along the shore and short white ripple dashes. The floodplain is chocolate-brown mud feathered into a saturated, clumpy meadow green. Fields are golden. Mosaic plazas carry most of the saturation (red diamonds on white, orange stars on blue and white, teal borders). The ground stays calm, so the buildings stand out.
2. **Readability.** The housing tiers read at full zoom-out:
   - Low tier: sand-coloured huts with red-and-white striped awnings and pots.
   - Middle tier: pergola-topped houses on green plots.
   - Top tier: stacked whitewash blocks with green rooftop trellises and a painted stripe under the parapet.

   Temples are tall pylons with painted lotus bands and pink and blue streamers on poles. The granary shows red grain in round roof bins. Roads are pale cream paving with a lighter edge.
3. **Life and stock.** One field showed two crop stages at once (tall gold at the back, green sprouts at the front) with a farmer walking through it. There is a felucca with a white sail on the river, market stalls piled with coloured pots, storage yards with crates and jars, and walkers and banners everywhere.
4. **Interface.** Dark espresso-brown chrome (about #3A2418) with a thin gold rim, cream icons and serif-capital titles:
   - Top left: a speed-control pill.
   - Left edge: a rail of about 40 px round buttons. The alerts button carries a red count badge.
   - Top centre: a trapezoid tab with the city, money, date and population.
   - A vertical strip of square build icons, an ornate minimap at bottom left and a right-hand rail of overlay buttons.
   - Placement shows a ghost building, green arrow markers and a red diamond on the blocked tile.

   The advisor modal is maroon with gold trim and covers about 65% of the screen. Without it, the chrome covers about 12-15%.
5. **Verdict.** This is the target palette: copy the calm ground, the shore band, the two-stage fields and the roof clutter. Adapt the brown-and-gold chrome to a phone top bar and dock, and avoid big modals.

### Caesar III (1998)

1. **Palette and light.** Terracotta roofs, cream walls, yellow-green grass and a violet-blue sea. The open ground is a speckled grey-beige scrub.
2. **Readability.** Red roofs make housing pop, and the Colosseum, temples and reservoirs (blue rings) have unmistakable shapes. The speckled ground lowers contrast outside the city.
3. **Life and stock.** Walkers, cavalry, aqueduct arches and a mosaic forum.
4. **Interface.** A stone right-hand panel (about 13% of the width): minimap, a grid of small build buttons, then a large decorative relief. The stone top bar has dark pill readouts for money, population and date, and info opens in a beige marble dialog. Coverage is about 20%.
5. **Verdict.** Borrow the pill readouts. Avoid noisy ground and decorative filler in panels.

### Zeus + Poseidon (2000)

1. **Palette and light.** The most saturated classic: white marble with a strong blue-violet shade, red roofs (teal on Atlantis), mustard-olive grass, crisp grey outcrops and a deep blue sea.
2. **Readability.** Sanctuaries are stepped platforms with giant bronze statues, readable from far off. Housing rows are white under red roofs.
3. **Life and stock.** Market stalls with goods, walkers in bright clothes.
4. **Interface.** A glossy turquoise right-hand panel: category tabs, a minimap, a one-line message area and a large god relief. The top bar is navy. Panel text is small, blue on cyan, and hard to read.
5. **Verdict.** It proves a cool violet shade suits white buildings. Avoid bright glossy chrome and low-contrast text.

### Emperor: Rise of the Middle Kingdom (2002, GOG)

1. **Palette and light.** Olive greens, dark slate roof tiles, cream walls, tan roads with stone kerbs, red and violet accents. Darker than Pharaoh.
2. **Readability.** Grey roofs give housing rows a strong rhythm, and special buildings break it with coloured roofs and height.
3. **Life and stock.** Ox carts, red-clad soldiers, a storage yard with goods on straw mats.
4. **Interface.** A dark wood top bar with a violet patterned border and gold serif text. The wood-grain right-hand panel has an icon column, and each category page has a gold title and its own overlay buttons ("See Desirability"). Coverage is about 20%.
5. **Verdict.** Borrow the idea that each build category's sheet offers the overlay that matters for it.

### Children of the Nile (2004, 3D)

1. **Palette and light.** Ochre earth, a turquoise Nile, a purple dusk sky, mud-brick houses with purple and yellow friezes, dark palms. The distance turns muddy olive.
2. **Readability.** Wealth shows as size and whiteness, but at city scale it becomes a field of similar pale boxes.
3. **Life and stock.** Close-up people at work, boats, a half-built pyramid on the horizon.
4. **Interface.** Black-and-gold Egyptian chrome: a gold-ringed round minimap, round black-and-gold buttons, and a wide black census panel listing professions and counts. Coverage is about 20%.
5. **Verdict.** It says "Egypt" but feels heavy. Borrow the gold rim, and avoid the census wall.

### Nebuchadnezzar (2021)

1. **Palette and light.** Desaturated tan sand, golden striped wheat, green irrigated plots, magenta and yellow market cloth. The water is olive-brown and reads as swamp.
2. **Readability.** Clean footprints. The granary shows grain domes, and the categories are colour-coded.
3. **Life and stock.** Caravans, boats, full storehouses, and walker popups with a portrait and a one-line joke.
4. **Interface.**
   - Dark charcoal glass, a full-width top strip of about 16 goods counters, and a round month dial.
   - **Build dock:** stepped ziggurat-shaped category tabs, each tinted differently. Above them sits a card tray; each card shows the building's own render, the name above, the cost at top right with a coin icon, and requirement pips at top left.
   - **Placement:** a translucent green footprint plus a dashed yellow outline of the reach area.
   - Coverage is about 22% with the tray open.
5. **Verdict.** The closest match to the Thebes dock and tray: copy the card anatomy and tinted tabs. Avoid the olive water and a 16-item top strip.

### Banished (2014)

1. **Palette and light.** Realistic and muted: thatch and orange roofs, dark pines, olive grass. Winter is white and blue-grey.
2. **Readability.** Houses look much alike; roof material and yard contents do the work.
3. **Life and stock.** **Chimney smoke rises from every house**, the strongest cheap "alive" signal in the whole set. Stockpiles, cows in pastures, and fields with distinct crops and bare tilled soil.
4. **Interface.** Free-floating dark windows. Construction windows show material counts ("Log 30 of 30") and a yellow progress bar, and the event log mixes red warnings with white news. In a player screenshot, windows covered about 90% of the map.
5. **Verdict.** Borrow the smoke and the construction progress bar. Avoid floating windows.

### Timberborn (2021-)

1. **Palette and light.** Voxel terraces, saturated grass, grey cliffs, navy water, warm wood, amber night glow.
2. **Readability.** Chunky silhouettes. **Red round status icons float over buildings with problems.**
3. **Life and stock.** Log piles in storage, beavers, water wheels.
4. **Interface.**
   - Slate-green panels with a bronze border, and dense resource tables at top left.
   - **A grouped alert list at bottom left**: a red icon, a label and a count (for example "lacks power (17)").
   - A bottom toolbar tinted by category, and a needs panel with green and red bars.
   - About 25-30% coverage in a busy shot.
5. **Verdict.** Borrow the problem icons in the world plus grouped counts, behind a badged corner button on phone.

### Anno 1800 (2019)

1. **Palette and light.** Cinematic realism, with warm window light at night.
2. **Readability.** **The tiers read by material**: workers live in red brick, engineers and investors in cream stone with slate and copper-green roofs, and the buildings get taller as the tiers rise.
3. **Life and stock.** Ships, carts, crowds, airships.
4. **Interface.** A navy top resource strip with a city-name plate. At left, a questbook of parchment cards with portraits. A square minimap at bottom left, and a bottom build bar with tier tabs. Menus are full-screen parchment newspaper and expedition screens with advisor portraits and green check marks.
5. **Verdict.** Show tiers through material and roof dressing. Present goals as portrait quest cards.

### Anno 117: Pax Romana (2025)

1. **Palette and light.** Terracotta, cream stone and warm cobbles, with crowds in teal and green and cool plazas and fountains as focal points.
2. **Readability.** Landmarks contrast through cool water against warm stone.
3. **Life and stock.** Dense crowds on every square.
4. **Interface.** A slim slate-navy HUD with gold line icons, covering about 10%:
   - A round minimap ringed with small buttons.
   - The bottom bar puts tier portraits on either side of the goods that tier needs.
   - **The construction stock (wood, bricks, tools) is always visible.**
   - Five tools: demolish, move, upgrade, copy, paint.
5. **Verdict.** Borrow the always-visible build stock and a small fixed tool set.

### Townscaper (2021)

1. **Palette and light.** Teal-grey sea and fog, candy walls, orange roofs, white trim. **White mode shows warm pink-orange lit faces against lavender-blue shade**, which is almost exactly the Thebes whitewash look. Night mode adds warm window dots.
2. **Readability.** Detail comes from adjacency: enclosed courtyards turn into gardens, and arches and stairs appear where blocks meet.
3. **Life and stock.** Seagulls.
4. **Interface.** A thin colour swatch strip on the left edge and a gear icon, under 2% of the screen. The grid shows as a dark teal mesh while placing.
5. **Verdict.** Borrow the white-mode lighting and adjacency auto-detail (for example, touching houses share a stair or rug).

### Islanders (2019)

1. **Palette and light.** Flat low-poly with a **whole-scene colour grade per island** (turquoise, autumn orange, snowy blue).
2. **Readability.** Tiny buildings in strong flat colours.
3. **Life and stock.** Minimal.
4. **Interface.** About 5% coverage. A translucent round score dial with an orange progress ring sits at bottom left. A pill of **flat building silhouettes in each building's own colours** carries blue count badges and a bright blue "+" ring for the next pack. A next-island thumbnail sits at bottom right.
5. **Verdict.** Borrow the progress ring, the count badges and the self-coloured silhouette icons.

### Mini Motorways (2021; launched on Apple Arcade)

1. **Palette and light.** One flat background tone per map, two-tone building slabs, and **long translucent cool-grey diagonal shadows**.
2. **Readability.** Colour matching carries everything. Roads are pale with a darker outline.
3. **Life and stock.** Tiny coloured cars.
4. **Interface.** Under 3%: corner glyphs, a week-dial clock and a small inventory row. **Demand shows as pins on a building, and a filling red timer ring warns before failure.**
5. **Phone.** The board stays clear.
6. **Verdict.** Borrow the pin-plus-ring warning and the translucent shadow.

### Dorfromantik (2022; PC and Switch)

1. **Palette and light.** Painterly, on pastel pink, peach or sky backgrounds. Every tile shows a soil-coloured side edge, so the board reads as a diorama.
2. **Readability.** Biomes are distinct at a glance, with white smoke wisps over villages.
3. **Life and stock.** Smoke, windmills, a train.
4. **Interface.**
   - **Valid slots are soft white hexes**, slightly lighter than the background.
   - The tile stack sits at bottom right with the next tile on top and a remaining-count badge. The score is at top right.
   - Quest tags pinned on the map show an icon and a target number.
   - **A completed quest pops an orange badge with a white check mark** above its area.
5. **Verdict.** Borrow the ghost slots, the quest tags pinned to the map and the check-badge pop.

### The Battle of Polytopia (2016-; phone-native)

1. **Palette and light.** Flat-shaded, saturated low-poly, with a layered soil edge on the board and tribe-coloured picket borders.
2. **Readability.** City plates are coloured pills with the name, a star level and population pips.
3. **Life and stock.** **Harvestable tiles glow with soft rings.**
4. **Interface.** Score, stars and turn in thin white text at top centre. **Four big round buttons at the bottom (Menu, Stats, Tech Tree, Next Turn ✓)** with labels, each about 13% of the screen height.
5. **Phone.** One thumb row, generous targets, and ✓ as the primary action.
6. **Verdict.** Borrow the glow rings for "you can act here" and the big ✓ for confirm.

### SimCity BuildIt (2014-; phone-native)

1. **Palette and light.** Hyper-saturated.
2. **Readability.** Landmark silhouettes; tower height signals zone density.
3. **Life and stock.** Boats, traffic, timers on everything.
4. **Interface.** Edges and corners only, about 12% coverage:
   - Top left: a level ring with a progress arc, plus population and happiness.
   - A left column of utility counters that turn red at their cap.
   - Top right: currencies and a gear. On the right: event icons with countdowns.
   - **Bottom right: a big yellow hard-hat Build button.**
5. **Phone.** The centre is always clear, and the primary action sits under the right thumb.
6. **Verdict.** Borrow the corner discipline and the level ring. Avoid the currency clutter.

### Pocket City (2017; phone-native)

1. **Palette and light.** Clean, bright isometric.
2. **Readability.** Blocky silhouettes with roof colour per zone.
3. **Life and stock.** **Citizen speech bubbles**, cars, lava disasters.
4. **Interface.** A white dock with a **raised central blue BUILD circle**, and Quests marked with a yellow "!". The build sheet rises as a tall white list of cards: thumbnail, name, tier bolts, description, green price. **A locked item shows a grey hex with the level it needs.** A red alert banner sits at top centre.
5. **Phone.** The sheet covers about 40% of the width.
6. **Verdict.** Borrow lock-shows-requirement and "!" on Quests. The Thebes sideways tray beats a tall sheet over the map.

### Pocket City 2 (2025)

1. **Palette and light.** A toy look with **lavender-grey roads and white buildings with blue-purple shade sides**, close to the Thebes shade.
2. **Readability.** Strong landmark silhouettes.
3. **Life and stock.** Cars, boats, planes, crowds.
4. **Interface.**
   - PC: two white translucent pill groups at the bottom: Mayor, Stats, Quests and Events at left, and a labelled "BUILD" group (Roads, Buildings, Zones, Nature, Repair, Demolish) at right.
   - Top left: a level hex with an XP bar. Top right: speed controls and a gear.
   - Mobile: **one big blue "Build" pill at bottom right** and a **small dark toast** under the city name. About 10% coverage.
5. **Phone.** Status top left, secondary actions bottom left, Build bottom right, centre untouched.
6. **Verdict.** Borrow this layout almost directly.

## Part 2: lessons across the games

### Palette and lighting lessons

- **Calm ground, busy buildings.** Pharaoh ANE and Townscaper look rich because their ground is quiet. Caesar III and Children of the Nile look muddy because theirs is noisy or dark. Keep the ground texture within about ±4% lightness, and put saturation into roofs, rugs, mosaics, banners and goods.
- **Warm light, cool shade.** Pharaoh ANE, Zeus, Townscaper's white mode and Pocket City 2 all shade white walls blue-violet. Keep the existing lavender shade and the `#3E3260` ground shadow, and push lit faces about 3-5% toward amber so white never reads as grey.
- **Water is teal, never olive.** Draw the river in three bands: a deep teal channel, turquoise shallows about a quarter-tile wide along the bank, and 3-6 px white ripple dashes drifting slowly.
- **Fertile soil reads as chocolate.** Pharaoh ANE's dark, feathered mud flats say "farm here" without an icon.
- **A global grade in one pass** (Islanders, Townscaper): after drawing the world, fill one rectangle with `globalCompositeOperation = 'multiply'` at 6-12% alpha. Use amber for the harvest, blue-teal for the flood and rose for dusk.
- **Translucent long shadows** (Mini Motorways) match the Thebes two-layer violet shadow. Keep them see-through so rugs and mosaics show through.

### Readability at small sizes

- **One roof prop per tier.** Pharaoh ANE separates its tiers at 30-40 px through the roof alone. Give each of the nine Thebes tiers one unique prop: a mat, a striped awning, pots, a pergola, a rug, a garden, a painted stripe, columns, a banner. Check that each one is legible at 32 px tile width.
- **Material and height step up** (Anno 1800): mud brick, then whitewash, then whitewash with teal columns and a coloured parapet band.
- **Separate stacked blocks:** add a 1 px highlight about 12% lighter along each roof's front parapet, alongside the existing dark contact line where walls meet the ground.
- **Roads lighter than sand, with a kerb:** about 8-10% lighter, with a 1 px edge in `#5A4A6A` at about 20% alpha (Pharaoh ANE, Mini Motorways).
- **A category accent colour** (Nebuchadnezzar's tabs, Pharaoh's painted bands): food green, industry terracotta, religion teal, entertainment pink, government gold. Paint the band or awning on each building, and use the same tint on its tab and card frame.
- **Card art is the building itself:** draw each card thumbnail by calling the building's canvas draw function at small scale.

### Making the map feel alive cheaply

- **Smoke:** 3-4 soft circles rising 10-14 px from bakeries, potters and some houses, alpha falling from 0.35 to 0, on a 2-2.5 s loop with a random phase per building.
- **Visible stock:** four fill states (empty, low, half, full) for granary bins and storage-yard piles, chosen from the stock ratio.
- **Crop stages:** four per field tile (bare, sprouts, green, gold), growing from one edge so a single field shows two stages.
- **Boats:** an occasional felucca drifting along the river centreline at about 0.3 tiles per second.
- **Banners:** two-frame flutter at 2-3 Hz on temple poles and festival plazas.
- **Speech lines** (Nebuchadnezzar, Pocket City): one line in the info sheet when a house or walker is tapped. Never float bubbles over the map centre.
- **Birds:** 3-5 V-shapes crossing every 30-60 s.
- **Later:** a dusk grade plus 2×2 px amber window dots on higher-tier houses.

### Interface chrome: parchment vs dark glass vs minimal

| Style | Seen in | On a warm, light map |
|---|---|---|
| Dark wood or leather with a gold rim | Pharaoh ANE, Emperor, Children of the Nile | Frames the map, high contrast, says "Egypt". Heavy if large. |
| Parchment or stone | Caesar III, Anno menus | Good for long text. Competes with sand and whitewash on a bar. |
| Dark glass | Nebuchadnezzar, Timberborn, Banished, Anno 117 | Neutral and modern. Busy when crammed. |
| Bright light pills | Zeus, Pocket City, SimCity BuildIt | Friendly, but blends into a whitewash city. |
| Minimal glyphs | Islanders, Mini Motorways, Townscaper, Dorfromantik | Needs very strong cues in the world. |

**Recommendation.**
- Always-on chrome (top bar, dock, tray, confirm bar) in **warm espresso glass**: about `#2E1E16` at 88-92% opacity, a 1 px rim in `#C9A35A` at 60%, cream (`#F3E6CC`) text and line icons.
- **Parchment** (`#F1E4C6`, brown text) only for the faces of slide-up sheets that hold paragraphs.
- Serif capitals for titles only. A plain sans at 14-16 px for numbers and labels.

**Coverage budget, from what I saw:** Islanders about 5%, Pocket City 2 about 10%, SimCity BuildIt about 12%, Pharaoh ANE about 12-15%, Nebuchadnezzar about 22% with its tray open, Banished up to 90%. On a 390×844 portrait phone, a 44 px top bar plus a 64 px dock is about 13% idle. A 100 px tray brings it to about 25%, which is fine only while building. At 844×390 landscape, shrink the top bar to 36 px and move the dock to a vertical rail on the right edge.

### How the next action is signposted

Seen in the set:
- Ghost slots where placement is valid (Dorfromantik).
- Green and red footprints (Nebuchadnezzar, Pharaoh ANE).
- A dashed reach outline (Nebuchadnezzar).
- Glow rings on tiles where you can act (Polytopia).
- Pins plus a timer ring on struggling buildings (Mini Motorways).
- Red status icons over buildings, with grouped counts (Timberborn).
- Count badges on buttons (Pharaoh ANE, Islanders) and a "!" on Quests (Pocket City).
- Countdowns on corner icons (SimCity BuildIt).
- Locked items that show their requirement (Pocket City).

Rules for Thebes:
- **Placement:**
  - Tint valid tiles with a cream fill at 25% alpha.
  - Draw the footprint as a 2 px outline: teal `#3FB6A8` when valid, terracotta `#D2553F` when blocked.
  - Show the reach radius as a dashed 1 px line (4 px on, 4 px off) marching at 8 px/s, only while placing.
- **A suggested spot:** a pulse ring at 1 Hz for 2 cycles (radius from 0.6 to 1.2 tiles, alpha from 0.6 to 0), then a static 1 px outline until it is tapped. Only one pulsing thing at a time.
- **Problems:** a 12-14 px terracotta disc with a cream glyph above the building, at every zoom level. A corner button with a count badge opens a sheet of problems grouped with counts, and tapping a row pans to the next instance.
- **Locked cards stay in the tray**, desaturated to about 45%, with a requirement chip ("Tier 3 houses") instead of a price.
- **Goals:** a top-bar chip showing progress ("Houses 6/10") that opens a sheet of portrait quest cards. Pin a tag on the map only for goals tied to a place.

### Celebration and reward visuals

Seen in the set: Dorfromantik's orange check badge, Islanders' filling score ring and the "+" that lights for the next pack, the level hex and XP bar in Pocket City, SimCity's level ring, Polytopia's "+6" stars, Anno's newspaper screens, and Pharaoh ANE's festival streamers.

Suggestions, cheap on canvas and never covering the map centre for long:
- **House tier-up:**
  - Squash and stretch from 1.0 to 1.08 and back over 160 ms, anchored at the base.
  - 6-8 confetti squares in the tier's accent colour, falling 20 px over 900 ms.
  - A "+Tier 4" chip that floats up 24 px and fades over 900 ms.
- **Building finished:** a cream dust puff (3 circles expanding and fading over 400 ms), then one gold sparkle at the roof peak.
- **Goal complete:**
  - A rounded-square badge pops above the goal's place: teal or orange, with a cream check mark and a 2 px white outline.
  - After about 600 ms it shrinks into the top-bar goal chip, and the count bumps for 120 ms.
- **Milestones** (a tier unlocked, a chapter, a monument course finished) are the only moments that earn a sheet: a short line and one illustration, closed with one tap, plus a 1.5 s streamer flourish on the plazas.
- **Rhythm:** at most one celebration every 2 s, so a burst of upgrades doesn't turn into noise.
