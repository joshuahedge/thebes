# Version 8.3 "Lines": rulings 1430-1449 (Track C, crews at any distance and the workers' camp)

The rule is src/sim/monuments.js (crewSpeed, crewPlan, guildsNear, siteCrews, campQuote, setCamp); the data is MONUMENT in src/data/monuments.js; the words are UI4_TEXT and `V83_C_TEXT` in src/ui/text.js; the sheet is src/ui/monumentSheet.js; the tents are src/render/monuments.js. Tests: tests/v83/c/.

1430. A builders' guild's crew works any open site its roads reach, a running ferry included, nearest first with the same tie rules. `crewPlan`, `guildsNear` and the goal "Build a builders' guild near it" all read `roadLinked` (`buildingsInReach` with Infinity, less what no road reaches: it lists such a building at distance Infinity, which would otherwise count as linked). — Cost if wrong: a guild a ferry away works a site the player did not expect; pausing the ferry takes the crew back.
1431. The speed is `crewSpeed(dist, camp)`: 1 up to `crewReach` (12) road tiles, then `crewLoss` (10%) less for every further `crewStep` (12) tiles completed, never below `crewFloor` (50%), 1 with a camp: `max(floor, 1 - 0.1 x floor((dist - 12) / 12))`. So 12 and 13 tiles are 100%, 23 is 100%, 24 is 90%, 35 is 90%, 36 is 80%, 40 is 80% (the design's example), 48 is 70%, 60 is 60%, 72 and more 50%. The controller's call, from the design's words ("10% less for each further 12 tiles") and its example; my first reading counted a step as soon as it began. — Cost if wrong: one function (ceil instead of floor gives 13 tiles 90% and 40 tiles 70%).
1432. `crewDayUnits(world, g, site)` multiplies a crew's day by its speed on the site it works (the site `monumentDay` is on; with none given, the one the crew plan gives the guild; a guild with no site works at 1). Work gold is per unit laid, so a slow crew is paid less each day and costs the same gold per unit. — Cost if wrong: a far crew is cheaper per month than a near one; the gold per unit is the lever if it should not be.
1433. `guildsNear(world, probe)` returns `[{ id, dist, speed }]`, nearest first, of the unpaused guilds not already in the crew plan, at any distance by road; speed is the walk's alone (a camp is the site's, set up once it stands). The probe's walk is not cached (a ghost visits a new footprint at every hover). — Cost if wrong: the chip names a guild that a busier site will take first.
1434. The site sheet has one line for each crew the plan sends it (`siteCrews`: id, dist, walk, speed, working): "Crew from the guild 40 road tiles away: works at 80% (the walk)", "... works at full speed", and with the camp "... works at full speed" (the one "Workers' camp: crews at full speed" row says why). A guild's own sheet adds "Crew at work on the small mastaba: 40 road tiles away, works at 80%" only when its walk slows it. "No crew" and the idle guild no longer say "within 12 road tiles"; the First's why says full speed within 12, slower beyond. — Cost if wrong: a sheet with two or four crews has two or four extra lines.
1435. The placing chip is the nearest free guild's walk and speed from the sim's own condition (`conditionsFor` crew: n, dist, speed): "Builders' guild: 40 road tiles (80%)", or "No builders' guild yet" with none free. — Cost if wrong: with every guild busy at other sites the chip says "yet" although a guild would come later.
1436. A workers' camp costs `round(campShare x costOf(world, type, { style }))`, at least 1, as the city pays the founding price at the moment it is set up (difficulty, rank growth and style: 15 gold for a small obelisk on Normal, 19 on Hard, 17 at Great capital). It is not stored. — Cost if wrong: a camp set up early in a rank costs less than the same camp later; the price on the button is always what the tap takes.
1437. `setCamp(world, siteId)` refuses with `not_site`, `finished`, `has_camp` and `no_gold`, in that order. `no_gold` is paying below 0, the rule for every monument gold (founding, work, gilding, the palace), not the building debt floor the plan names: work stops below 0, so a camp bought into debt would stop the crews it was bought for. — Cost if wrong: a player cannot take a camp into the debt floor, as they cannot take a founding.
1438. The camp is not undoable: as enlarging the palace does, it drops the site's own `place` entry from the undo stack, so Undo cannot take the site away with the camp's gold. `site.camp` is true while the site stands and `commitCrew` deletes it when the monument is finished (leaving a finished site as an 8.2.1 one); absent means no camp. — Cost if wrong: Undo is refused once a camp is paid for, as it is once work has begun.
1439. The camp's offer is a row on the site sheet, right under the crew lines it speeds up, not in the actions at the foot: a button ("Set up a workers' camp: 300 gold. Far crews work at full speed") while it can be paid, else its words alone ("Workers' camp: 300 gold. You have 120 gold: it can't put the treasury below 0"), then "Workers' camp: crews at full speed" once set up. It shows only once a crew would be slowed. The tap asks first, with the price and the effect, and says it cannot be undone. — Cost if wrong: a player with all crews near never learns of the camp; the tip and the crew lines are the way in.

1447.
