# Version 9.3.1, Group C: straight crossings (rulings 2035-2039)

Josh: "I still think building a bridge or ferry crossing is hard. It just needs to default to straight lines by default."

- **2035.** A road drag in Straight (not Route, not the Gate card) whose L, or whose finger's path, would cross open water (water with no bridge on it) is laid as one straight run from where it began, along the axis it moved furthest (a tie runs along the row). A drag that meets no water keeps its L and its finger's path. Cost if wrong: a road that should bend just after a bridge takes two strokes.
- **2036.** The Crossing card's drag is always one straight run, and it lays the crossing on that line or nothing: no snap to a nearby crossing (ruling 1347 no longer applies to the card's drag). Where the line can't be laid, the bar says why in the existing words and Show me goes to the nearest crossing that works. The sim's previewFerry still snaps by default (drawFerry and older callers judge a line as before); `snap: false` is an option. Cost if wrong: a drag one row off a good crossing is refused rather than mended; Show me or a tap mends it.
- **2037.** A tap with no drag still picks the nearest crossing shown (ruling 1348 kept), whatever line was drawn before; a new Crossing drag replaces the last line instead of joining it. Cost if wrong: small; one crossing per tick, as before.
- **2038.** Ends: the Crossing card's crossing lands where its straight line meets dry land on each side, whatever tile the finger began or lifted on (in the water or past the bank). The road tool keeps ruling 1395's look-ahead for a stroke that stops in the water ("drag on to the far bank, or ✓ lays the road to the bank"). Cost if wrong: on the road tool, a finger lifted a tile short of the far bank must drag on.
- **2039.** Words: How to play's Crossings entry says a line that meets water runs straight (V931C_TEXT.help in text.js's Group C section, read by one line in src/ui/v71/ferryText.js) and no longer says a bent line is refused. The bar keeps the existing words. Cost if wrong: none.
