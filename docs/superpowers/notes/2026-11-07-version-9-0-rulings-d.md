# Version 9.0 "Dynasty": rulings 1565-1584 (Track D, dynasty: Pass the throne, the titles, the heir's traits, the sealed score)

The titles and traits are src/data/dynasty.js (`TITLES`, `TRAITS`, `deeperLoss`) and src/sim/dynasty.js (`titleOf`, `titleMeasures`, `heirFactor`, `firstWarnAfterOf`, `heirHighFactor`, `countDynastyMonth`, `canPassThrone`, `passScore`, `passThrone`). The levers: src/sim/monuments.js `baseDayUnits`, src/sim/economy.js `monthFigures`/`taxesAt`, src/sim/palace.js `taxFactorOf`, src/sim/costs.js `soldiersPayOf`, src/sim/raids.js (the first `nextWarnDay`), src/sim/production.js (`heirRate`, the rate line, `harvestQuote`), src/sim/flood.js (`levelHarvest`, `nextFlood`, `floodOutlook`), src/sim/trade.js `tradePrices`, src/sim/immigration.js `monthlyArrivals`; src/sim/feats.js exports `fedNow`. The interface: src/ui/v90/passSheet.js (the sheet and the heir's card), src/ui/v90/dynastyUi.js (the heir's line, the score section's lines, the City caption, the tip), src/ui/scoreUi.js (its summary side calls it), src/ui/monumentSheet.js `taxModel`, src/boot.js (`hooks.passThrone`, `canPassThrone`, `startHeir`). Words: Track D's section of src/ui/text.js (`V90_D_TEXT`, the Taxes row's words, `TIP_TEXT.heir_trait`, How to play's Dynasty). Tests: tests/v90/d/.

1565. A title is the highest fit (measure ÷ bar) among the titles whose bar is met, ties by the table's order (Unconquered, Builder, Merchant, Bountiful, Generous); none met is the Steady. — Cost if wrong: a title a player did not expect; the score sheet shows it as they play.
1566. The bars start at Unconquered 3 raids (none lost, no tribute), Builder 2,000 units, Merchant 0.40 of the bills, Bountiful 0.90 of month ends fed, Generous 0.50 of month ends at Low (High ≤ 0.1), the share titles needing 24 counted months; the balance check sets them. — Cost if wrong: one title dominates; data retunes.
1567. Each trait is two numbers read through `heirFactor` (or, for the first warning's days and the Generous's High, `firstWarnAfterOf` and `heirHighFactor`) in the lever's one function; the score never reads it. A lever whose factor is 1 takes the 8.4 arithmetic untouched (no `x * 1` reordering, no `1 - (1 - f)`), so every Steady number is 8.4's to the last bit. — Cost if wrong: a trait felt too little or too much; data retunes.
1568. The Unconquered's pay discount stacks with the captured weapons' (0.8 x 0.75 of the full pay), rounded once. The weapons' own "saves N" line and `tally.spoilsGold` (src/sim/spoils.js `spoilsSaving`, `spoilsMonth`) count the saving off the heir's own pay (fix round), reading `TRAITS` from the data leaf. — Cost if wrong: a winning Unconquered pays 40% less for a year.
1569. Pass the throne seals the best of the reign's seals and today's score (today's on a tie), with the Pass's own day, `passed: true` and the title; Start in the heir's new-city card does the seal, the records and the new city at once, and Back (like Not yet) changes nothing. — Cost if wrong: none to the sim.
1570. New city without passing keeps the current heir trait, and the abandoned city joins no list. — Cost if wrong: a player who never passes keeps one trait.
1571. The heir's first-screen line is a caption (the info tier) in the guidance row, which is the goal strip's place on a phone, shown when a heir's city opens before its day 10 and kept in Recent; not a second line under the goal strip, which hides with no goal (Help Off) and on a phone whenever the row is a tip's. — Cost if wrong: the line shows for 6 seconds rather than 10 game days; the city summary and the tip say it again.
1572. The Bountiful's weakness (Q6) is a low flood's loss x1.5, applied in src/sim/flood.js `levelHarvest` (and `nextFlood`), so the farms (`harvestFactor`, `harvestQuote`), the flood's words and the food forecast all read one number; flood.js reads `TRAITS` from the data leaf because it may not import the sim's dynasty.js. A drought's share is not traited: its words are src/sim/crops.js `foodOutlook`'s, outside the track. — Cost if wrong: a Bountiful heir shrugs off a drought; adding it means the drought's words too.
1573. Pass the throne is offered from the City milestone (`atLeast(world, 'city')`, the month end that makes it), never by the people alone, and never in the lesson (its game has no `passThrone`, `canPassThrone` or `startHeir` hook). Its heir's card keeps New city's choices (size, layout, the preview's seed: `hooks.newCityChoices`). — Cost if wrong: a city at 400 people waits for the month's end to pass.
1574. (Replaced by 1584 in the fix round.) Neither of the Generous's counts may exceed the month ends counted from Town. — Cost if wrong: none.
1575. The regnal number is one more than the rulers already of that name (records.addRuler's count), written in Roman numerals: the first is "Ramesses I". The name field shows on every Pass, prefilled with the dynasty's name (else "Ramesses"), 16 characters with a label and a hint, and is kept by `setDynasty` only at Start. — Cost if wrong: a player renames by accident on a later Pass; Back undoes it.
1576. The runner-up ("Close behind") is the next title by fit, met or not, with a fit above 0; a title ruled out (a raid lost, a tribute paid, Peaceful, too few counted months, High too often) has fit 0 and is never it. — Cost if wrong: a reign far from every bar shows no runner-up.
1577. The score section says, from City, "Remembered as: …" with its why line and the next title's progress ("Close behind: the Merchant (trade paid 31% of the bills; 40% earns it)"), and heads a heir's city with "Heir of …" and the trait's two lines at any rank. — Cost if wrong: one more block in the summary.
1578. The City caption ("You may now pass the throne (game menu)") comes with the City milestone's event, so once a city; the `heir_trait` tip fires at a heir's city's month end and shows once a device, as every tip. — Cost if wrong: none.
1579. The Merchant's weakness touches every producer that makes one good from another (`consumes`), the brickworks included, as the design's "anything that consumes an input" says. — Cost if wrong: a Merchant's brick monuments go 15% slower; a data list can narrow it.
1580. A Pass also writes the city's records row (`noteCity`) and a chronicle stamp (`addSeal`), as Seal this reign does, so the passed reign's best and stamp show on the title card like any sealed one. — Cost if wrong: a passed reign shows twice, as a stamp and as a ruler.
1581. The palace's Taxes row words each choice from `taxesAt` (the rate's factor, the Generous's High, the Builder's 5%); without a city to quote, the words are 8.4's, and for the Steady they are 8.4's exactly. — Cost if wrong: none.
1582. The floodplain food forecast (`floodOutlook`) counts the Bountiful's 15% on both "now" and "without the flood", so the difference is still the flood's alone. — Cost if wrong: none.
1583. Every word that names a flood's percentage for this city (the summary's flood line, the farm's "flood coming" row, the forecast and rise captions, the Nilometer's rows) takes the sim's factor (`floodLook`'s `factor` and `levelFactor`, `nextFlood`'s `harvest`, `levelHarvest`), so a Bountiful heir reads its deeper loss; words with no city (How to play, the Nilometer's rule) keep the data's. Start in the heir's card is guarded against a second tap, sets the dynasty's name only once the seal is made, and a ruler row the records do not keep (read back, since a failed write is swallowed) pops the seal, keeps the city and says "Couldn't pass the throne". — Cost if wrong: a refused write leaves a chronicle stamp for a Pass that did not happen.
1584. The Generous reads two new month-end counts, `lowMonths` and `highMonths` (world version 12's `V90_TALLY`, 0 for every new and migrated city), counted from Town in `countDynastyMonth`, never 8.4's `taxLowMonths`/`taxHighMonths`, which count from the first month. — Cost if wrong: two more tally keys in every save.
