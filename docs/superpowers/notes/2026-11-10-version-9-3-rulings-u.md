# Version 9.3 rulings, Track U (the decree's interface), 1975 to 1984

One line each, with its cost if wrong.

- **1975. The words** live in `U93_TEXT` (src/ui/text.js, the Track U section): each decree's name, twist, bonus label, the mark ("Sea Peoples, week 47"; two names joined with " and "), the card, chip, end card, menu rows, locked line and share line. "Decree" is the mode's one name. Cost if wrong: a string change in one section.
- **1976. A decree run is the lesson's shape.** `current.decree`, hooks that drop Pass the throne, the heir picker, a new city, Export, Home, the ruler list and `records` itself (so no seal or chronicle row can be written from it), saves to the decree slot, no guide, no daily goals (`syncDaily` returns at once under `hooks.decreeRun`). Cost if wrong: a city row or a stamp from a decree run in the records.
- **1977. The title card** sits after the main buttons and before the lesson and Options rows, so Continue stays first on a phone. The gate is a city row with a Town split or any ruler. A `d1` link opens the card on its week even before the gate, and keeps a named sender as a rival (never your own name). Cost if wrong: a new device sees the card early on a friend's link.
- **1978. The chip** is the first row under the top bar (a pill, "Year N of 6" and the mark), not inside the bar, because the phone's bar is full at 150% text. Tap reopens the card, or the end card once the run is over. Cost if wrong: one row less map at the top.
- **1979. The first screen** is the decree card as a sheet over a paused game; Begin starts the clock at 1x. From the chip it says Close and does not touch the speed. A continued run reopens on it too, paused. Cost if wrong: one extra tap on Continue.
- **1980. A forbidden card stays in the tray, locked**, with "Not under this decree" (unlike a milestone-locked card, which the layered tray hides), so the player can see why. `reasonText('decree')` says the same for a refused placing. Cost if wrong: a greyed card in Defence under Open city.
- **1981. The numbers shown are the numbers the sim uses.** The interface reads `ruleFactor` (monument sheet, placing), `ruleUnitPay` (soldiers' pay) and `ruleReach` (the firehouse's placing count, its danger sheet, the building sheet's reach and the selected building's reach overlay, `reachTiles` in src/render/overlays.js), and `capacityOf` (the store sheet's Stored line and the Fetch control's amounts, src/ui/s47/carts.js), per Track R's rulings 1956 and 1960. Prices keep reading `costOf`, which Track R bends with `ruleCost`. The granary's fill drawing (src/render/art/stages.js) still reads the plain capacity, so a Low Nile granary looks full at half. Cost if wrong: a number on screen that differs from the sim's.
- **1982. The ruler list's decree rows** are merged with rulers by date, newest first: mark, medal words, score of 600, six parts, tries, the map square. No regnal number, no cartouche. Play this map starts a plain city (New city's question) on the week's map at Normal, not a decree run. Cost if wrong: a player expects a decree run from the row.
- **1983. The end card** is 9.2's lesson end card in order: the total and medal, then the three choices (Share, Try again, Back to your city), then your best, the six parts and the rivals on this week (the best one side by side). The run is noted once (`records.noteDecree`), marked by `world.decree.noted` (an optional field, so old saves and WORLD_VERSION are untouched). Cost if wrong: a second open of the card counts a second try.
- **1984. R and S are merged into this branch and read directly**: `decreeScore` (R), `createSaveStore({ slot: 'decree' })`, `records.noteDecree` (given the end card's score and the mark's words, since S takes the score from its caller), `records.decrees`, `records.addRival` with a week, `decreeLink` (with `splitMonths`) and `decreeShareText` over S's `DECREE_SHARE_TEXT`, which keeps S's words; U's own share line is gone. The end card offers only Share, Try again and Back to your city, none of them sim commands, so R's ruling 1962 refusals never meet it. Cost if wrong: a try counted without a row, or a share line in two places.
