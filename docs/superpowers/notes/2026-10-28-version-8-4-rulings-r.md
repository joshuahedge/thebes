# Version 8.4 "Gold and war": rulings 1485-1499 (Track R, raids: the clock's rules, the scouts, the low-Nile lean, captured weapons, the celebration)

The rules are src/sim/raids.js (`laterWarnDay`, `leanWait`, `raidDay`, `raidMonth`, `raidAlerts`, `raidMood`), src/sim/scouts.js, src/sim/spoils.js and src/sim/costs.js `soldiersPayOf`; the data is `RAIDS.leanDays`, `SCOUTS` and `SPOILS` in src/data/raids.js and `DANGER_MOOD` in src/data/mood.js; the drawing is src/render/scouts.js; the words are `V84R_TEXT` in src/ui/text.js; the moment is src/ui/v60/battle.js; the sound is `SFX.fanfare`. Tests: tests/v84/r/.

1485. The clock's rules are built and tested at 8.4's numbers (first warning 300; later gaps 360-600; the 60-day lean) while `RAIDS` keeps today's (720; 720-1440; `leanDays` 0), as ruling 1463 says: `leanDays` is a new data number, `laterWarnDay` leans only above 0, and every Track R test sets 8.4's numbers through one helper (`at84` in tests/v84/r/helpers.js) that puts the data back however the test ends. The lesson walk-through is held at both. A save's scheduled warning keeps its day. — Cost if wrong: wave 2 sets four numbers, and a test that leaked them would move every later raid number (the lesson tests would hear it).
1486. The lean: `laterWarnDay` draws the gap as always, then an arrival (the warning's day + `warningDays`) that falls 1 to `leanDays` days before Shemu's first day waits until that day (`leanWait`); Shemu's first day is read from `SEASONS` (month 3, day 60 of the year), never typed. It is never earlier than drawn, takes no draw, and gives gaps of 360-660 at 8.4's numbers. The first warning (`raidDay`'s clock) never leans; `payTribute` and `endRaid` call `laterWarnDay`, so a paid-off raid's successor leans too. Over 1,000 seeded draws 45-55% of arrivals land in Shemu (about a third without it). — Cost if wrong: raids grow predictable by season.
1487. Scouts are read from the state, never stored: `scoutsAt(world)` gives `{ entry, dir, days }` while `nextWarnDay - SCOUTS.days <= day < nextWarnDay` with no warning up and no raid under way, `entry` being `nextRaidEntry` at the raid's index (the warning's own). None on Peaceful, before the clock, with no entry, or while `world.lesson` is set (the lesson's raid, staged for today, has an empty window anyway; the flag is a second lock). Two or three scouts (`scoutCount`, seeded by `RAID_SALT.scouts`) stand still at the entry, drawn as the `raider` figure with ids from 200000. `raidDay` pushes one `raid_scouts` event on the window's first day; `raidAlerts` adds a non-critical `raid_scouts` alert while they stand; the caption comes from that event in battle.js. The alert's fix says "A raid warning may follow within N days" (N the days left to the warning, from the sim), the caption keeps the design's words ("raiders may come within a month", the month from `SCOUTS.days`). — Cost if wrong: a city loaded inside the window shows the scouts and the alert but not the caption (the event is the first day only); none to the sim.
1488. A beaten raid leaves captured weapons and Victory. `raidSpoils` (called by `endRaid`) sets `raid.spoils = { until: day + SPOILS.days }` and puts the same on the report; a lost raid changes nothing and a tribute never reaches it, so nothing is left by a paid-off raid. `soldiersPayOf` is `soldiersPayWith` (spoils.js): the full sum, less `SPOILS.share` rounded once while `day < until` (the full-pay sum moved to spoils.js so costs.js and spoils.js never import each other). `raidMonth` (empty until now) calls `spoilsMonth`, which adds the month's saving to `tally.spoilsGold` after the month's pay. The mood row of a beaten raid is `victory`, `DANGER_MOOD.raidBeaten` (+4) for `victoryDays` (90); a lost raid keeps `raid` at -8 for 180. An 8.3 save's beaten raid shows Victory for what is left of its first 90 days, then nothing (it was -3 for 180). — Cost if wrong: a small gold and mood lift per win.
1489. The celebration is the alert row's moment: `momentWords` adds "Captured weapons: soldiers' pay 25% less for a year." (from `SPOILS`) to a beaten raid whose report carries `spoils`, the `fanfare` plays once on a beaten `raid_end`, and the line is in the screen-reader announcement. It never pauses, never opens a card, and a lost raid has neither. `SFX.fanfare` is four rising brass-like notes ending on a held chord, about a second, softer than the milestone's. — Cost if wrong: easy to miss; the Victory row and the forecast note say the same.
