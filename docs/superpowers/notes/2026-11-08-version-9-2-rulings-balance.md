## The controller's ruling: the gate makes the court quieter, not the game harder (design 3.3; 9.0's T2)

1865. Each request and demand is worth twice the favour, won or lost: `REQUESTS.met` [10, 15] -> [20, 30], `REQUESTS.missed` [10, 20] -> [20, 40] (src/data/court.js); `DEMANDS.favour` met [8, 12] -> [16, 24], failed [15, 20] -> [30, 40], declined 5 -> 10 (src/data/reign.js). Why two: in the Steady's great-work years (11-20) 9.1 sent 11 requests and 10 demands, the gate 5 and 5, so each letter now carries what two did. Measured on the Steady's great-work run, favour at the years' ends 13-20: 9.1 74-100 with one or two gifts a year; the gate alone 58-68 and not one gift in 20 years; doubled 49-100 with one or two gifts a year. Losses double with gains, so a city that ignores the court loses favour as fast as on 9.1 (the careless city again has every due doubled). Nothing else moves: the gate's rhythm (one letter at a time, 5 to 8 months apart), the rewards in gold, the cards' and the events' favour (no card came in the long runs' great-work years on either build; the events' favour choices are 4 or 5), the dues, the gifts. Tried and set aside (gains and losses x2.5 and x3: seed 10 loses raid 8 and never reaches the Seat; gains alone x2.5: seed 5 loses raid 2; x2 with the gold rewards doubled too: the same targets, the great work's lowest 1,517). — Cost if wrong: one met or missed letter now moves favour 20-40 points, so the court's mood swings in bigger steps.
