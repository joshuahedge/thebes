# Version 9.3 fix wave, group A rulings (1990-1994)

One line each, with its cost if wrong. Tests: tests/v93/x/a-decree.test.js; the balance runs: `-Grep "v93 k balance"` (re-run, 20 of 20 pass).

- **1990 (K1):** a months-measure bar is at most `MONTHS_BAR_MAX` = 60 (src/data/decrees.js). The scripted run reaches Town in month 6 and counts 66, so no run can count more than that, and the old 83 could never score 100. Fire season's bar is 60 (was 83), so the script now earns a full bonus part (100, total 316); its medals are [310, 370, 430] (were [290, 350, 410]). Cost if wrong: a very quick Town (before month 6) is the only way to count more than 66, so 60 still leaves a small margin; a player who plays Fire season well hits 100 a little sooner than the other decrees' bars allow.
- **1991 (K2):** a well month (Plague's measure) counts only while the city has at least `WELL_MONTH_HOMES` = 15 Cottage-or-better homes. The scripted city has 11 at Town and 29 by month 12, so 15 drops its first few small months, and a collapsed city (0 homes, 13 people) counts none. Re-measured: Plague's measure is 4 (was 7), so its bar is 5 (was 9) by ruling 1986's x 1.25 rule; the script's bonus is 80, total 218, so its medals stand at [210, 270, 330]. Plague + Low Nile: measures 2 and 25, total 230, medals stand. The card's bonus line now says "(15 or more Cottage homes)" from the constant (src/ui/text.js, the one-line edit in Track U's section). Cost if wrong: a very small city can still avoid sickness for months, but it stops counting at 15 homes of the plan, so the bonus then asks for a bigger city.
- **1992 (R1):** The Vizier's Levy has no `courtReward` factor now (it was 2): the ask is doubled and the reward follows it, so the card's "pays double" is true. The Levy's measure (requests met) is unchanged at 3 in the run, so its bar 4 and medals [260, 320, 380] stand; Stone famine + Vizier's Levy's total moved from 246 to 255 (more gold), so that pair's medals are [250, 310, 370] (were [240, 300, 360]). Cost if wrong: a Levy week pays less gold than before, so the court is a little less of a windfall.
- **1993 (known 10):** three commands the interface reaches never had the decree-ended refusal: `meetDemand`, `layCrossing` (so `drawFerry`, and a ferry through `buildCrossing`) and `sealReign`. All three now refuse with `decree_ended` (scoreUi's seal() goes back to the summary on a refusal). tests/v93/x/a-decree.test.js reads the sim functions the interface imports and fails if one that answers { ok, reason } lacks the check and is not named as a read or a delegate. Cost if wrong: the scan reads source text, so a command that answers in another shape slips past it.
- **1994:** ruling 1986's changed pairs: Sea Peoples + Fire season [410, 470, 530] (was [400, 460, 520]; the Fire half of its bonus is now 100, total 417). K's test for "bonus part 70-90" allows 100 for a capped months bar.

Moved pins: none outside my files. tests/v93/r/end.test.js's tally test now builds its Town world with 15 homes (was a bare Town world), since a well month needs them (ruling 1991).
