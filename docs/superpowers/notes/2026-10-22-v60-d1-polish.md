# Version 6.0, Track D1: the polish pass

Every change the polish pass made, and what it looked at and left alone. Written 22 Oct 2026 on branch `v60-d1` (Track D1, rulings 740-759).

## How it was done

An audit script (a throwaway module run in the page, not kept) walked every screen below at 390x844, 1280x800 and 1920x1080, at 100% and 150% text, under Gentle (what headless Edge shows) and Full for the title. For each state it listed: controls under 44x44 px, controls with no accessible name, text clipped by its own box, text or controls past the screen's edge and a page that scrolls sideways. It was then read by eye in screenshots (`docs/superpowers/notes/screenshots/raw/v60-d1-*.png`, gitignored). The tests in `tests/v60/d1/` hold what was fixed.

Screens covered: the title (fresh, with a saved city, Settings panel, What's new, Huge chosen, a phone held either way), the game early and as the seed-11 sample city, the build tray, a house sheet, City summary, Settings, How to play, the menu, City advice, Goals, the minimap sheet, the court sheet and the court's demand, the great work, plague, drought, locusts, a burning raid, a Large plot and the Huge map. Wave 1's sheets are in that list on purpose.

## What changed

1. **Title card: one tidier set of rows** (rulings 742, 747). Continue, New city with its difficulty and map size under it, then one row of Lesson (How to play until the lesson exists), Settings and What's new, then Install and Bring your city only where offered. This week's Nile (button and hint) and Play with sound / Play muted are gone.
   - Before (`v60-d1-before-title-fresh-*`): a five-row stack with a long weekly line, How to play and What's new sharing a row and Play with sound on its own.
   - After (`v60-d1-title-fresh-*`, `-saved-*`, `-landscape-*`): the three buttons share one row at 100% text; at 150% they take a row each rather than clip.
2. **A word never clips in the title's row** (`min-width: max-content`). The first version let "Settings" run past its button at 360x640 and 150% text; caught by `tests/v60/d1/title.test.js`, which now checks every button on the card at 390x844, 360x640, 844x390 and 1280x800 and at 100% and 150%.
3. **The Settings panel on the title** scrolls inside the card (not the page), with its rows wrapping at large text and the tagline and version giving their lines up, as the What's new panel does.
4. **The Huge note** ("Best on a tablet or computer") is bold, not colour, and sits under the size's own line; on a phone held sideways it stays when the size line hides.
5. **How to play's key list at large text.** At 1280x800 and 150% text a key such as "Arrows or WASD" ran past the side panel's edge and took the "Does" column with it (`v60-d1-before-help-keys-150-*`). The table is now fixed-layout, the keys may wrap in a 40% column and the words wrap anywhere they must (`v60-d1-after-help-keys-150-*`). Clean at all three sizes.

## Looked at and left alone

- **Dock category tabs and the tray's cards past the right edge at 390 px wide** are a scrolling row by design (the audit reports their text as off screen).
- **The top bar's labels at 390 px and 150% text** (Gold, People, Mood, Date) are visually hidden by design (the numbers stay; the budget test holds it).
- **The side panel at 150% text on a laptop** gives a sheet about half the height; it scrolls. That is the panel's design (a 320 px column holding the minimap, Layers, the tabs and the sheet), not a spacing bug.
- **Words on other tracks' sheets**: the house sheet's Fire row reads "Fire: Fire risk: rising..." while Health reads "Health: risk rising..." (the label repeats in one and not the other). The words belong to Tracks B1 and C and tests pin them; recorded for the wave 4 review rather than changed here.
- **The title card at 1920x1080** stays 360 px wide over a large backdrop (the showcase framing tests hold its box); it reads well and every control is 44 px or more.
- No tap target under 44x44 and no unnamed control turned up on any screen above (the audit was clean apart from the two by-design items).

## Rulings (740-759)

740. Huge (112) is offered as the picker's fourth size, so links, records and `?size=` take it; the generator is unchanged (three raid entries, as Large). Cost if wrong: Huge raids come from the same three entries over longer walks; one rule in `entriesFor`.
741. Huge's zoom-out floor keeps its frame within 1.25 x the tiles Large shows at 0.5 (its 80 x 80 map at most): 0.5 on any screen up to 1920 x 1080, higher on a bigger one. Cost if wrong: a big monitor cannot zoom Huge out as far; one constant.
742. This week's Nile (button, hint, `weeklySeed`) goes; the title is Continue, New city (difficulty and size under it), Lesson, Settings, What's new. Cost if wrong: a weekly seed returns in a few lines.
743. A phone is a screen whose shorter side is under 600 px; it is told "Best on a tablet or computer" while Huge is chosen, never blocked. Cost if wrong: a small tablet gets a note it does not need.
744. + and - zoom (= and _ too); the speed steps move to ] and [ (. and , beside them). Cost if wrong: a player used to + for faster must learn the new keys (How to play lists them).
745. The keys that move the view leave a control that needs them (sliders, lists, tabs, radios, menus, anything marked `data-own-keys`, text fields) and leave the arrow keys to a scrolling panel they are inside. Cost if wrong: a custom widget with no role that wants the arrows must carry `data-own-keys`.
746. Sound and music are on by default; a device that stored a choice keeps it. Cost if wrong: a returning player who never chose and has `sound: false` stored stays silent until Settings.
747. The title's third button is Lesson when `hooks.lesson` exists (Track D2 sets it) and How to play until then; Settings opens on the title card with the game's own rows. Cost if wrong: D2 forgets `hooks.lesson` and the title keeps How to play.
748. No tip for the desktop keys or the Huge map: they are choices and settings, not things a city unlocks; How to play holds both. Cost if wrong: one tip row each in `TIPS_V60_D1`.
