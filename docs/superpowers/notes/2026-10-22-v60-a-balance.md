### Costs that grow (rulings 640, 641): `COST_GROWTH`

| Rank | wages | upkeep | monuments | court |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Village, Town | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Market town | 1 | 1.05 | 1 | 1.05 |
| City | 1 | 1.08 | 1 | 1.1 |
| Great city | 1.01 | 1.1 | 1 | 1.15 |
| Capital | 1.01 | 1.12 | 1 | 1.25 |
| Great Capital | 1.08 | 1.25 | 1.1 | 1.4 |
| Seat of the Two Lands | 1.1 | 1.3 | 1.15 | 1.5 |

- Why so little up to Capital: the capital run sits at Capital from month 20 and must keep 60-70% of its taxes spent over months 49-72 (it was
  67.5%, wages 42.9 and upkeep 9.9 points of it). Each point of wages or upkeep growth there moves that share; 1.01 wages and 1.12 upkeep take it to
  67.3% (upkeep rounding per building gives back some). The village is a Market town from month 8 and must stay at 68-75% with its fetch order
  (73.7%): upkeep 1.05 there costs it 0.2 points.
- Beyond Capital the growth is real (wages ×1.08-1.1, upkeep ×1.25-1.3, monuments ×1.1-1.15): no older run reaches those ranks.
- Seat first set at wages 1.12, upkeep 1.35, monuments 1.2: the great-work run then fell into debt in years 13-17 while its crews worked (the
  treasury below 0 stops a crew's day), so 1.1 / 1.3 / 1.15.
- Easier levels (641): a multiplier is 1 + (row − 1) × the difficulty's cost (0.8 Relaxed and Peaceful, 1.25 Hard).
- Money stays whole (640): `src/sim/economy.js` rounds each building's upkeep and its staff's wages, so the card's bill is what the building pays.

### The court's due (rulings 642, 643): `COURT_DUE`

- Taken on the first day of each year from year 8 (the yearly tribute's first year), with the tribute's goods. The rank's share of the treasury
  (Capital and beyond 45%) or of a year's taxes at this month's rate (Capital 20%, Great Capital 22%, the Seat 25%), whichever is more.
- Why a share of the treasury: the late run starts year 7 with 54,000 banked and a steady ~13,000 a year of profit, at Capital, 1,090 people,
  the very city the capital run's band pins. Growth by rank alone cannot take a city that only stays at Capital below its starting bank; a share
  of the treasury holds the bank at profit ÷ share (13,000 ÷ 0.45 ≈ 29,000 at steady favour), which is the plan's "about two years of profit".
- Why a floor of a year's taxes: a treasury share alone can never put a city into debt; the floor (20% of 43,000 at Capital) does, once
  favour is low.
- Favour: ×2 at 30 or less, ×0.75 at 70 or more (first set ×0.5 / ×1 / ×2, then ×1.5 at low: the careless run then ended at +1,958, not in debt).
- Halved while the great temple rises (first 0.5, then 0.25: at 0.5 the great-work run went into debt for five years).
- Late run, dues taken: 27,310 (year 8, steady favour), 18,530, then 10,590-13,130 a year at high favour.
- Careless run, dues taken: 60,210 (year 8: low favour after the missed tribute), then 17,330 a year (the taxes floor ×2), so −3,600 at year 9.

### The court's demands (rulings 644-650): `DEMANDS`

- From Town, the first 10 months after the city's first month end at Town (or after the load), then every 10-14 months; one at a time; an
  offer waits 3 months. Deadlines: goods 8-12 months, gold 4-6, a building 12-18, soldiers 4-6.
- Amounts: goods 3-5 months of the city's output, gold 1-2 months of taxes (at most 60% of the treasury), each × the court's scale and the
  difficulty's cost; soldiers 30-50% of the units at home. Rewards: goods 50-80% of their value, gold 25-40% back, 60 a unit, a building 50-80% of
  its price. Favour: met +8-12, failed −15-20, declined −5.
- The late run's player accepts what it can meet (answer policy, ruling 660); 7 met and 7 declined or failed over years 1-15.

## The great work with the dangers on (rulings 781, 785): not made safe by any number; a doubt for Josh

Ruling 791 left the great-work run winning on day 7,030 of its 7,200, with 2,313 gold the lowest it ever held. The margin is pinned (`great.test.js`: a win by day 7,100, never under 2,000 gold).
What the dangers do to that run, measured:

- **The run is one of two that win.** The same script on the other seeds, dangers off: seed 10 wins on day 6,910 with 76,000 gold; seeds 6-9 fall into debt (-20,000 to -72,000: seed 6 never
  reaches the Seat, 7 and 8 reach it and lose). The books at the Seat are near even: wages go from 22,000 to 32,000 a year when the two new streets' 60 homes arrive, taxes are 45,000-50,000, net
  2,000-7,000, the due 8,000-11,000, the crews 5,000-6,000 while they work. One lost raid (the run loses three even with the dangers off), a plague or a low Nile puts it below 0, where the court
  still takes its due, the mood loses 20 points and the people go.
- **With the dangers on from the city's day 0** (grace to year 4), all six seeds fall into debt and none reaches the Seat (the Estate comes a year or more later, above). With the late run's tower
  spots added, seeds 6 and 7 survive (3,300 and 22,400 gold at year 20, lowest -2,000 and -6,500, every raid beaten, 1,900 people) and 5 and 8 collapse; none finishes.
- **With the dangers on from a year after the temple's founding** (the Seat reached as in the tuned run): seed 10 beats every raid and holds 164,000 gold, but the temple waits from year 16 for its
  beer offering (300; the stores hold 250-300 in that city) and is not done by year 20; seed 5 loses the same three raids as with the dangers off and collapses.
- **Each danger alone** (the other three and the raid extras off, on from day 0): plague, low Nile and raids each fall into debt (-39,000, -19,000, -45,000); locusts alone reach the Seat and
  do not finish (2,837 gold, lowest -5,624). So it is not one danger's size.
- **Tried, and moved nothing that mattered** (each over 2-6 seeds; every number put back): a gentler plague (loss 25%, spread 0.08), a gentler low Nile (cut 0.3, eight months), gentler locusts,
  the dangers' gaps doubled, no chieftain and no band growth, no wreck and no loot, Great Capital and Seat costs and dues cut to a third of their growth (or to the Capital's), the Capital's
  due at 25% of the treasury and 10% of taxes, low favour's double due at 1.25, a player's cushion of 6,000 and of 12,000 gold, the great temple 10% and 17% lighter (the tuned run then wins on
  day 6,976 but dips to -3,830, or does not win), smaller offerings (beer 200, linen 150: the tuned run does not win, 934 gold), Memphis stone bought for the temple (win day 7,014, 10,073 gold at
  the end: stone is not what holds the crews; their workers are, years 14-18).
- **Why no number was moved:** the scripted player lives on its treasury (it spends to 400 each month) and the run's outcome flips with any change to its trajectory (a smaller offering turns the
  one winning seed into a debt). Tuning a data row until *that* script survives would fit one chaotic path, not the game. A real player's answers (a bank held against the due, more farms and
  breweries before a stage's offering, stone and beer bought, workers moved to the crews) are not in the script and are untested.

**For Josh's decision (rules, not numbers):** (1) the court's due is taken from a city in debt, which is what turns a bad year into a spiral: a rule that the court takes no due, or half, from a
treasury below 0 would make a shock survivable; (2) a stage's offering sits at the stores' resting level (beer 300 against 250-300 held in one city) and a stage that waits has no Advice item
naming what to do about it; (3) the raids at the Seat are lost with eight working towers where the late Capital beats the same size with the same count, because of where the towers stand (next).

**Doubt: Advice's tower fix stands beside the raid's entry.** The advice for a raid short of towers (and Show me) puts the ghost at the warning's entry on the map's edge. A tower there needs a road
and workers it cannot have (the confirm bar says "needs a road"), so a player who follows the advice literally builds towers that stand empty; the late run's hand-chosen spots (the main street's
ends and the trade road) are what beat the raids. In the grand district run the player's tower fix cannot be placed beside the entry (no road can be routed), so it builds its towers in the middle
of the city and loses raids with the dangers on. Not changed: the target is a design choice (Tracks C and B1), and a fix would move every run's raids.
