# Version 9.3 fix wave, group H (xh): rulings 2010-2014

- **2010.** The three variants of the 2x2 homes (tiers 6 to 9) get distinct plans, in housing.js `bigPlan`: v0 an L (tall back block, wing down the right), v1 a low wide block with a tall tower at the back right and a low pavilion in front, v2 a narrow tall block with a set-back storey and a low terrace range with a lower step in front. Cost if wrong: v1 and v2 roof tells (pool, garden, pots, flag, pergola) sit on new roofs; a tell on the wrong roof would float or be hidden (checked in the sheet shot).
- **2011.** Each variant has its own shadow blocks (review H1), taken from the same `bigPlan` as the drawing. registry.js `withLooks` reads an optional `shadowsFor(opt)` on a table entry (a one-line change, not in `slotFor`). Supersedes ruling 1894's "one shadow set per tier" for tiers 6 to 9. Cost if wrong: none for other tables (an entry without `shadowsFor` is unchanged).
- **2012.** A Capital road is paved only where it is built up, like a City road, and kerbed elsewhere (review G1). Supersedes ruling 1916 ("a Capital road is always paved"). The test in tests/v93/g/streets.test.js was updated, not deleted. Cost if wrong: a Capital's outer roads look like City kerbs rather than avenues.
- **2013.** The Capital street reads differently at play zoom (known item 4): warm sandstone cream with a gold-brown kerb instead of the City's cool white, slabs in three lanes with staggered joints, palms and flower planters beside the lamps. Cost if wrong: only the Capital's baked streets change; no sim or save effect.
- **2014.** Home heights (HOME_H) are unchanged; tests/v93/h bounds and lit-face tests still pass with the new plans.
