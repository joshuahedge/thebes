# Version 9.2, Track V (the look): rulings 1845-1854

Source: the design's section 8, the plan's "Track V", the first review's section 3 and finding 2.11, the deep review's section 4. Nothing here touches the sim, the save or a balance pin. Screenshots: `docs/superpowers/notes/screenshots/raw/v92/v92-v-<what>-<before|after>-<WxH>-t<text>.png` (gitignored).

- **1845.** Track V is cut first if the weekly limit nears 90% before wave 3. (Written in the plan; kept.) Cost if wrong: none now, the track is built.
- **1846. Roads.** Looked at first: ruts, pebbles and the edge exist and are baked into the terrain chunk, so they cost no frame time; what the far zoom lacked was a broad cue. Two strokes are added to the same bake (`terrainRoads.js`): a kerb shadow (the road path moved 2.4 art px down the screen, so only the low side shows it) and a darker centre where the carts run (the edge tone at 30% over the top, 4.6 art px wide). Nothing is drawn per frame. Cost if wrong: roads a shade busier at near zoom; two constants (`KERB`, `CART_TRACK`) retune it.
- **1847. Plots at the far zoom.** From the markers' far zoom out (0.6, `FAR_ZOOM`, tested equal to `MARKER.farZoom`) an unbuilt plot or monument site is a sand square with a shadow under its low side (two fills and one fine edge for every footprint in view) instead of the halo, string and stakes; the sign stays on it. Near, the string and stakes are as before. The older test `v70 p ... costs a few strokes however many there are` is updated: the crowd at the far zoom is 2 fills (was 0). Cost if wrong: a far plot reads less like "pegged out"; the near art is untouched.
- **1848. Burnt rubble.** `drawScorch`'s grey blotch is a tight soot patch under a charred wall stump (a slab with a bite out of its top, a lit top, a dark long face and a darker end, brick courses) with seven blackened bricks strewn round it. The tile's own sprite (heap or burnt shell) stands over the tile's middle, so the stump lies just outside the front edge, on the lower-left or lower-right side by the tile's hash, where it shows. Burning buildings keep their stain. All rubble in view is a few batched fills. Cost if wrong: the stump is hidden where a front neighbour stands over it; the soot patch still reads.
- **1849. Health layer.** The ring and dot on every home go (`drawHomeRisk`, health only; Crime keeps its marks, and the plague's heavy ring-and-cross on a sick home is Track B1's own mark and stays). A home a staffed physician reaches has its footprint edged in a solid line, a home none reaches in a dashed one; the band's tint and hatching are as before (a hatch for "no cover" would have hatched every home of a young city). The legend's two keys (`overlayUi.js dotKeys`, health only) are the two edges, same words. Cost if wrong: a busier map in a big city; the edge is one stroke width.
- **1850. Frame the city.** `cityBounds(world, extra)`, `fitCamera(view, bounds, ...)`, `boundsOutside` and `cameraControl.frameCity(bounds, insets, margin, ms)` in `src/ui/cameraControl.js`: fits every building and road tile to the map the panel, bars and tray leave free, zooming out to it and never in past zoom 1 (and never under the least zoom). Bound to the Map button's double tap (`corners.js`: the second tap closes the Minimap the first opened, then frames) and to a key (1851). Cost if wrong: a double tap flickers the Minimap sheet shut and open.
- **1851. The key is F** (and Shift+F). The key table (`KEYS` in `src/data/ui.js`) had no F; Ctrl+F stays the browser's. Help gets "Fit the city to the screen" (Track V's text section) and the gestures paragraph names both ways. Cost if wrong: Track A's key plans clash; one line in `KEYS` moves it.
- **1852. A city opens framed.** In `createUI`, a city whose camera starts on its start area (`world.focus`, as every game's does) and whose built area plus start area do not all show at the opening zoom is framed at once (a jump); one the screen already holds does not move, and a camera a caller placed elsewhere (every test rig) is never reframed. This is the heir's first screen and a saved city at zoom 1. Cost if wrong: a very big city opens at the least zoom.
- **1853. The score chip.** `scoreIcon` is a medal: two ribbon tails over a round medal with a gold star (`css/v92.css`); its state is "medal", it stays hidden from screen readers (the chip's own words carry the number). Cost if wrong: none; one function.
- **1854. Cartouche headings.** Checked at 375x667 and 150% text with a longest name (16 letters) and its number: the Rulers row fits on one line, but the sealed-ruler card ended the name in an ellipsis ("Neferkaure Amen...") that hid the end of the name and its number. Fixed in `css/v92.css`: the heading wraps, balanced over two lines. Cost if wrong: a taller heading on a very narrow row.
