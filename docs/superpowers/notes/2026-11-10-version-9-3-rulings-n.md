# Version 9.3, Track N: the rest of the art (rulings 1920-1929)

- **1920** The palace's five styles are redrawn through the primitives: walls with a soft shade and a contact line, doors and windows set in under painted lintels, stepped parapets and a projecting cornice on the halls, roof clutter (jars, linen), a small gilded dome on the gilded and lapis pavilions. Shapes, heights and bounds are unchanged. Cost if wrong: the five styles look busier at 0.5 zoom; the tall pylons and banners still carry the tiers.
- **1921** Trees, rocks, reeds and scrub share one `contactShadow` (a soft cast to the right plus a dark patch at the foot). Palms gain a lit and a shaded trunk edge and a crown shade; acacias gain two side lobes and a flared foot; rocks a shaded face and facet; reeds seed heads. Cost if wrong: the cached tree sprites carry a few more draw calls each (they are cached once).
- **1922** Finished monuments shade their main bodies (`barShade`) and gain the contact line and the pyramid's heavier silhouette; sites rising course by course are NOT shaded (they draw live every frame, so the bar stays off the hot path). Cost if wrong: a finished monument costs about 6 to 20 more gradients a frame; a site under way looks as before.
- **1923** Plaza open edges gain a dark base line, a lit lip and a faint shade on the paving; joined edges stay clean so a court still reads as one. Sphinx plinths take the wall shade and contact line.
- **1924** Every figure and animal stands in `footShadow` (a soft cast and a dark foot patch); citizens, women, priests and soldiers gain a shaded side on kilt or robe, a shaded far leg, a neck and a cheek light. Silhouettes and the walk poses are unchanged (the walk helper still moves the same points).
- **1925** The river's nilometer and hunting lodge take the wall shade; the gazelle, ostrich and croc take `footShadow`; the fishing boat gains a shaded strake, a lit gunwale and dark water under the hull. Archery range and stables (armies.js) get stepped parapets, shaded walls, set-in doors.
- **1926** Rubble and burnt or wrecked shells gain a cast shadow and the heap a lit crest.
- **1927** Seasons: sheaves take a cast shadow and a shaded side; the flood pool a darker near rim.
- **1928** Boats (the trade boat, the ferry, the fishing boat) share one hull treatment: a shaded lower strake, a lit gunwale and a dark water line; the trade boat's sail takes a shaded half. The boats live in `trade.js` (a B file) and `ferry.js`; only the boat functions were touched.
- **1929** Carts gain a dark contact patch under the wheel and a shaded underside and lit rim on the bed. Not done (rarely seen, listed for the controller): the raider chariot, elephant and raider boat shadows in armies.js, the ibis, heron and hippo shadows, the fishing wharf, and the charred stump (Track V's, left as it was).
