# Version 9.3.1, group G: rulings 2030-2034 (the placing ghost's depth)

Josh: "When I place buildings or houses, the outline of that building plot sits above the existing house sprites which looks odd." The renderer drew every ghost in `drawOverlay`, after every sprite.

- **2030. A ground layer under the sprites.** The renderer has a new hook, `opts.drawGround(g, range)`, after the terrain, the ambient layer and the ground shadows and before the sorted sprites (art px, art origin set). createUI draws in it the next house's spots, the desirability tint a building would change, and each ghost's ground: its squares' washes, hatching and outlines, the whole footprint's outline, the road joins, the water and road it counts, an area's plots and skipped squares, the house tool's plots. A home in front now covers them as it covers the ground. Cost if wrong: a footprint behind a tall home is hidden the way the ground there is; only its badge shows.
- **2031. Only a monument has a ghost to stand up, and it is sorted with the buildings.** A building's ghost (the palace's too) is a footprint and a badge, with no sprite, so "the ghost sprite in depth order" means a monument's silhouette. createUI hands it to the renderer as `opts.placing: [{ x, y, w, d, draw }]`, which inserts it into the sorted objects at its footprint's depth key, id -1 (behind a building of the same depth), and draws it there at its 35%. The fade of a monument the ghost lies behind (`fadesBehind`) stays. Cost if wrong: if Josh wants a see-through building on every ghost, that is new drawing work; the `placing` list already takes any draw.
- **2032. The marks stay over everything; no faint pass of the hidden part.** `drawGhost` now runs in two passes (`drawGhostGround`, `drawGhostMarks`; `drawGhost` still draws both, for demolish, the dev scenes and the tests). The ✓ ! ✕ badges (their colour is the verdict's), the per-square ✕ and !, the small glyphs (═ ≈ ∩ ✓ !) and the ferry's boat and price are in `drawOverlay`, over everything, as are the bank hint, the open layer (Ground's room, Water, Food) and the lesson's ring, as before. No outline-only pass of a hidden part: the badge sits at the footprint's middle and always says where and whether, the bar names the verdict, and the one thing tall enough to swallow a large footprint, a monument, already fades when a ghost lies behind it; an outline drawn over the homes is the look Josh asked to remove. Cost if wrong: a 1x1 ghost wholly behind a tall home reads only by its badge, and an open layer (Ground's wash) now lies over the footprint, which mutes its outline; a faint dashed pass in `drawOverlay` is a few lines.
- **2033. Roads and the other line tools go under too; demolish stays over.** The brief expected the road drag's preview to be ground already. Checked: it was drawn in `drawOverlay` like the buildings, so a road drag along a row of homes outlined their fronts (shot v931-g-05-after-road, before this ruling). Road, wall, gate and ferry previews now lie on the ground with their glyphs on top, like a real road. Demolish keeps its whole draw over the sprites: its coral marks the very building that would cover it. Cost if wrong: a road's squares behind a tall home are hidden as the built road would be; going back is one line (`groundGhosts` in createUI).
- **2034. At night the footprint dims with the ground.** Night's tint is one multiply after the sprites (7.0, Track S), so anything under the sprites is tinted. The footprint now is; its badges and glyphs, the bank hint and the layers stay above the tint. Cost if wrong: at the deepest night the footprint's outline is darker; the badge still says where and whether.
