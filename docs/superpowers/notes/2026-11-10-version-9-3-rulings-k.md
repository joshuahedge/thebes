# Version 9.3, Track K rulings (1985-1989)

One line each, with its cost if wrong. The runs: tests/v93/k/decrees.test.js, `runCapital(72, { dangers: true, decree })` on the script's seed-5 map, each decree and each pair. Fourteen runs take about a minute, so the file loads only for `tests/run-tests.ps1 -Grep "v93 k balance"` (tests/v93/k/all.js reads the page's grep), never in the default suite or in `-Grep "v93"`.

- **1985:** `runCapital` gains `decree` (`{ week, ids }`), handed to `createWorld`; without it the run is as it was (the golden fingerprint matches). The balance file is kept out of the suite by a conditional import, since 9.2's balance folder has no runs to copy. Cost if wrong: nobody reruns the numbers after a sim change, and the bars drift.
- **1986:** the bar is the run's measure x 1.25 to two significant figures (a count under 10 to the whole number); medals are the run's total rounded down to 10, +60, +120, pairs likewise. The plain script answers no letter and raises no defence, so The Vizier's Levy's run also answers the court (tests/v60/a/answer.js) and The Southern Host's meets each warning with towers (lateRun's `meetWarning`) and two archery ranges by where the bands strike. Every other run is the plain script. Measured (measure, bar, total, medals): Sea Peoples 6, 8, 404, 400; Low Nile 26, 33, 309, 300; Plague 7, 9, 216, 210; Stone famine 3,396, 4,200, 249, 240; Vizier's Levy 3, 4, 268, 260; Open city 3, 4, 243, 240; Golden caravans 0.059, 0.074, 298, 290; Fire season 66, 83, 296, 290; Southern Host 3 of 4 elephants, 4, 418, 410. Pairs: Low Nile + Caravans 45 / 0.061, 311, 310; Sea Peoples + Fire 6 / 67, 407, 400; Stone + Levy 3,394 / 3, 246, 240; Host + Open city 3 / 3, 418, 410; Plague + Low Nile 5 / 25, 238, 230. Every run is at Town or above at year 2 (Golden caravans and its pair at Great City, the rest Capital), and no run lost a raid (Sea Peoples 6 of 6, every other 3 of 3). Cost if wrong: a bar set from a player the decree doesn't assume (the Levy's and the Host's) is too easy or too hard for those two.
- **1987:** The Southern Host's `elephantsFrom` is 1, not 2. `elephantRaid` compares it with the raid's stage, which is counted in time, and a six-year run never reaches stage 1 (it starts 1,890 days after the raid clock), so on 2 no elephant ever came. On 1, every raid after the first may bring them (index 0 is excluded anyway), which is what "from the 2nd raid" means in the twist. R's lever test still passes. Cost if wrong: elephants come a raid earlier than the twist line says on a long-lived decree save (none outlives 6 years).
- **1988:** medals follow the plain script's last-day total, and that city declines in its last year on most runs: a drought from month 61, mood falling to 14, culture 64 to 1 by month 66, and fires burning a temple in month 72. Runs that escape it (Sea Peoples' earlier raids, the Host's towers) score 400-420 and so get bronzes 170-200 above Plague's 210. This was left as measured, because the spec sets bronze from that total. Cost if wrong: the medal scales differ a lot between decrees, so a bronze on Plague is far easier than one on the Host.
- **1989:** Sea Peoples' boats now come in a decree run. The fix mirrors 1987. A decree's `boatsFrom` (`ruleBoatsFrom` in rules.js) replaces `ENEMY_TYPES.boat.fromRaid` where armies.js and raids.js gate boats (`mayCome`), and Sea Peoples sets it to 1. Before, boats waited for stage 4, which a six-year run never reaches. With no decree `mayCome` equals `enemyMayCome` for every kind and stage (tests/v93/k/boats.test.js, and the golden matches). A Sea Peoples run on 2027-W06 (wide-bends) with the sample city sees a boat raid within six years, and the same city without the decree sees none. The river: of the first 12 weeks and every Sea Peoples week to 2028-W49, four are Great oasis weeks with no river. They are 2026-W47 (the very first week), 2027-W01, 2027-W18 and 2027-W41. So the twist now reads "more of them by boat where the river allows" (src/ui/text.js). I chose this over keeping Sea Peoples to river weeks because the rotation is the spec's fixed order. Changing it would also change D0's week tests, S's links and rivals' weeks, and the order players see. The balance runs don't move: the script's map has no river, so its raids land no boats, and Sea Peoples' bar and medals stand. Cost if wrong: about one Sea Peoples week in four plays without boats, though the card says so.
