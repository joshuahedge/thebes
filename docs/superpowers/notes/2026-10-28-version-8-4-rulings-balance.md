The clock is src/data/raids.js `RAIDS` (`firstWarnAfter`, `laterMin`, `laterMax`, `leanDays`); the new runs are tests/v84/balance/ (`spending.js` the spending player, `spendingRuns.js` the capital and late runs it plays, `farSiteRun.js` the far site, `targets.test.js` the targets B1-B11 no older test reads); the older runs' drivers and pins are tests/stage4/balance, tests/stage47/pressure, tests/v50, tests/v60/a, tests/v60/balance and tests/v70/balance. 1522. The spending player (design section 7) is a test player, not a rule: each month end, Low once the treasury holds 12 months of Normal-rate taxes (a palace laid first if there is none, 300 gold, on the open spot with a road farthest from any home) and Normal below 6; the busiest site rushed while the treasury stays above 6 months of taxes plus 30 days of the rush (the quote's `dayGold`), cancelled below; luxuries as the grand run's habit buys them. "Spent on the new jobs" is taxes given up + the Rush premium + luxuries, over the Normal-rate taxes; "spent in all" adds wages, upkeep, soldiers' pay and monument work (the premium is in it once). B3's "years of profit" is the late run's own yardstick (net less the work gold) taken at the Normal rate and without the premium, so the new jobs' gold is not taken off the measure that judges it. Measured: the capital spending run holds 28,729 at year 6, spends 19.0% on the new jobs and 91.7% in all (all of it Rush: it never holds 12 months of taxes, so Low is never set); the late spending run ends at 1.39 years of profit. — Cost if wrong: B2 and B3 say nothing about the tax rate; a run with a palace and a fuller treasury would.

1525. B7 is not met, and no number the balance check owns meets it. With 8.4's clock the long great-work runs meet twice the raids (13 by year 20, 8.3: 7) and fall into debt after the Seat: the dangers-off run is not won (4 of 5 offerings, the lowest -11,128, 3 raids lost); seed 5 reaches the Seat but is not won (the lowest -11,326, 2 raids lost, its works quarter burning from year 12); seed 10 never reaches Great Capital (-64,927, 3 lost); the dangers-on late run loses 2 of 10 raids (it lost none). Tried and measured: war elephants off, Victory +8 for 180 days, the luxury thrift at 1/1 or 1,000/2,000, and towers to the sheet's count change nothing that matters; halving the archers' and chariots' growth per raid (`MIX.perRaid`, src/data/armies.js, not this check's) beats every raid but leaves the runs in debt; later gaps of 540-900 days (outside Q11) win seed 5 on day 6,406 with 21,237 the lowest but not the dangers-off run. The cause is that every raid-indexed escalation (the mix's per-raid growth, the adaptation step, two-sided raids from the 4th, elephants from the 8th) now comes in half the game time, against scripted players that live on a thin treasury. The pins hold what is measured, each marked 8.4-BALANCE-OPEN with its old value, for the controller to rule: either the escalations count years (or halve their per-raid steps) so twice the raids are not twice as hard, or the great-work script is re-tuned to 8.4, or B7 is restated. — Cost if wrong: the reign's win is not shown to be reachable by the scripted player at 8.4's raid pace.
