# Version 9.2 Track Q (a quiet city): rulings 1775-1794

1775. Alerts roll up by cause: `rollUpAlerts` (src/sim/alerts.js) makes one row per code in `ROLLED` (no_road, no_storage, storage_full, no_input, understaffed, bazaar_empty, bazaar_sold_out, fire, blaze, fire_risk, site_stalled, illness, crime_risk, labour_shortage) with its count, its ids in id order and `members`; `alertsFor` stays the flat per-building list (the markers, the advice and a building's own problem list read it), and the UI's `alerts.rows()` is the rolled list. Letters, dated warnings and one-off notices are never merged. The six-year Capital lists 11 rows. Cost if wrong: a cause that differs inside one code (a bazaar with no food versus one that sells out is two codes, but two stalls of different reasons are one row) reads as one problem; its View steps through the lot.
1776. The Map badge counts rows (wide: the non-critical rows; a phone: every row less the banner's one). The Minimap sheet opens with City advice's top three under "Fix these first" (the same items and Show me as the advice sheet, read once a game day), then the rows; View on a rolled row steps through its buildings. Cost if wrong: a phone player who has one row per fire sees one badge number less than before.
1777. One gate over the four letter streams, on the optional world field `letterGate { lastDay, nextDay, n, served, want }`: absent (every city until its first letter on this build) it is open and the four schedules are 9.1's; the first letter creates it. A letter waits while another is open (a city event waiting, a demand offered and not taken up, a court card, a court request not yet read; each for at most 6 months from the day it came, so an ignored card cannot silence the court for good) and none comes within 5 to 8 months of the last (drawn with `hash2` from the world's seed). When the gate opens the stream served longest ago goes first (a stream that asks and sends nothing for three days goes to the back), so none starves: without it the court always won and the events never came. No world-version bump. Cost if wrong: an old city gets its first letter on the old schedule and the gate from then on. Pins that moved with it (WAVE2-PIN): the events' count in the sample Capital (was more than 5 in six years, now more than 1), the cards' and requests' own-schedule tests (they clear the gate), the Steady's year-6 gold (50,457 to 50,657), the late run's B3 ratio (1.55 to 1.09); the long balance runs listed in the report are for the balance check.
1778. The goals strip and the rank bar (`#goals`) move into the side panel above the minimap on the wide layout while the panel is unfolded (createUI `placeGoals`, the same element so every hand on it works); folding the panel gives them back to the top left. The top left keeps one banner and one tip. Cost if wrong: a folded panel on a wide screen shows the strip over the map again.
1779. The far zoom for markers is 0.6 and under (the plots' own `CROWD.zoom`). The design's "a badge wider than a tile" never holds inside the camera's 0.5 to 2 (a tile is 32 to 128 px, a badge 22), so the rule is the zoom where a tile is 38 px or less. At the far zoom: one badge per rolled row on its frontmost building in view (its ids go with it, front first, so a tap selects it), one for the homes about to lose a tier, no footprint rings. Cost if wrong: at 0.6 a city shows a count where it showed single badges.
1780. The ring's white halo is lighter at every zoom (alpha 0.75 to 0.4, 1.6 to 1.0 extra px). Cost if wrong: a ring is harder to see on pale ground.
1781-1790. Reserved, unused.
1791. Screenshot mode moved out of the menu and the City summary (the Seal and Share rows, and Share score's sheet) into Options > Display with a note; Share score stays. Cost if wrong: a player who knew the menu entry looks in Options.
1792. The interface tidies: one badge to a tray card (Suggested over New); the card shows the price alone (the monthly cost and workers are in the confirm bar); toasts are parchment with an ink outline, in the place they had above the tray and the corners; Options is Sound, Display, Controls, Help, Saves, About (last), each a collapsible details open to begin with; New city's replace note is amber. The menu's New city already asked with Export first (8.2), so only its note changed. Cost if wrong: a test or a habit that read the upkeep line on a card.
1793. The debt sheet's closed levers say why: "No palace yet: taxes cannot be raised." (no palace), "Taxes are already at High: they cannot be raised.", "A higher tax rate would bring in nothing more.", "No trade route is open yet: nothing can be sold.", "No trading post or quay is working to send goods from.", "Nothing above your Keep floors to sell.", each with a Show me (the palace, or the trade sheet at the post) only where there is something to show. Cost if wrong: a reason the sim cannot tell apart reads as the nearer one.
1794. Toasts never stack: `show` takes `group` and `waits`; a toast of a group never shows beside another of it, and one that waits never replaces a toast that is up; it shows, once, when there is room. "Paused while you were away" and the install ask are the group `welcome`, and the install ask waits (boot.js: its one call gets the two options). Cost if wrong: the install ask on a phone shows later than its 12 s window suggests.
