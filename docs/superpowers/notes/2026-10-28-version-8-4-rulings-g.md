# Version 8.4 "Gold and war": rulings 1465-1484 (Track G, gold: the tax rate, Rush a course, luxury imports)

The tax rate is src/data/palace.js (`TAX_RATES`, `TAX_ORDER`), src/sim/palace.js (`setTaxRate`, `taxRateOf`, `taxMood`), src/sim/economy.js `monthFigures`/`monthlyEconomy`, src/sim/mood.js and src/sim/score.js; Rush is src/data/monuments.js `MONUMENT.rushPace`/`rushGold`, src/sim/costs.js `rushGoldPerUnit` and src/sim/monuments.js (`rushQuote`, `setRush`, `crewDayUnits`, `planCrew`, `workPay`, `commitCrew`); the luxuries are src/data/housing.js, src/data/buildings.js (the bazaar), src/sim/distribution.js, src/sim/housing.js, the new src/sim/luxury.js and src/sim/advice.js. The interface: src/ui/monumentSheet.js (the palace's Taxes, the site's Rush), src/render/monuments.js (the "Rushed" label), src/ui/sheets.js and src/ui/nextStepText.js (the Luxury row and step), src/ui/tradeSheet.js (the luxury marks), src/ui/adviceText.js and src/ui/adviceUi.js. Words: Track G's section of src/ui/text.js (`V84_G_TEXT`). Tests: tests/v84/g/.

1465. The tax rate (Low x0.75 and +8 mood, High x1.25 and -8) lives on the palace as `palace.tax` (absent is Normal); with no palace, or once the palace is demolished, it is Normal; it reaches people only through mood. Setting it costs nothing and is not undoable. — Cost if wrong: a city at mood 100 gains nothing from Low; one data row to retune.
1466. The score's taxes term reads last month's `taxesNormal` (an 8.3 record without it reads `taxes`, which was Normal), so the rate never moves the score and the score version stays 2. — Cost if wrong: a High city's score does not show its extra gold.
1467. Rush doubles every crew's day on the course under way at 3x the work gold a unit, paid day by day through `workPay` (a day that would cross 0 is not worked); it ends as that course is laid (`commitCrew` clears `site.rush`, `coursesRushed` +1) and can be cancelled at any time with nothing refunded or owed. Materials, the great temple's offerings, the gilding and the capstone still wait. — Cost if wrong: a rush starved of stone looks idle; the sheet says why.
1468. Villas and Estates need one luxury of any kind (incense, cedar, ivory), 0.01 a resident a month times the tier's use, rounded up per home (a Villa 3, an Estate 4); it is listed after their other needs (the Estate's before jewellery). Homes take and use them as one pool, cheapest first by `GOOD_VALUE` (incense, cedar, ivory). A Fine manor stocks a Villa's month (design Q4) but uses none until it is a Villa: a household good is made in the city, a luxury is bought at 45 to 60 gold, and in the great-work run Fine manors that could not rise burned enough bought cedar to take the treasury below 0. Bazaars sell all three, 40 of each at most. — Cost if wrong: grand homes fall where no route is open; Advice names the fix.
1470. The month's taxes are rounded to whole gold once for the city (not per home); last month's record gains `taxesNormal`, `rush` (the premium) and `luxuries` (gold paid to caravans and boats for luxuries); each month end at Low or High adds to `taxLowMonths` or `taxHighMonths`; `luxuryBought` counts the units. The Taxes mood row asks for no road (the Palace row does). — Cost if wrong: none to play.
1471. Before `world.luxuryFrom` a missing luxury counts as met, both in the month's check and on the house sheet, so the row and the sim say the same (a migrated city's Villas hold for 12 months). — Cost if wrong: a home shows "Has it" during the grace with none in stock.
1472. A luxury's cause: the bazaar steps as for any good; then the cheapest luxury the bazaar or a store in its supply reach holds (`next_delivery`); then one held only in a far store (`good_far`, the existing fix); then the trade causes (src/sim/luxury.js): `luxury_ordered` (a route selling one is open and set to Buy above 0: waiting, unless no caravan or boat will come), `luxury_not_bought` (a route selling one is open but not bought, Buy at 0, or its switch Off), `no_luxury_route` (the cheapest partner the city can trade with, preferring one whose trading post or Quay stands; with none open to its rank yet, no fix and the words say when). `luxury_ordered` is new beside the plan's two codes, so a Buy order already set is waited on, not asked for again. — Cost if wrong: one more cause to word.
1473. The trade fix is `{ select: the landing, trade: { partner, good, open } }`: Show me opens the Trade sheet (City advice through a new optional `openTrade` that createUI passes; without it, the landing's sheet, which has Trade); a route still to open is priced at its route cost. Where the partner's trading post or Quay is missing the fix builds it (the post near the home, the Quay at Advice's own `quaySpot`). Two small edits outside Track G's table carry it: src/ui/createUI.js (one argument) and src/ui/adviceUi.js (one line); src/ui/adviceText.js gains the trade fix's Show me and cost line. — Cost if wrong: a merge touches createUI.js; trivially kept.
1474. Rush on the site's sheet sits under the camp's offer: the button carries the days saved and the gold ("Rush this course: about 135 days sooner, about 9,360 gold more"), the line under it what it does and the quote's terms (today's crews at full supply); it asks first and is judged again on yes; Cancel needs no question (nothing is owed). A rushed site's sheet says "Rushed: about N days to go" with Cancel, and the map labels it "Rushed" on a pill (render only, never animated). — Cost if wrong: one more row on a long sheet.
1475. A rushed crew stops at the end of the course under way (`planCrew`'s oneCourse), so no unit of the next course is laid at the rushed price; the crews after it that day work at their own pace. rushQuote's days match a run of the sim exactly at full supply. — Cost if wrong: a rushed course's last day loses a fraction of a crew-day.
1476. The premium is tallied each crew-day as the whole-gold share of that day's pay above the work gold (`round(pay x 2/3)`), so `rushGold` can differ from the quote's `extra` by about a gold a crew-day; a rushed site whose crews were short of a material today records it in `site.rushShort` ("Rushed: waiting for stone"). Both are optional fields, absent meaning none. — Cost if wrong: the tally drifts a few gold over a course.
1477. City advice gains 'gold_spare' (last in ADVICE_KINDS, weight 2): the treasury holds more than 12 months of Normal-rate taxes (`ADVICE_FIX.spareMonths`) with a palace whose rate is not Low; Show me opens the palace's sheet at its Taxes. Never with no palace, so never in the lesson. src/data/advice.js gains the kind (no other track touches it). — Cost if wrong: a nudge a rich city may not want; Low silences it.
1478. The grand run's luxury habit (tests/v50/grand/grandRun.js `actTrade`, also used by the great-work run through `playMonth`): it does the trade fix as the Trade sheet's Show me leads: opens the route (keeping RESERVE), switches the good on, and sets it to Buy at the level the words name (at least `LUXURY_KEEP` 30). Because a caravan buys down to the last gold, the habit buys luxuries only while the treasury can spare them (`LUXURY_THRIFT`: Ignore below 4,000, Buy again from 8,000, at the start of each month's play): without it the great-work run's caravans emptied the treasury the great temple's crews are paid from, and the run was not won. The great-work run also keeps the great temple's plot free until it is founded (`setReserved`, tests/v60/a/greatWorkRun.js `TEMPLE_PLOT`): with the luxuries the advice asked for a brewery that the satellite habit laid on the plot, and the dangers-on seed 5 never founded its temple. Measured with the habit: the grand run's first Fine manor, Villa and Estate stay months 74, 89 and 98 (an Estate stands months 98-120; 235 luxuries bought; the lowest gold 17,804). The pins it moved carry `WAVE2-PIN 8.4 Track G` for wave 2: the dangers-on great work's lowest gold (seed 5, 20,000 to 4,000), seed 10 without the habits (won by day 6,200 to not won: it never reaches the Seat), and three advice tops (grand y7, great y14, the grand district at year 10). — Cost if wrong: a test player's habit, not a rule; a real player can still empty a treasury on cedar (worth a later Trade-sheet warning).
1479. The lesson (design section 8, item 4): its city has no palace, so its rate is always Normal and no step waits on taxes. Forced to Low every step comes on the same day; forced to High every step is still reached, but High's -8 mood draws families more slowly, so the workers step comes ten days later: the rate reaches a step only through mood. Item 5: no palace, monument site or home wanting a luxury exists at any step, and 'gold_spare' never shows. — Cost if wrong: none (the lesson cannot set a rate).
1480. Bazaars draw luxuries from their stores only while some home in the city wants one (a Fine manor, Villa or Estate), so a city without one keeps its incense in its stores for the festivals, as in 8.3. — Cost if wrong: a bazaar keeps no luxury on its stall until the first Fine manor.
1481. Rush offers nothing on a site with no crew today (the crews row and the stall's cause already say so); it says why for no gold for a rushed day, a stage's offering, the gilding or the capstone. `has_rush` is reported before the other reasons, with the quote's numbers, for the rushed row. — Cost if wrong: none.
1482. The goods icons: cedar rides a cart as logs with ringed ends, ivory as a pair of tusks on a cloth (shape and colour, never colour alone); tests/stage47/carts/render.test.js's shape list gains them. — Cost if wrong: art only.
1483. The dev scenes src/dev/v50/grand.js and houseStep.js give their Fine manor and Estate cedar on hand and on the stall, so the steps they show (the street, the food, an Estate kept) stay what they were made to show. — Cost if wrong: none to play.
1484. A Buy order for a luxury whose Keep at least is below the homes' use between two visits (`luxuryKeepFor`: every Fine manor's, Villa's and Estate's monthly use at the tier it stocks for, times CARAVAN_EVERY_MONTHS, rounded up to the Trade sheet's steps) is its own cause, `luxury_short`: "Trade keeps only 20 cedar near the trading post; the homes use 11 a month. Raise cedar's Keep at least to 40", with Show me to the Trade sheet. Without it a city that set Buy once at a small level was told only to wait, every visit. The grand run's habit sets the level the words name. — Cost if wrong: one more cause; a partner's quota can still cap what comes (Byblos sends 60 cedar a visit at most).
