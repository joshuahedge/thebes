# Thebes 10.0.1 "Your feedback": Implementation Plan

Josh's 12 Nov 2026 playtest of 10.0 on desktop, with his answers to four design questions. One release, three tracks in parallel, then a final review, one fix wave, the docs, and a push (Josh: "Build and push").

## Global Constraints

- Everything in 10.0's Global Constraints still holds: plain ES modules, no build step, no Node; the sim stays deterministic; nothing in `src/render` or `src/ui` writes the world; "Pharaoh" in no game text; new strings only under `// Version 10.0.1, Track <X>` in `src/ui/text.js`; mobile and desktop both stay clean (one panel at a time, nothing floating over the middle of the map).
- `WORLD_VERSION` stays 12 unless a track must add saved state (then it says so in a ruling and bumps it with a migration and a test).
- The Motion setting holds: Gentle keeps figures still (fades only), Still shows nothing moving.
- Tests go in `tests/v1001/<track>/` (each folder's `all.js`; `tests/v1001/all.js` imports them; describe names start `v1001 <track>`).
- Rulings: `docs/superpowers/notes/2026-11-12-version-10-0-1-rulings-<track>.md`. Ranges: U 2340-2354, S 2355-2374, A 2375-2389, wave 4 2390-2399. Where the plan and the code disagree, the code wins and the ruling says both.

## Track U: the screen (rulings 2340-2354)

- **U.1 The tip card on desktop.** Today `.tip-row` (`src/ui/tips.js:50-65`, `css/ui.css:396-404`) lays the portrait, the text, "Show me" and "Got it" in one no-wrap flex row inside a fixed 440 px cap, so at 2000x1024 the text gets about 95 px (one or two words a line). On the wide layout: the text takes the full width and the buttons sit on a row beneath it, right-aligned; the cap scales with `--ui-scale` (about 440 px x scale); phones keep today's look unless the same squeeze shows at 150% text (check 390x844 and 375x667 at 100/150%). The "Goal done" card under it keeps its own layout. Test: at 1920x1080 and 2000x1024 the tip's text box is at least 300 px wide and its buttons do not share its line; at 390x844 the phone layout is unchanged (or better, stated).
- **U.2 The "Rushed" label.** `drawRushLabel` (`src/render/monuments.js:340-353`) sits on the footprint's front edge, drawn after the gangs, and covers the site and its markers. Move it above the site (over the scaffold top / the site's back edge), smaller and quieter, and keep it clear of the site's problem marker and other pills (reuse the fire labels' `layoutLabels` stepping in `src/render/fire.js:86-97`, or an equivalent). Keep `tests/v84/g/ui.test.js` green (update its expectation if it pins the position, with a ruling). Before/after shot of a rushed Builders' guild site.
- **U.3 Keys you can see.** Keys 1-7 already open the build tabs (`KEYS.category`, `createUI.js:974-980`) but nothing shows it. Show each tab's key as a small badge on its dock button (desktop with a fine pointer only; hidden on touch), and in each tab's tooltip/label. While a tab's tray is open, 1-9 pick its cards in order (a small number badge on each card; Esc closes the tray and 1-7 open tabs again); a picked card behaves as a click. Also show keys in the speed/pause buttons' tooltips. Test: with a tray open, pressing 2 picks the second card; with no tray, 2 opens the second tab; badges absent with a coarse pointer.
- **U.4 Key bindings in Options.** A "Keys" section in the Options sheet: every action in `KEYS` with its current key(s), a "Change" control that listens for the next key, a clash warning (the clashing action named; the new key is not taken until resolved, or the other loses it, say which in a ruling), "Reset to defaults", stored in settings (a `keyBindings` object of overrides; unknown actions ignored; older saves with none load the defaults). `createKeyboard` reads the effective map. The card keys (1-9) and tab keys (1-7) are bindable too. Keyboard-only use works (focus, Enter, Esc cancels a listen). Test: rebind pause to P, P pauses and Space no longer does; reset restores Space; a clash is refused or moved as ruled; settings survive a reload.

## Track S: the rules (rulings 2355-2374)

- **S.1 Defenders go anywhere.** Today soldiers, archers and chariots only engage raiders within their patrol of home (`DEFENCE.patrol` 8, archer 10, chariot 12; `src/sim/combat.js:284`, `archerPost` 213-225). Change: a unit with no raider in reach marches to the nearest raider anywhere on the map (nearest to the unit, ties by id), fights, and walks home when no raider is left. Towers keep `towerRange` 4 (they are buildings). Chariots use `driveTowards`. Keep determinism (same seed and orders give the same raid). Update the patrol words in text and the help/explainers so no caption says "within N tiles" for units any more; Advice that reads patrol (`advice.js:589`, `adviceFixes.js`) follows.
- **S.2 Raiders a bit faster, defenders to match.** Raider foot speed 1 → 1.5 tiles a day (`UNITS.raider.speed`, `src/data/raids.js:53`), enemy archer/chariot/elephant scaled x1.5 the same way; the player's soldier/archer/chariot scaled x1.5 too, so the ratio holds. Band movement today steps whole tiles (`step < speed`, `raids.js:731`): give it a fractional accumulator so 1.5 means three tiles every two days, deterministic. Raids still force the game to 1x when they start (unchanged).
- **S.3 Balance.** S.1 and S.2 make raids easier. Before changing anything, measure raids on 10.0 with the existing balance scripts and raid tests (losses per raid: buildings burnt/looted, gold lost, defenders lost; for the lesson raid and two scripted cities); then measure after. If losses per raid drop by more than about a third, toughen bands (size or strength) until they are within that, and rule both numbers. The lesson's raid must still be winnable as the lesson teaches it. Update the pinned tests (`tests/stage3/contract/dangerData.test.js:76`, `tests/stage46/armies/armies.test.js:40`, `words.test.js:121`, `tests/stage3/raids/raid.test.js:252-268`, `tests/stage46/armies/combat.test.js:147`, balance/longrun pins) each with a ruling.
- **S.4 Storehouses fetch for their neighbours.** Workshops already take inputs from any store within `producerReach` 12 road tiles (`production.js:50-59`), bazaars restock from `supplyReach` 16 (`distribution.js:49-72`). The gap: goods held further away. Change: a store with no orders of its own (`b.orders` empty) acts as if it had Keep-stocked orders for the inputs of the workshops within their reach of it and the goods of the bazaars within theirs, wherever no store within that workshop's/bazaar's reach already holds the good; its carts fetch from the nearest store with surplus anywhere (`carts.js` `cartDay`/`dispatch`/`nextTrip`, 448-505). A player's explicit order on that store replaces the automatic ones. No ping-pong: two automatic stores never fetch the same good from each other, and a store never fetches a good it is the source of (tests prove both). The store's sheet says "Fetching for nearby workshops: barley, clay" (one line) so the player sees it. Advice's Keep-stocked tips (`carts.js` `farGoodTip` 527, `keepStockedAdvice`) stop firing where auto-fetch covers it. Deterministic; no new saved state if derived each day (if it must be saved, say why and bump `WORLD_VERSION`).

## Track A: the motion (rulings 2375-2389)

- **A.1 A steady walk.** `src/render/units.js:61-69` glides a unit from its old spot to its new one over `GLIDE_DAYS` 0.6, then stands 0.4 of the day, so raiders and soldiers move stop-start. Make walkers glide continuously across the whole day (and across S.2's fractional steps: interpolate between the sim's last two positions over the step's real duration), with a stand only when the sim says they stopped (looting, fighting, waiting). Render-only. Test: sampled positions across a day advance monotonically with no flat 40% stretch for a walking unit; a looting raider stands.
- **A.2 Walking animals.** Gazelles and ostriches (`wildlife.js` herds, one static pose plus mirror) get a walk cycle (3-4 frames) while the herd moves and a graze/stand pose while it rests; frame rate tied to ground speed so feet don't slide. Herons and ibis get a take-off: wings open and two flap frames when they lift (10.0's heron lift, `riverLife.js`), and a fold on landing. Flying birds (`ambient.js:290-296`) flap (2-3 wing frames) instead of a fixed stroke. Hippo: ears/nostril flick and a slow bob while surfaced; crocodile: a slow tail sway when basking and a proper slide. Full only for loops; Gentle: poses swap slowly with no cycles (stand, graze, fly as a glide); Still: no motion.
- **A.3 Cost and look.** All new frames are baked once into the sprite cache (no per-frame path building); the LIFE/V budget tests still pass. Before/after shots at 390x844 DPR 2 and 1920x1080 of the wharf and a herd scene; look at them before reporting.
